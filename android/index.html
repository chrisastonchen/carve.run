Carve for Android

Carve for Android and Android Auto is in early access on Google Play. Joining takes about two minutes. Do it on your phone, or on a computer signed in to the Google account your phone's Play Store uses.

  1. Join the Carve beta group

    It's open to anyone, and it's what lets Google Play offer you the app. Choose Join group; there's nothing to post.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Open the test and tap Become a tester. If it says you can't join yet, give it a minute after joining the group and reload.

    Open the test

  3. Install Carve

    Get it from Google Play like any other app. Updates come the same way.

    Get Carve on Google Play

Please stay in the test for at least two weeks. Google asks for a group of early testers over 14 days before an app can go public on Play, so every tester who stays helps Carve get there.

Carve runs on Android 10 or later. Android Auto comes with Carve Apex. Questions, or stuck on a step? Write to support@carve.run.

On an iPhone? Carve is on the App Store.

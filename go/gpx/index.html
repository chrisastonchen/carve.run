Frequently asked questions

What is Carve?

Carve is a route planner and navigation app for people who love to drive. Discover great roads, plan your own routes or import existing ones, and share them with your car club or friends. Unlike ordinary navigation apps, Carve follows the roads you chose instead of replacing them with a faster route. Navigate turn by turn, even offline, and see your group on the map as you drive.

Does Carve work without cell signal?

Yes, with Carve Apex. Turn on Available Offline for a route while you have a connection, and Carve downloads the map and every turn along it. The drive then works the whole way with no signal — in canyons, in the mountains, and on remote back roads.

Can I see my friends on the map when we drive together?

Yes, free with an account. Send your group the route's link and drive it. Everyone on the route shows on the map and the car's screen, with how far ahead or behind they are. Drivers on the same link or drive plan see each other's names; anyone else shows as “Driver”. It's on unless you turn off See others on the map when you start a drive, which hides you too. It works only near the route, ends with the drive, and keeps no history.

How much does Carve cost?

Planning without limits, in the app and on a computer at carve.run/plan, importing GPX, Ride with GPS and Google Maps routes, sharing and driving with a signal are free, and so are the 1,200+ drives and guidance back to your route after a wrong turn. A free account adds your group on the map, CarPlay and Android Auto, and recording your drives. Carve Apex is $49.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers, or $5.99 a month, and adds offline maps, 3D previews, unlimited AI route planning, road conditions and popular times. See pricing.

Can I plan a route on my computer?

Yes, free. The planner at carve.run/plan builds a route on a big screen: search or click to add stops, add a great road whole, or import a GPX file or a Ride with GPS or Google Maps link. Saving it gives you a link to share, a QR code to open it on your phone, and printed turn-by-turn directions.

Can I import a route from Ride with GPS, a GPX file or Google Maps?

Yes, free. Open a GPX file from Ride with GPS, Garmin or another app, or paste a Ride with GPS or Google Maps link, in the app's Import or at carve.run/plan. Carve keeps the route's exact roads, and you drive it turn by turn.

Does Carve work with CarPlay and Android Auto?

Yes, free with an account. Your route, turns and stops appear on the car's screen through Apple CarPlay on iPhone or Android Auto on Android.

What happens if I take a wrong turn?

Carve doesn't reroute you onto a different road. It points you back to the route you planned and gives turn-by-turn directions to where you'll rejoin it, free. With the route downloaded for offline (Carve Apex), the way back works with no signal too.

What are hazard reports?

See debris, an animal or a crash on the road? Report it with one tap while you drive, and drivers behind you on the route are warned before they get there.

Does Carve work on a motorcycle?

Yes. Mount your phone and pair your helmet headset, and Carve's spoken turn-by-turn directions come through it like any other Bluetooth audio. Everything else works the same on two wheels: the twisty roads, routes that never swap in the fastest way, offline maps where there's no signal, and Group, so your riding group sees each other on the map.

Is Carve good for car clubs, riding groups and group drives?

Yes. Plan the drive once, or import the route you already have, and share one link or QR code: everyone opens the same route in Carve on iPhone or Android, members without the app can view it and print the turns, and changes you make reach everyone on the same link.

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026

The short version. Carve keeps your routes on your phone. Your location is used on the device to draw the map and guide the drive; the coordinates needed to fetch map tiles and directions are sent to Mapbox and Apple, who provide those services. Carve has no ads, no advertising trackers, and no analytics of its own. We don't sell data, and we don't see your payment details.

Who we are

Carve is an iPhone and CarPlay app for planning and driving routes, made by Chris Aston Chen (“Carve”, “we”). This policy explains what the app does with information, and what your choices are. Questions: support@carve.run.

What stays on your device

  • Routes you plan, save, or import — their names, start and end points, waypoints, and the road path and turn instructions computed for them, including the elevation profile.
  • Drive sessions — which stops you've reached and your last known position on a route, so a drive resumes after an interruption.
  • Offline maps — map tiles and terrain data downloaded for your routes (Carve Pro).
  • Settings — traffic, voice guidance, and similar preferences.

This data is stored in the app's own storage on your device. It is included in your device backups according to your iOS settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

Carve asks for location access to centre the map, to plan from where you are, and to guide a drive. During a drive it tracks your position continuously, including in the background while the drive is active, so that it can announce turns, clear stops, and detect when you've left the route.

Your location is processed on the device. It is not sent to any server operated by Carve. Coordinates are sent to third-party services only as needed to do what you asked for — see the next section. You can withdraw location access at any time in iOS Settings; the map still works, but drives can't be guided.

Services that receive data

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Mapbox Requests for the map tiles, terrain data, and traffic covering the area on screen or along a route; the coordinates of a route's points when computing its road path or directions back to it. Maps, offline downloads, road paths, elevation profiles, and drive-time guidance. Mapbox's SDK may also collect anonymised usage telemetry under Mapbox's privacy policy; you can turn this off from the ⓘ button on any Carve map.
Apple Place names and coordinates for address search and reverse geocoding; route bounds for the map thumbnails in your library; your Apple Account for purchases; drive status for CarPlay and Live Activities. Search, stop labels, thumbnails, Carve Pro purchases, and the car and lock-screen displays — all under Apple's privacy policy.

Neither service receives your name or contact details from Carve.

Sharing routes

When you share a route, Carve produces a link that contains the route itself — its name, points, and stops. Anyone who has the link can open the route, and links you send are outside Carve's control. Share links do not include your location or your drive history.

Carve Pro and purchases

Subscriptions are sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and billing; Carve never sees your card or bank details. The app receives a transaction record from Apple that identifies the subscription, not you. Carve Pro does not require an account.

Accounts and sync

Carve does not currently offer accounts, and nothing about you or your routes is stored on Carve servers. If accounts and cloud backup are introduced later, they will be optional, this policy will be updated first, and you'll be able to delete your account and its data from within the app.

What Carve does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own. If you've opted in to sharing analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share aggregated crash reports with us.
  • No selling, renting, or trading of personal information.

Children

Carve is a driving app and is not directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

Because your data lives on your device, you control it directly: delete a route in the app, remove offline downloads from a route's menu, revoke location access in iOS Settings, or delete the app. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, or erase personal data and to complain to a supervisory authority; write to support@carve.run and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the effective date above moves and the app will point you to the new version. Earlier versions are available on request.

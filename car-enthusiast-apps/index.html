The best apps for car enthusiasts in 2026

By Chris Aston Chen · Updated November 2026

Owning a car you love comes with its own set of apps: one for the drive, one for the track day, one to read the code when the check-engine light comes on, and one to remember when the oil's due. These are the ones worth a place on your phone, grouped by what they're for.

The short answer. For the drive itself, Carve (plan great roads, drive them turn by turn with your friends or club on one map, free). For a co-driver calling corners, Rods. For track days, RaceChrono Pro and MotorsportReg. For diagnostics, Car Scanner ELM OBD2 (free) or OBD Fusion. For maintenance, CARFAX Car Care. For collector cars, Hagerty Drivers Club.

For the drive

1. Carve Our app

Best for scenic drives, group drives and car clubs

Plan a drive on great roads, from 1,200+ hand-picked drives or stop by stop in the app or on a computer at carve.run/plan, or import a GPX file, a Ride with GPS link or a Google Maps link. Share one link and every driver gets spoken turn-by-turn on exactly that route; Carve never reroutes you onto a faster road, and after a wrong turn it leads you forward to where you rejoin it. With Group, everyone on the route is on one map, and on CarPlay or Android Auto.

Price: Free. Individual (Apex for one driver), $49.99 a year with a 14-day free trial, adds offline maps, 3D previews and road conditions. Platforms: iPhone with CarPlay, Android with Android Auto, web planner. See Carve

2. Rods

Best for corner-by-corner pace notes

A pocket co-driver: it calls each corner aloud before you reach it, rated Easy/Medium/Hard or on the rally 1-6 scale, plus speed bumps, surface changes and 80,000+ speed cameras, and on CarPlay it can show the next corner on supported instrument clusters. Planning is free; driving with its callouts is a 14-day trial, then a subscription. Its listings don't mention group drives or Android Auto.

Price: Free to plan; the US App Store lists yearly plans from $35.90, or $9.59 a month. Platforms: iPhone with CarPlay, Android. Carve vs Rods

3. Road & Rally

Best for hosting meets and rallies

Road Finder grades roads on curvature and flow, Convoy puts the group on a shared live map, and organizers can host meets and multi-day rallies with RSVPs and paid registration. Turn-by-turn and Convoy are free; VIP adds offline maps and full curated guides. It lists CarPlay on iPhone, not Android Auto.

Price: Free; VIP is an in-app purchase. Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Android. Carve vs Road & Rally

For the track

4. RaceChrono Pro

Best for track days

A lap timer and data logger for motorsport, with lap and sector timing, 2,600+ tracks, support for external GPS receivers and OBD-II readers, and video with your data overlaid. It's for the circuit, not the public road.

Price: $19.99 on the US App Store. Platforms: iPhone, iPad.

5. MotorsportReg

Best for finding and signing up for track events

The place to find and register for track days, HPDE, autocross and other motorsport events, and for organizers to run them. It's for the event, not the drive to it.

Price: Free to search events. Platforms: Web.

For diagnostics

6. Car Scanner ELM OBD2

Best free OBD2 scanner

Reads live data, sensor readings and trouble codes through your car's diagnostic port, clears codes, and builds custom dashboards and trip and fuel stats. Needs an ELM327-compatible Wi-Fi or Bluetooth LE adapter.

Price: Free; Pro from $3.99. Platforms: iPhone, Android.

7. OBD Fusion

Best paid OBD2 app with dashboards

Trouble codes, check-engine status, custom live dashboards, fuel economy and data logging, with enhanced diagnostics for some makes as add-ons. Needs a supported Wi-Fi ELM327 or Bluetooth LE adapter; generic classic Bluetooth adapters aren't supported.

Price: $9.99, with in-app purchases. Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Android.

For the garage

8. CARFAX Car Care

Best for service records and reminders

Log maintenance and repairs, get reminders when service is due and recall notices, track mileage and fuel economy, and estimate repair costs.

Price: Free. Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Android.

9. Hagerty Drivers Club

Best membership for collector cars

A membership for classic and collector car owners: the Hagerty Drivers Club magazine, roadside assistance for your collector cars, event perks and valuation tools, with the Hagerty app for your benefits and roadside help.

Price: $70 a year. Platforms: Hagerty app on iPhone and Android.

Questions

What apps do car enthusiasts use?

For driving: Carve for scenic drives and group drives, Rods for corner callouts, Road & Rally for meets. For the track: RaceChrono Pro and MotorsportReg. For diagnostics: Car Scanner ELM OBD2 or OBD Fusion with an OBD2 adapter. For upkeep: CARFAX Car Care. Many also have the Hagerty app for Drivers Club.

What's the best app for car clubs?

Carve. Organizers plan the club's routes, days and meet times on the web with Carve Club, share one link, and every driver gets turn-by-turn on the same route with the whole group on one map. See the best group drive apps.

What's the best app for finding driving roads?

Carve has 1,200+ hand-picked drives across the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the UAE and Baja California, rated Leisurely to Intense, on one map at carve.run/drives. See the best scenic route apps.

Do I need an adapter for OBD2 apps?

Yes. Car Scanner ELM OBD2 and OBD Fusion read your car through an OBD2 adapter in the diagnostic port; both list the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE adapters they support, and cheap clones often don't connect.

What's the best lap timer app?

RaceChrono Pro is a lap timer and data logger with 2,600+ tracks and support for external GPS receivers and OBD-II readers, $19.99 on the US App Store.

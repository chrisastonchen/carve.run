The drive

NC 209, known as the Rattler, is a 36.6-mile two-lane mountain highway from Lake Junaluska to Hot Springs. It starts at a busy junction with US 19, 23 and 74, crosses I-40 early on and climbs over Hebo Mountain to Betsy's Gap, its highest point at 3,895 feet, where it enters Madison County.

It passes through Luck, Trust and Spring Creek. The road forms the eastern edge of Pisgah National Forest for part of its length. The last stretch, about 10 miles, is very winding, and the road ends in Hot Springs. The drive is 36.1 miles and takes about 1 hr 5 min. NC 209 is the main part of the Appalachian Medley scenic byway.

Good to know

  • NC 209 is not recommended for commercial trucks.
  • Hot Springs, at the end of the drive, was hit hard by Helene and has reopened to visitors.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NCDOT DriveNC: traffic and road conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Rattler, NC 209, take?

NC 209 from Lake Junaluska to Hot Springs is 36.1 miles and takes about 1 hr 5 min.

How long is the Rattler loop?

Visit Haywood lists the Rattler as about 36 miles one way to Hot Springs, and the full loop as about 98 miles.

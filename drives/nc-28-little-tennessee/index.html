The drive

North of Franklin, NC 28 follows the Little Tennessee River valley and passes Fontana Lake. This drive runs the other way, from Lauada near Bryson City south along the river to Iotla, on the edge of Franklin. It is 16.1 miles and takes about 30 minutes.

NC 28 is an 81.2-mile highway through Nantahala National Forest and overlaps parts of the Mountain Waters Scenic Byway. The current road was established in 1938.

Cowboy Trail: Longview to Lundbreck

22, Cowboy, Morrison Road · Southern Alberta Foothills, Alberta

Highway 22, the Cowboy Trail, from Longview south to the Crowsnest Highway at Lundbreck: an open two-lane road over ranch foothills along the front of the Rockies, past Chain Lakes Provincial Park. Few services between the two ends.

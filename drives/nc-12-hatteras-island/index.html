Highway 12: Nags Head to Hatteras

NC 12, Cape Hatteras National Park Road, Jug Handle Bridge, North Carolina Highway 12 · Outer Banks, North Carolina

NC-12 from Whalebone Junction down the Outer Banks through Cape Hatteras National Seashore, past Bodie Island and Cape Hatteras lighthouses and the Jug Handle Bridge to Hatteras village: flat and open between dunes and sound. Storms close it at times.

The drive

NC 226A, known to motorcyclists as the Diamondback, climbs from NC 226 near Woodlawn up the Blue Ridge to Little Switzerland. The route follows Armstrong Creek and Three Mile Creek, then climbs Grassy Mountain. The early part is curvy but climbs only slightly; the climb toward Little Switzerland has a sensational amount of curves, over 1,900 feet of rise.

Destination McDowell counts more than 190 steep, climbing curves in about 12 miles, with switchbacks that loop almost 360 degrees, rocky outcroppings and sheer drop-offs. This drive is 9.6 miles and about 20 minutes, with an Intense rating. It ends near Little Switzerland, where it connects to the Blue Ridge Parkway.

Good to know

  • Trucks are not recommended on the steep, winding grade.
  • The road is narrow with sheer drop-offs and tight hairpins.
  • I found no current closure on 226A, but check DriveNC for road work.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NCDOT DriveNC.

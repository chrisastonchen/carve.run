The drive

This Ruta Panorámica drive climbs from the edge of Mayagüez, a west coast city, up into the coffee hills to Maricao. It follows PR-105 for 13.4 miles and ends on PR-357 in Maricao, about 40 minutes later. PR-105 runs concurrently with the Ruta Panorámica in several segments, including near Maricao itself. The bends are tight, nearly all the way, and the road rises about 3,100 feet in total, reaching near 1,400 feet.

Maricao's economy boomed with coffee in the 1800s, and coffee is still farmed in the town, which holds a Coffee Harvest Festival each February. Maricao State Forest, also called Monte del Estado, lies along the Ruta Panorámica south of the town, with a stone observation tower at the summit.

It is a slow road through small villages, narrow in places. PR-105 carries on east of Maricao toward the Yauco side.

Good to know

  • Lanes are narrow and the going is slow, with frequent blind bends.
  • Landslides after heavy rain are a known problem on Puerto Rico's mountain roads, so check conditions in wet weather.
  • Signs show kilometers while speed limits are posted in miles per hour.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Puerto Rico DTOP: avisos y comunicaciones.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Ruta Panorámica from Mayagüez to Maricao take?

Mayagüez to Maricao on PR-105 is 13.4 miles (21.6 km) and takes about 40 minutes without stops.

Is PR-105 from Mayagüez to Maricao twisty?

Very. It is twisty most of the way, with tight bends and a climb of about 3,100 feet from the coast into the mountains.

What is Maricao known for?

Maricao is a coffee town in the mountains with a Coffee Harvest Festival each February. Maricao State Forest, or Monte del Estado, is nearby on the Ruta Panorámica.

Is PR-105 part of the Ruta Panorámica?

Yes. PR-105 runs concurrently with the Ruta Panorámica in several segments, including near Maricao.

The drive

This short drive crosses the northern edge of the Hunua hills from Clevedon to Kawakawa Bay: 16 km (10 mi) in about 20 minutes on Clevedon-Kawakawa Road, with two twisty stretches and moderate bends between them. Clevedon Coast Oysters sells oysters a few kilometres out of town, and Waitawa Regional Park is just beyond.

Kawakawa Bay is a sheltered bay with a boat ramp, a small store and wide tidal flats. The drive carries on along the Kawakawa Bay Coast Road, a sealed but narrow road of about 2.6 km that follows the Tāmaki Strait shore, and it ends with views over the water to the Hauraki Gulf islands.

This is the first leg of the Pacific Coast Highway route out of Auckland, and Orere Point, with its store and beach reserve, is the next service stop on the way to Kaiaua.

Good to know

  • The Kawakawa Bay Coast Road is sealed but narrow, so take care and expect to give way.
  • Waitī Bay, at the end of the coast road, has parking for about 20 vehicles and toilets.
  • Orere Point, further along the Pacific Coast Highway, has a store, a holiday park and a beach reserve, and is the main service stop before Kaiaua.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Auckland Transport: roadworks and disruptions.

Questions about this drive

How long does Clevedon-Kawakawa Road take to drive?

Clevedon-Kawakawa Road from Clevedon to Kawakawa Bay and the coast road beyond is 16 km (10 mi) and takes about 20 minutes.

Is the Kawakawa Bay Coast Road sealed?

Yes, the Kawakawa Bay Coast Road is sealed but narrow, about 2.6 km long, from the boat ramp to Waitī Bay.

Where is the next fuel and food after Kawakawa Bay?

Orere Point has a local store and holiday park, and it is the main service stop on the Pacific Coast Highway before Kaiaua.

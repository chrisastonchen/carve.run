Cascade Lakes Highway

Cascade Lakes, Century, OR 372, 46 · Deschutes National Forest, Oregon

Century Drive and the Cascade Lakes Highway from Bend up past Mount Bachelor, then along Sparks, Devils and Elk lakes under South Sister and Broken Top to the Lava Lake turnoff. Beyond the ski area it's closed by snow from about mid-November to late May.

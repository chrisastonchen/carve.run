Teton Pass: WY-22

WY 22, ID 33, Teton Pass, WY-22, ID-33, Highway 22, Highway 33, Main · Teton Range, Wyoming and Idaho

WY-22 from Wilson over 8,431-foot Teton Pass and down into Idaho to Victor: sustained 10% grades and tight bends on both sides, with views back over Jackson Hole from the top. Very steep; heavy commuter traffic. Open all year.

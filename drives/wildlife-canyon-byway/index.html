The drive

The Wildlife Canyon Scenic Byway, signed as Highway 17, runs about 35 miles from Banks on Highway 55 to Lowman on Highway 21, following the South Fork of the Payette River. Visit Southwest Idaho calls it a well-maintained road with non-stop views.

Crouch, in Garden Valley, is about 10 miles in. At Grimes Pass, large pullouts with telescopes let you watch elk, turkeys and other wildlife, and the guide suggests starting early or ending late for the best chances. The drive is 33 miles and takes about 1 hr. From Boise it makes a loop: Highway 55 to Banks, this road to Lowman, then Highway 21 south.

Good to know

  • Most services, including gas, are in Crouch and Lowman.
  • The byway is open year-round, but conditions in the mountains change quickly from late fall to early spring.
  • Pine Flats and Deadwood campgrounds are along the route.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Idaho 511.

Questions about this drive

Can you see wildlife on the Wildlife Canyon Scenic Byway?

Banks Lowman, Banks-Lowman · South Fork Payette, Idaho

The Banks–Lowman Road from the Payette River at Banks up the South Fork Payette through Garden Valley to Lowman: river curves through canyon and ponderosa pine, joining ID-21 at the end. A good link between ID-55 and ID-21.

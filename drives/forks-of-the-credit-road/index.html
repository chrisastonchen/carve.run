Forks of the Credit Road

Forks of the Credit, Mississauga Road · Caledon Hills, Ontario

Forks of the Credit Road from Highway 10 down into the Credit River valley and up to Belfountain: a short, tight run on the Niagara Escarpment with a well-known hairpin. Trucks are barred, and weekends bring motorcycles and cyclists.

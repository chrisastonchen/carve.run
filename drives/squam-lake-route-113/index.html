Route 113: Squam Lake

NH 113, NH 25, Main Street, Squam Lake Road, Skinner Street · Lakes Region, New Hampshire

NH-113 from Holderness along the north side of Squam Lake, where On Golden Pond was filmed, then through hardwood forest to the old village of Center Sandwich. A curvy, quiet two-lane road with lake glimpses near Holderness.

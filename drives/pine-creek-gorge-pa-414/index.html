The drive

PA 414 follows Pine Creek through Pine Creek Gorge and Tiadaghton State Forest. This drive runs 30.9 miles south from Morris, through Blackwell, Cedar Run and Slate Run, to Waterville, where PA 414 meets PA 44. It is a sweeping road of about an hour, with mostly gentle curves.

The highway crosses Pine Creek and the Pine Creek Rail Trail several times. The rail trail is a 62-mile biking route through what Tioga County calls the Grand Canyon of Pennsylvania, which is surrounded by about 165,000 acres of Tioga State Forest. Wikipedia describes the first leg of PA 414 out of PA 44 as very narrow and rugged.

The Highway 44 drive begins where this one ends.

Good to know

  • The road near the Waterville end is very narrow and rugged, so take it slowly.
  • Expect bikers and walkers where the road meets the Pine Creek Rail Trail.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check 511PA.

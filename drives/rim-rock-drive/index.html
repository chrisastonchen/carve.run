Rim Rock Drive

Rim Rock · Colorado National Monument, Colorado

The 1930s road through Colorado National Monument from Fruita to Grand Junction: switchbacks and tunnels up the cliffs, then the rim above red sandstone canyons and Independence Monument, with sheer drops and low walls. Park entrance fee.

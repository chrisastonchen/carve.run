Old Canada Road: Route 201

US 201 · Kennebec Valley, Maine

US-201 north from Bingham along Wyman Lake and the Kennebec River to The Forks, the rafting base for the Kennebec Gorge, then through the North Woods to the Attean View above Jackman. Long, quiet and gently curved; logging trucks.

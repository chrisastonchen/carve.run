The drive

The first 61 miles of the Blue Ridge Parkway, from Rockfish Gap past Humpback Rocks, Ravens Roost and the Whetstone Ridge crest, then down along Otter Creek. No fee; 45 mph limit. The James River bridge just past the end is closed for repairs.

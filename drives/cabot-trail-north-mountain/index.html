The drive

This leg of the Cabot Trail goes from Pleasant Bay across the top of Cape Breton to Ingonish Beach. About a third of the whole Cabot Trail runs through Cape Breton Highlands National Park, with ocean and mountain views.

Cape North is described as a headland at the northernmost point of the Cabot Trail, and the route ends at Ingonish, where the Keltic Lodge resort and Ingonish Beach are.

The drive is 45.3 miles and takes about 1 hr 20 min. It climbs roughly 4,000 feet in all, to a high point of 1,500 feet.

Good to know

  • Parks Canada charges daily admission in the national park.
  • Visitor services in the park run from mid-May to late October, though the park is open year-round.
  • Check 511 Nova Scotia and Parks Canada before you go.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Parks Canada: Cape Breton Highlands National Park.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Cabot Trail from Pleasant Bay to Ingonish take?

Pleasant Bay to Ingonish Beach is 45.3 miles and takes about 1 hr 20 min without stops.

Is there a fee on this part of the Cabot Trail?

Parts of it are inside Cape Breton Highlands National Park, which charges daily admission.

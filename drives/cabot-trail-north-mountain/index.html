Cabot Trail: Pleasant Bay to Ingonish

Cabot Trail, CT · Cape Breton Highlands, Nova Scotia

The Cabot Trail across the top of Cape Breton, past the Lone Shieling's old hardwoods, up and over North Mountain's steep grades and hairpins to Cape North, then down the Atlantic side by Neils Harbour to Ingonish Beach.

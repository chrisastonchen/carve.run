Cross Creek: CR 346 and CR 325

346, 325, Cross Creek · Alachua County, Florida

CR 346 east from Micanopy and CR 325 south through Cross Creek, between Orange and Lochloosa lakes, past Marjorie Kinnan Rawlings's farmhouse to Island Grove: a narrow country road under moss-hung oaks, part of the Old Florida Heritage Highway.

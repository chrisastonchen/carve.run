Usery Pass Road and Bush Highway

Usery Pass, Bush · Usery Mountains, Arizona

Usery Pass Road climbs out of east Mesa through saguaro over a low pass in the Usery Mountains, then the Bush Highway sweeps down toward the Salt River with Four Peaks and Red Mountain in view, ending at the Saguaro Lake turnoff.

Rock Creek Road

Rock Creek Road · Eastern Sierra, California

Rock Creek Road climbs from Tom's Place on Highway 395 up Rock Creek canyon past Rock Creek Lake to Mosquito Flat, at about 10,200 feet one of the highest trailheads in the Sierra. The top stretch is a single lane; closed in winter.

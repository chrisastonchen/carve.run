Brandon Gap: Route 73

VT 73, Brandon Gap, Gap Road, Brandon Mountain Road, Park Street · Green Mountains, Vermont

VT-73 from the White River valley near Rochester over the Brandon Gap, where a pullout looks across beaver ponds to the Great Cliff of Mount Horrid, then down through Forest Dale to Brandon. Quiet, with long sweeping curves.

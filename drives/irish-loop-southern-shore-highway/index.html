The drive

Route 10, the Southern Shore Highway, runs south from Goulds on the edge of St. John's down the east coast of the Avalon Peninsula. It is the eastern half of the Irish Loop, and it passes through a string of fishing communities: Witless Bay, Tors Cove, Cape Broyle, Ferryland and Fermeuse.

Off Witless Bay lie the islands of the Witless Bay Ecological Reserve, where the seabird colonies include the largest Atlantic puffin colony in North America. Ferryland is the site of the 17th-century Colony of Avalon, founded in the 1620s and a National Historic Site of Canada. South of Ferryland the road crosses open country to Portugal Cove South, the community nearest Mistaken Point, whose Ediacaran fossils are a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and then ends at Trepassey.

The curves are gentle, and the coast and the villages are the reason to go. It is 82.1 miles and takes about 2 hr 20 min, a leisurely pace. The full Irish Loop returns by Route 90, so this drive is only the southern-shore side.

Good to know

  • Seabirds nest on the Witless Bay islands from roughly April to early September, so that is the season for the colonies.
  • Fog is common on the barrens south of Ferryland, and Mistaken Point is named for fog that led sailors to mistake it for Cape Race.
  • Route 10 is the only road on this stretch, so there is no quick alternative if it is closed.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check 511 Newfoundland and Labrador.

Questions about this drive

How long is the Irish Loop's Southern Shore Highway?

Route 10 from Goulds to Trepassey is 82.1 miles and takes about 2 hr 20 min without stops.

Is the Southern Shore Highway twisty?

No. Route 10 has mostly gentle curves, and the coastal scenery and small fishing villages are the draw.

What is there to see on Route 10 on the Avalon Peninsula?

The seabird islands off Witless Bay, the Colony of Avalon archaeological site at Ferryland, and the Mistaken Point fossil reserve near Portugal Cove South.

Where does Route 10 on the Irish Loop start?

This drive starts at Goulds, on the south edge of St. John's, and heads south to Trepassey.

The drive

This drive follows the Blue Ridge Parkway north from the Roanoke area near US 460 to Peaks of Otter, 22.7 miles in about 40 minutes. Peaks of Otter, at miles 84 to 87, has three mountain peaks, popular viewing sites and a shuttle bus to Sharp Top.

The curves are mostly gentle, with one twisty stretch. The road reconstruction between MP 95.9 and 106 is complete, and so is the pavement work at MP 86 to 89.

Good to know

  • The Parkway has no fee, and its speed limit is never higher than 45 mph.
  • Commercial vehicles are prohibited on the Parkway unless approved by the Park Service.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Blue Ridge Parkway: road status and closures.

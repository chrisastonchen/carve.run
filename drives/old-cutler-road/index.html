The drive

Old Cutler Road south from Cocoplum in Coral Gables past Matheson Hammock, Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden and the Deering Estate to Palmetto Bay: a winding historic road under banyans and live oaks, one of Miami-Dade's first.

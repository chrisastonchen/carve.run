Nebo Loop

Nebo, Payson Canyon · Wasatch Mountains, Utah

The Nebo Loop from Payson up Payson Canyon past alpine lakes and aspen to 9,300 feet beside Mount Nebo, then down past the red hoodoos of Devils Kitchen to Salt Creek Canyon near Nephi. Narrow with blind curves; trailers not allowed.

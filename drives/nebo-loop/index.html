The drive

The Nebo Loop climbs from Payson up Payson Canyon past alpine lakes and aspen to 9,300 feet beside Mount Nebo. It then drops past the red hoodoos of Devils Kitchen to Salt Creek Canyon on Highway 132 near Nephi. It is 35.9 miles and about 1 hr 15 min, with three twisty stretches.

The Forest Service describes Devils Kitchen as a geologic site with picnic facilities, a paved trail and an observation deck. A half-mile round-trip trail leads to the hoodoos. The Payson Lakes Recreation Area is about 12 miles south of Payson.

The road is narrow with blind curves, and it closes for winter.

Good to know

  • The road is narrow with blind curves, and trailers are not allowed, according to local reporting.
  • It usually closes for snow around November 1 and reopens in late May or June.
  • Fall color weekends can be slow.

Closed by snow from about November to late May. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Forest Service: Nebo Loop.

Mount Hamilton Road

Mount Hamilton Road, CA 130 · Diablo Range, California

CA-130 from east San Jose up Mount Hamilton: a near-continuous run of tight bends past Joseph D. Grant County Park to Lick Observatory at about 4,200 ft, with views across the Santa Clara Valley. Narrow in places; expect cyclists.

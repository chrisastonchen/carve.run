The drive

PA 45 leaves Boalsburg along Boal Avenue and heads east into Penns Valley, an agricultural valley. It follows Penns Valley Road north of Spring Mills, enters Millheim as West Main Street and runs on through the valley to Mifflinburg, entering as Chestnut Street.

Penns Valley is a cluster of small communities along the main road, including Millheim, Centre Hall and Spring Mills. Millheim's historic district includes 176 Georgian and Victorian buildings. Mifflinburg was once known as Buggy Town for its more than fifty buggy and sleigh factories in the 1880s.

Drivers through Penns Valley frequently share the road with the horse-drawn buggies of local Amish residents.

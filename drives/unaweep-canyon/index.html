Unaweep Canyon: Highway 141

CO 141 · Uncompahgre Plateau, Colorado

CO-141 from Whitewater through Unaweep Canyon, the granite valley with a creek draining from each end, over the Unaweep Divide and down past the Driggs Mansion ruin into red-rock country to Gateway. Quiet two-lane road with few services.

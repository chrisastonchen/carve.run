Million Dollar Highway

US-550, Ouray to Silverton · San Juan Mountains, Colorado

US-550 between Ouray and Silverton, blasted into the walls of the Uncompahgre Gorge and over Red Mountain Pass at 11,018 feet. It's one of the most dramatic paved roads in America, and one of the least forgiving.

The drive

Strictly, the Million Dollar name belongs to the 12 miles from Ouray through the Uncompahgre Gorge to the top of Red Mountain Pass; this route carries on down the far side to Silverton, the way most people drive it. Leaving Ouray, the road switchbacks up the gorge wall with sheer drops, tight hairpins and long stretches with no guardrail.

Over the pass, the land opens into the old Red Mountain mining district before the road drops through forest to Silverton, a former mining town above 9,000 feet. The drive is part of the San Juan Skyway, an All-American Road.

Stops along the way

  1. Bear Creek Falls About 2¾ miles south of Ouray: a viewing platform over a 205-foot waterfall beside the road.
  2. Red Mountain Pass The top, at 11,018 feet. The climb from the Ouray side reaches an 8% grade.
  3. Silverton The end of the road: a former mining town in a high mountain valley.

When to go

US-550 is open all year, but it's at its best from late spring to early fall, when it's dry and clear of snow. In winter, avalanches close it for hours or days at a time, and chains can be required. In summer 2026 CDOT began repairing a retaining wall and the Bear Creek Tunnel just south of Ouray, with one-lane traffic, delays of 15 to 20 minutes and some overnight closures; check whether it's finished before you go.

Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs. Before you set off, check COtrip, CDOT's road conditions.

Good to know

  • Long stretches have no guardrails, narrow lanes and tight hairpins. Keep your speed down, and use the pullouts to let faster traffic by.
  • Seventy named avalanche paths cross the road between Ouray and Silverton.
  • At over 11,000 feet the weather changes fast; bring a warm layer even in summer.
  • Box Cañon Falls is just outside Ouray, off the first switchback on County Road 361.

Emerald Bay Road: Highway 89

CA 89, Emerald Bay, West Lake Boulevard · Lake Tahoe, California

Highway 89 along Lake Tahoe's west shore from South Lake Tahoe to Tahoe City, over a narrow ridge high above Emerald Bay with steep drops on both sides, then past Meeks Bay and Homewood. Summer traffic is heavy; snow can close the Emerald Bay stretch.

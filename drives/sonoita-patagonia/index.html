The drive

AZ 83 rolls south from I-10 at Vail through oak-dotted hills to the high grassland and wineries around Sonoita. There AZ 82 follows Sonoita Creek down to the small town of Patagonia, with the Santa Rita Mountains in view.

The curves are gentle and traffic is light, so this is a relaxed drive more than a twisty one. AZ 82 continues south of Patagonia past Patagonia Lake to Nogales.

The drive

Alabama 180 runs west from Gulf Shores along the Fort Morgan peninsula. The two-lane lies between the Gulf of Mexico and Bon Secour National Wildlife Refuge, which has about 7,000 acres of habitat for migratory birds, sea turtles and the Alabama beach mouse. The refuge visitor center is on Highway 180.

The road ends near Fort Morgan, where the Mobile Bay Ferry connects to Dauphin Island. Fort Morgan State Historic Site sits at the mouth of Mobile Bay, and it charges admission.

The route is 17.5 miles and takes about 22 minutes. It is flat.

Good to know

  • Bon Secour refuge trails are open sunrise to sunset, and bicycles are not allowed on them.
  • Fort Morgan State Historic Site charges an admission fee.
  • Beach traffic and cyclists use this road in summer.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check ALGO Traffic (Alabama DOT).

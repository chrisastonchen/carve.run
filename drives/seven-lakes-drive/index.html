Seven Lakes Drive

Seven Lakes · Harriman State Park, New York

Seven Lakes Drive from Sloatsburg through Harriman State Park, past Lake Sebago, Lake Kanawauke and Lake Tiorati and through two traffic circles, to Route 6 near Bear Mountain. Wooded curves and lake views about an hour from the city.

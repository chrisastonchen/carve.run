Highway 1: Finland to Ely

MN 1, State Highway 1, Highway 1, Sheridan · Superior National Forest, Minnesota

MN-1 inland from the North Shore at Illgen City through Finland and Isabella to Ely: about 60 miles of winding two-lane through the Superior National Forest, with lakes, bogs and spruce and almost no towns between.

The drive

Hayden Valley is a broad sub-alpine valley of about 50 square miles between Yellowstone Falls and Yellowstone Lake. This drive runs north from Fishing Bridge Junction to Canyon Junction on the Grand Loop's Canyon-to-Lake section, along the valley's eastern side near the Yellowstone River.

The valley floor is an ancient lake bed from when Yellowstone Lake was much larger. It is known as one of the best places in the park to see animals: bison, elk, grizzly bears, coyotes and wolves all live there. Mud Volcano and its neighbors, Mud Geyser, Sulphur Caldron and Black Dragon Caldron, are geothermal features at the valley's southern end.

The road is 15.2 miles and takes about 35 minutes with no stops, though a wildlife jam can add much more.

Good to know

  • The road is closed to regular vehicles in winter, and opening dates depend on weather.
  • Yellowstone charges an entrance fee: $35 for a private vehicle, good for seven days.
  • Expect bison jams: wait patiently, stay in your vehicle, don't honk, and pull fully off the road to watch.
  • Keep 25 yards from bison and elk and 100 yards from bears and wolves. Bison are dark and hard to see at night.

Closed to cars from November 1 to about May 1. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Yellowstone National Park: park road status.

Questions about this drive

What animals can you see in Hayden Valley?

Hayden Valley is home to bison, elk, grizzly bears, coyotes and wolves, and it is one of the best places in Yellowstone to see animals.

How long does the Hayden Valley drive take?

Fishing Bridge Junction to Canyon Junction is 15.2 miles and takes about 35 minutes, longer if bison are on the road.

When is the road through Hayden Valley open?

The Fishing Bridge to Canyon road is closed to regular vehicles in winter, and opening dates depend on weather. Check the park road status page before you go.

Is there a fee to drive through Hayden Valley?

Yes. The road is inside Yellowstone National Park, where a private vehicle pass is $35 and lasts seven days.

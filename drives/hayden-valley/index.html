Hayden Valley: Fishing Bridge to Canyon

Grand Loop · Yellowstone National Park, Wyoming

The Grand Loop north from Fishing Bridge to Canyon Village beside the Yellowstone River: LeHardy Rapids, Mud Volcano, then the open meadows of Hayden Valley, where bison herds often stop traffic. A gentle road taken slowly. Park fee.

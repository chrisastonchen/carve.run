The drive

From Babb up the Swiftcurrent valley along Lake Sherburne into Glacier National Park's Many Glacier valley, ending by Swiftcurrent Lake and the Many Glacier Hotel under Grinnell Point. Park entrance fee; closed in winter.

Good to know

Closed at the park boundary from the first lasting snow (October or November) until spring plowing, usually late April or May. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check the state or county's road conditions.

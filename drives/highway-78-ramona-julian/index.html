The drive

Highway 78 from Ramona past Santa Ysabel to Julian: rolling climbs and sweeping curves through oak and ranch country up into the Cuyamaca Mountains. Dudley's Bakery sits at the Highway 79 junction at Santa Ysabel; Julian is known for its pies.

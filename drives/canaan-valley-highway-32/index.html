Highway 32: Harman to Davis

WV 32, Appalachian Highway, Appalachian Way, South Main Street · Canaan Valley, West Virginia

WV-32 north from Harman over the ridge into Canaan Valley, a broad mountain valley at over 3,000 feet, past Canaan Valley Resort State Park and the wildlife refuge to Davis, the turn for Blackwater Falls. Easy curves; the valley is the draw.

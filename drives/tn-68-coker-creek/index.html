The drive

Highway 68 starts at Tellico Plains, where it meets TN 165 and the road east to the Cherohala Skyway, and runs south to Ducktown. It is 31.3 miles and climbs about 4,000 feet in total.

Along the way it passes through Coker Creek in Monroe County, crosses the Hiwassee River in a sparsely populated area, and enters the Cherokee National Forest. It meets TN 123 at Turtletown and ends at US 64 and US 74 in Ducktown. The route is Twisty, with three tight stretches.

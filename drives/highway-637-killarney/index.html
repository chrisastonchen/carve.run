The drive

Highway 637 is the only road to Killarney. It runs 42 miles west from Highway 69 at Rock Bay to the village on Georgian Bay, through Canadian Shield forest, lakes and wetlands. Wikipedia says it opened on July 20, 1962; before that, Killarney was reached mainly by water, air or winter road.

Near the end the road runs beside Killarney Provincial Park and its white quartzite La Cloche ridges. The road ends at Killarney.

Good to know

  • It's a dead end: you return by the same road.
  • Traffic is light, with about 500 vehicles a day in a 2010 survey.
  • Using Killarney Provincial Park needs a day-use permit.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Ontario Parks: Killarney.

Questions about this drive

How long is Highway 637 to Killarney?

It's 42.2 miles and takes about an hour from Highway 69.

Is Highway 637 the only road to Killarney?

Yes. Highway 637 is the only road into the village.

Do you need a permit for Killarney Provincial Park?

Yes, to use the park. Check Ontario Parks for current day-use rules.

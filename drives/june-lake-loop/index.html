June Lake Loop

June Lake Loop, June Lake Loop Road, Boulder Drive, CA 158 · Eastern Sierra, California

Highway 158 leaves Highway 395 at June Lake Junction and loops past June, Gull, Silver and Grant lakes under Carson Peak and the steep east face of the Sierra, rejoining 395 north of Grant Lake. The north part closes in winter.

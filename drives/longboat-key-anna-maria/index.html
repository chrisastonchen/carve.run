Longboat Key to Anna Maria

789, Gulf of Mexico, Gulf Drive, Gulf Dr, John Ringling · Sarasota Bay, Florida

SR 789 north from St. Armands Circle up Longboat Key, over the Longboat Pass drawbridge, then Gulf Drive through Bradenton Beach and Holmes Beach to Anna Maria: a slow barrier-island road past mangroves, beaches and low island towns.

North Cascades Highway

WA 20, North Cascades Highway, SR 20, State Route 20, Highway 20 · North Cascades, Washington

Highway 20 from Marblemount up the Skagit River past turquoise Diablo Lake, over 4,855-foot Rainy Pass and 5,477-foot Washington Pass, then down a long hairpin below Liberty Bell to Mazama. No gas in between; closed in winter.

When to go

Closed by snow between Ross Dam and the Silver Star gate near Mazama from about late November to late April or May. Usually closed December to March; it's open the rest of the year.

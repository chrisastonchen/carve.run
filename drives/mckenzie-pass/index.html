McKenzie Pass: Highway 242

OR 242, McKenzie · Central Cascades, Oregon

OR-242 from the McKenzie River up tight switchbacks through old forest to the lava fields at the pass and the Dee Wright Observatory, then down through pine to Sisters. Narrow, with no vehicles over 35 feet; closed by snow from November to June.

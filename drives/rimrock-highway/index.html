The drive

SD 44 begins at US 385 west of Rapid City and, on the western outskirts, passes north of Canyon Lake. This drive follows it up from Canyon Lake in Rapid City for about 14 miles to US 385 near Pactola.

Pactola Reservoir is the largest and deepest body of water in the Black Hills. Its dam sits on Rapid Creek about 10 miles west of Rapid City, and US 385 crosses the top of it. The Forest Service visitor center at the dam's south end has exhibits, travel information and views of the reservoir.

Questions about this drive

How long does Rimrock Highway take?

SD 44 from Rapid City's Canyon Lake to US 385 near Pactola is 14.3 miles and takes about 20 minutes.

Where does the Rimrock Highway end?

This drive ends at US 385 near Pactola Reservoir, whose dam is about 10 miles west of Rapid City.

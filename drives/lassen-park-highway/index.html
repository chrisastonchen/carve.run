Lassen Volcanic Park Highway

CA 89, Lassen · Lassen Volcanic National Park, California

Highway 89 through Lassen Volcanic National Park, from the Mineral side past the Sulphur Works and Lake Helen to an 8,512-foot crest, then down past Summit Lake and the Devastated Area to Manzanita Lake. $30 park fee; closed by snow in winter.

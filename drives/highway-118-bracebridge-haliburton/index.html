The drive

Highway 118 runs east from Highway 11 at Bracebridge across Muskoka and into the Haliburton Highlands. It's a 48-mile drive of about 55 minutes through Canadian Shield country: rock cuts, lakes and wetlands, with steady curves rather than tight ones.

The route passes Carnarvon and ends in the town of Haliburton. It's a quiet cross-country link between Muskoka and the Highlands.

Questions about this drive

How long does Highway 118 from Bracebridge to Haliburton take?

Bracebridge to Haliburton is 48.1 miles and takes about 55 minutes.

Is Highway 118 twisty?

It has steady curves through rock cuts and lakes, but it's a leisurely drive rather than a tight, twisty one.

Where does the Highway 118 drive start?

It starts at Highway 11 in Bracebridge and runs east to Haliburton.

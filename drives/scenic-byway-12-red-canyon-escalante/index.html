Highway 12: Red Canyon to Escalante

UT 12, SR 12, Scenic Byway 12, Journey Through Time, Main Street · Bryce Canyon Country, Utah

Scenic Byway 12 from US-89 through the red hoodoos and two rock tunnels of Red Canyon, past the Bryce Canyon turnoff, down to Tropic and across the Grand Staircase–Escalante badlands to Escalante. Open all year; snow can close it briefly.

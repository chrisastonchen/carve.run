The drive

Highway 18 along the rim of the San Bernardino Mountains from Crestline past the Lake Arrowhead turnoff, Running Springs and Snow Valley to Big Bear Lake, with long views down to the valley on clear days. Busy on weekends; chains may be required in winter.

Davis Mountains Scenic Loop

TX 118, TX 166, TX 17, State Street, State Highway 118, State Highway 166 · Davis Mountains, Texas

The loop from Fort Davis: TX-118 up Limpia Canyon and past McDonald Observatory into the heart of the Davis Mountains, then TX-166 around the west side past Sawtooth Mountain and back to Fort Davis on TX-17. Open range, so watch for cattle.

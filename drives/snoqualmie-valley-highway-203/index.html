Snoqualmie Valley: Highway 203

WA 203, WA 202, Fall City, Carnation, Railroad Avenue, SR 203, State Route 203, Highway 203, SR 202, State Route 202, Highway 202, Lewis Street, Tolt Avenue · Snoqualmie Valley, Washington

Highway 203 follows the Snoqualmie River's farm valley south from Monroe through Duvall, Carnation and Fall City, with the Cascade foothills on both sides, then Highway 202 climbs beside the river to 268-foot Snoqualmie Falls.

Highway 16: Strawn to Possum Kingdom

TX 16, FM 2353, Central Avenue, State Highway 16 · Palo Pinto Mountains, Texas

TX-16 north from Strawn over the Palo Pinto Mountains, then FM 2353 along the east shore of Possum Kingdom Lake past Morris Sheppard Dam and back to TX-16: open ranch hills first, then lake and cliff views. Mostly long sweepers.

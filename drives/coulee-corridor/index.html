Coulee Corridor

SR 17, SR 155, US 2, Coulee Boulevard, Grand Coulee Avenue · Grand Coulee, Washington

SR 17 and SR 155 up the Grand Coulee from Soap Lake to the town of Grand Coulee: past the Lake Lenore Caves and Dry Falls, once a waterfall several miles wide, then along Banks Lake beneath Steamboat Rock. Straight roads; the basalt walls carry it.

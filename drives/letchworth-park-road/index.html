The drive

Letchworth Park Road runs the length of the west rim of the Genesee River gorge in Letchworth State Park. The river drops over three major waterfalls here, and the cliffs rise as high as 600 feet in places.

This drive starts at the Portageville entrance, the south end, where the Glen Iris Inn and the Middle and Upper Falls are. It then follows Park Road north along the rim, past Inspiration Point, and leaves through the Mount Morris entrance. The route has two twisty stretches.

The road is slow by design, with overlooks, trailheads and picnic areas along it. It is a pleasant half hour without stops, and most visitors take much longer.

Good to know

  • The park is open year-round, 6 am to 11 pm.
  • A vehicle entrance fee is collected from early May through late October, 9 am to 5 pm.
  • Overlooks and trailheads along the road mean stops and pedestrians.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Letchworth State Park.

Questions about this drive

How long does Letchworth Park Road take to drive?

Letchworth Park Road from Portageville to Mount Morris is 14.9 miles and takes about 30 minutes without stopping.

Is there a fee to drive through Letchworth State Park?

Yes. The park charges a per-vehicle entrance fee, collected from early May through late October between 9 am and 5 pm.

Is Letchworth State Park open in winter?

Yes. The park is open year-round, 6 am to 11 pm.

Where is Inspiration Point in Letchworth?

Inspiration Point is an overlook on the west rim of the gorge, passed on Park Road between Portageville and Mount Morris.

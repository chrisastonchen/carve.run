Highway 79: Descanso to Julian

CA 79, CA-79, Route 79, Rucker · Cuyamaca Mountains, California

Highway 79 from Descanso through Cuyamaca Rancho State Park, past Lake Cuyamaca and through oak woodland and meadows, to the old gold-mining town of Julian, known for its apple pie. Gentle, well-graded curves most of the way.

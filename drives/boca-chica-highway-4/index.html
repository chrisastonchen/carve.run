Highway 4: Boca Chica

TX 4, Boca Chica · Rio Grande Delta, Texas

TX-4 east from Brownsville across the tidal flats of the Rio Grande delta, past the Palmito Ranch battlefield and SpaceX's Starbase launch site, to Boca Chica Beach near the mouth of the river. Closed at times for launches and tests.

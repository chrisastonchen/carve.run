The drive

Highway 4 is the only road to Boca Chica Beach, at the southern tip of the Texas coast. It runs east from Brownsville across the flat tidal country of the Rio Grande delta, with tidal flats to both sides and almost no bends at all.

About a dozen miles out it passes the Palmito Ranch battlefield, where the last land battle of the Civil War was fought on May 12 and 13, 1865, a month after Lee's surrender; a state marker and a small viewing platform stand by the road. Farther on, the highway runs through Starbase, the SpaceX launch site that is now its own city, and ends at the beach, near where the Rio Grande meets the Gulf.

Good to know

  • Highway 4 and the beach close at times for launches and testing; since September 2025 the City of Starbase sets the closures and posts them.
  • TxDOT plans to widen about 14 miles of Highway 4 into a four-lane divided highway, so expect work zones once construction starts.
  • ATVs and off-road vehicles are banned on the beach, dunes and tidal flats.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check City of Starbase: Beach and Road Access.

Questions about this drive

How long does Highway 4 from Brownsville to Boca Chica Beach take?

Highway 4 from Brownsville to Boca Chica Beach is 17.3 miles and takes about 25 minutes.

Is Boca Chica Beach open?

Boca Chica Beach and Highway 4 close at times for SpaceX launches and tests; the City of Starbase's Beach and Road Access page shows the current status.

Who closes Highway 4 to Boca Chica?

Since September 2025, a state law lets the City of Starbase close Highway 4 and Boca Chica Beach for launches and testing; Cameron County used to do it.

What is the Palmito Ranch battlefield?

Palmito Ranch, beside Highway 4 east of Brownsville, is where the last land battle of the Civil War was fought, on May 12 and 13, 1865.

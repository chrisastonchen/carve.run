The drive

The Prospect Mountain Veterans Memorial Highway is a short toll road with one job: getting you high above Lake George. It climbs in tight curves from US 9, just south of Lake George village, to parking areas near the summit, with the lake laid out below and the Adirondacks behind it.

It's a dead end, so you come back down the way you went up. The view is the point; take your time at the top. The last stretch to the summit itself is on foot or by shuttle from the upper parking area.

Good to know

  • There's a toll per car and a lower one per motorcycle, collected by New York's DEC.
  • Storms can close it for a day or two even in season.

Open from late May to about Veterans Day (November 11). Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NY DEC: Prospect Mountain Veterans Memorial Highway.

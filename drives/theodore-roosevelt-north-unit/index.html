North Unit Scenic Drive

Scenic Loop Drive, Scenic Drive · North Dakota Badlands, North Dakota

The 14-mile road into Theodore Roosevelt National Park's North Unit, above the Little Missouri past cannonball concretions and River Bend Overlook, climbing to the end of the road at Oxbow Overlook. Park entrance fee.

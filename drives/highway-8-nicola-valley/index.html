The drive

Highway 8 follows the Nicola River downstream from Merritt to Spences Bridge. It starts in ranchland and sagebrush hills and narrows into a canyon as it nears the Fraser.

The 2021 atmospheric river washed out long sections. The province rebuilt them over the following years, restoring 13 kilometers of lost roadway, reinstating three bridges and building two new ones, and the highway is open to two-lane traffic again.

It is a quiet road with a few reduced-speed sections, and it ends just short of the Trans-Canada at Spences Bridge.

Questions about this drive

How long does Highway 8 from Merritt to Spences Bridge take?

Highway 8 from Merritt to Spences Bridge is 39 miles and takes about 55 minutes.

Is Highway 8 open after the 2021 floods?

Yes. Highway 8 is open to two-lane traffic after long repairs from the 2021 floods.

Where does Highway 8 end?

Highway 8 meets the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1) at Spences Bridge.

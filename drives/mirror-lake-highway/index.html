Mirror Lake Highway

UT 150, SR 150, Mirror Lake, Center Street · Uinta Mountains, Utah

UT-150 from Kamas up the Provo River into the Uinta Mountains, over Bald Mountain Pass at 10,700 feet past Mirror Lake and dozens of others, and down the Bear River toward Wyoming. Closed by snow in winter; fee to stop.

When to go

The high section closes by snow from about November to late May or June. Usually closed December to April; it's open the rest of the year.

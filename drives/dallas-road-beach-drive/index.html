The drive

Victoria's waterfront drive from Ogden Point to Cattle Point: Dallas Road past Beacon Hill Park, Clover Point and Ross Bay, then King George Terrace and Beach Drive through Oak Bay past Willows Beach, facing the Olympic Mountains. Slow, with cyclists.

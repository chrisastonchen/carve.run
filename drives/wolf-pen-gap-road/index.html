The drive

Georgia 180 begins at Georgia 60 in Suches, inside the Chattahoochee-Oconee National Forest. This drive takes it east for 11 miles over Wolfpen Gap to US 129 near Vogel State Park, in about 20 minutes. It is Intense, with three twisty stretches in the second half.

The western section, known as Wolfpen Gap Road, runs about 11.8 miles. It passes Vogel State Park, the access to Sosebee Cove, a Coosa Backcountry Trail crossing and Lake Winfield Scott. The highest point on the highway is Wolfpen Gap, at 3,260 feet. Georgia 180 meets US 19, US 129 and Georgia 11 near Vogel State Park.

Highway 168: Bishop to Lake Sabrina

West Line Street, CA 168, Lake Sabrina Road · Eastern Sierra, California

Highway 168 west from Bishop crosses the Owens Valley and climbs Bishop Creek canyon, known for its aspens turning gold in early fall, to Lake Sabrina below the Sierra crest. The last mile above Aspendell closes in winter.

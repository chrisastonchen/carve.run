Highway 168: Tollhouse to Huntington Lake

Tollhouse Road, Huntington Lake Road, CA 168 · Sierra National Forest, California

The old Tollhouse grade climbs from the foothills at Tollhouse in tight switchbacks to rejoin Highway 168, which carries on through oak and then pine forest past Shaver Lake and up to Huntington Lake, where the highway ends.

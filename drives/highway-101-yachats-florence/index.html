Highway 101: Yachats to Florence

US 101, Oregon Coast · Central Oregon Coast, Oregon

US-101 south from Yachats along the shore of Cape Perpetua, past the tide pools of Strawberry Hill and Neptune, then around Heceta Head's lighthouse and Sea Lion Caves before dropping to Florence. Ocean on the right the whole way, with turnouts for the views.

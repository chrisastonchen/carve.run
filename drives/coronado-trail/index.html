Coronado Trail: US-191

191, Coronado · White Mountains, Arizona

US-191 climbs from Clifton past the vast Morenci copper mine and up the Mogollon Rim in hundreds of tight bends, many posted at 10 to 15 mph, through pine and aspen to Hannagan Meadow and Alpine. Allow three hours or more; few services.

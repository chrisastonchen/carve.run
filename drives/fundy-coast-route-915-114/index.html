The drive

This drive starts at Hopewell Cape on Route 114. Hopewell Rocks Provincial Park is there: over twenty free-standing sea stacks along two kilometres of shoreline, with an ocean floor you can walk at low tide. The park's admission is valid for two consecutive days so you can see both high and low tides, and its own tide times say when it is safe on the ocean floor.

From Riverside-Albert the drive takes Route 915 along Shepody Bay to Alma, then Route 114 into Fundy National Park, on the Bay of Fundy, where the tides are a major draw. It ends at Wolfe Lake.

Good to know

  • The Hopewell Rocks charge their own admission, separate from Fundy National Park.
  • Fundy National Park charges daily admission; check Parks Canada's fees page for how it applies to your stops.
  • Check New Brunswick 511 for roadwork and closures before you go.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check New Brunswick 511.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Fundy Coast drive from Hopewell Cape to Alma take?

Hopewell Cape to Wolfe Lake on Routes 114 and 915 is 43.4 miles and takes about 1 hr 15 min without stops.

Do the Hopewell Rocks need their own ticket?

Yes. Hopewell Rocks Provincial Park sells its own admission, valid for two consecutive days. Check the park's tide times before you go.

Where is Fundy National Park?

It is near the village of Alma, on Highway 114, on the southeast coast of New Brunswick along the Bay of Fundy.

The drive

US 52 south of Dubuque is the Iowa Great River Road alternate route, and it follows the Mississippi River valley down to Bellevue. The road runs through Sageville and along Twin Valley Drive, with bluffs rising on one side and the river bottoms on the other.

Near the start, the road gives access to the Mines of Spain, a state recreation area named for a 1796 land grant to Julien Dubuque. The E.B. Lyons Interpretive Center there is a Great River Road interpretive center. The drive ends at Bellevue, where Bellevue State Park is known for its Mississippi River views and a butterfly garden.

The route is 24.5 miles, takes about 36 minutes and climbs 1,913 feet. Traffic is lighter outside weekends.

Good to know

  • Most of the drive is on a winding two-lane with a bluff on one side.
  • Services are in Dubuque and Bellevue, with little between them.
  • Weekends are busier than weekdays.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Iowa 511.

Catoctin Mountain: Highway 77

Foxville Road, MD 77 · Catoctin Mountain, Maryland

MD-77 west from Thurmont up Hunting Creek between Catoctin Mountain Park and Cunningham Falls State Park, then across the ridge through Foxville and down to Smithsburg. Short and curvy, an hour from Washington and Baltimore.

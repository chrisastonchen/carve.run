The drive

Yellowstone's East Entrance Road from the entrance to Fishing Bridge: a climb through burned and regrowing forest over 8,530-foot Sylvan Pass, past Sylvan Lake, then along Yellowstone Lake's north shore. Park fee; closed to cars Nov to Apr.

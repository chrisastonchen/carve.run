The drive

Highway 10 follows the West Branch of the Delaware River southwest from Walton. For most of the drive it runs along the northern edge of the Cannonsville Reservoir, with the water on one side and wooded hills on the other.

Near the reservoir's midpoint the road crosses the water on the Cannonsville Bridge. It then continues past the foot of the dam and runs to Highway 8 near Deposit.

The curves are gentle and traffic is light. Deposit is near the Highway 17 Quickway.

Questions about this drive

How long is Highway 10 from Walton to Deposit?

Highway 10 from Walton to Deposit is 24.2 miles and takes about 30 minutes.

Does Highway 10 cross the Cannonsville Reservoir?

Yes. Highway 10 crosses the water on the Cannonsville Bridge near the middle of the reservoir.

Where does Highway 10 end near Deposit?

This drive ends near Deposit, just before Highway 8 joins. Highway 10 meets the Highway 17 Quickway a little farther on.

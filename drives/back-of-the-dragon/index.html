Back of the Dragon

Park Boulevard, Tazewell Avenue, Main Street, VA 16 · Smyth and Tazewell Counties, Virginia

VA-16 from Marion to Tazewell, the Back of the Dragon: about 32 miles and 438 curves over Walker, Brushy and Clinch mountains, starting past Hungry Mother State Park. Busy with motorcyclists on fall weekends; the road's welcome center is in Tazewell.

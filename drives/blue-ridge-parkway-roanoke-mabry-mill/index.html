Blue Ridge Parkway: Roanoke to Mabry Mill

Blue Ridge Parkway · Blue Ridge, Virginia

The Parkway south from US-220 at Roanoke onto the Floyd plateau: farm fields and split-rail fences, Smart View and Rocky Knob, ending at Mabry Mill, one of the most photographed places on the Parkway. No fee; bridge work may bring single-lane stops.

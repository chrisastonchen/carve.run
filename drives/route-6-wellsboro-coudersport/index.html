Route 6: Wellsboro to Coudersport

US 6, Roosevelt, Tioga Street, Grand Army, West Main, East Second · Pennsylvania Wilds, Pennsylvania

US 6 west from Wellsboro across the Northern Tier to Coudersport: past Ansonia at the head of the Pine Creek Gorge, through Galeton and over Denton Hill by the Pennsylvania Lumber Museum. Easy curves through hardwood forest and small valley towns.

Kingsbury Grade

NV 207, Kingsbury · Carson Range, Nevada

NV-207 from Stateline on Lake Tahoe over 7,344-ft Daggett Pass and down the steep east face of the Carson Range to Foothill Road: tight switchbacks with long views across the Carson Valley. Chain controls in winter storms.

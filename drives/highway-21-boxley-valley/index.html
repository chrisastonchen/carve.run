Highway 21: Clarksville to Ponca

AR 21, AR 43, AR 16, Highway 21 · Boston Mountains, Arkansas

AR-21 north from Clarksville through the Ozark National Forest by Ozone and Fallsville over the Boston Mountains, then AR-43 down through Boxley Valley to Ponca on the upper Buffalo River. Elk graze the valley fields, most often at dawn and dusk.

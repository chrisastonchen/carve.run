Highway 80: Otsego Lake

NY 80, Lake Street, State Route 80 · Cooperstown, New York

NY-80 north from Cooperstown along the west shore of Otsego Lake, past the Fenimore Art Museum and the Glimmerglass Opera, with the lake through the trees much of the way, to Springfield Center at the lake's north end.

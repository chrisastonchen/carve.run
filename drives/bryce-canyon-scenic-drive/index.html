The drive

Highway 63 enters Bryce Canyon and continues as the park road along the plateau rim, climbing south through ponderosa and fir. It passes the amphitheater turnoffs and Natural Bridge, and ends at Rainbow Point at 9,100 feet. It is 18.9 miles and about 40 minutes, with one twisty stretch.

The road dead-ends at the point. The Park Service suggests stopping at the viewpoints on the way back, when they are on the right.

A park entrance fee applies.

Good to know

  • The park entrance fee is $35 per private vehicle, and non-US residents age 16 and older pay an extra $100, according to the Park Service.
  • The road can close past mile 3 for a day or more after snowstorms.
  • Check the park's alerts page for current road status.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check National Park Service: Bryce Canyon conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long is the Bryce Canyon scenic drive?

From Highway 12 to Rainbow Point is 18.9 miles and takes about 40 minutes without stops.

How much does it cost to drive Bryce Canyon?

The park charges $35 per private vehicle. Non-US residents 16 and older pay an extra $100 per person.

Is the Bryce Canyon road open in winter?

Yes, but it can close past mile 3 for a day or more after snowstorms.

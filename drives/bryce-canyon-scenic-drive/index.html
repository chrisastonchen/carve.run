Bryce Canyon Scenic Drive

UT 63, SR 63, Bryce Canyon, Scenic Drive, South Main Street · Bryce Canyon National Park, Utah

UT-63 into Bryce Canyon and along the park road on the plateau rim, climbing south through ponderosa and fir past the amphitheater turnoffs and Natural Bridge to Rainbow Point at 9,100 feet. Park entrance fee; the road dead-ends at the point.

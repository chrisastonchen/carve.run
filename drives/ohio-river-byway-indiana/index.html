The drive

IN-56 east from Madison to Vevay, then IN-156 past Markland Dam, Florence and Patriot to Rising Sun: the Ohio River Scenic Byway along the river's bottomlands and bluffs. Gentle curves rather than twisty, with the river close by much of the way.

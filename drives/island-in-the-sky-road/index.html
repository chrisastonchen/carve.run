Island in the Sky Road

UT 313, SR 313, Island in the Sky, Grand View, Dead Horse · Canyonlands National Park, Utah

UT-313 from US-191 up a canyon and a set of switchbacks onto the mesa, then the Canyonlands park road south along the Island in the Sky past Mesa Arch to Grand View Point, high above the White Rim. Park entrance fee.

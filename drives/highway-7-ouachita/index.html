Highway 7: Hot Springs to Ola

AR 7, Scenic 7 · Ouachita Mountains, Arkansas

The Ouachita stretch of the Arkansas Scenic 7 Byway: north from Hot Springs through the Ouachita National Forest by Jessieville and Hollis, over pine-covered ridges and river crossings to Ola. Two lanes and steady curves most of the way.

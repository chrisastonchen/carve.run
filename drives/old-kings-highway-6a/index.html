Old King's Highway (Route 6A)

MA 6A, Main Street, Old King, Route 6A, Sandwich Road, Cranberry Highway · Cape Cod, Massachusetts

MA-6A along Cape Cod Bay's north-side towns from Sandwich to Orleans: sea captains' houses, salt marsh, old churches and village centers in Barnstable, Yarmouth Port, Dennis and Brewster. A slow, historic two-lane rather than a fast one.

The drive

Route 6A, most of it known as the Old King's Highway, is described as Cape Cod's most historic and scenic highway. This drive runs east from the Sagamore Bridge end at Sandwich through Barnstable, Yarmouth, Dennis and Brewster to Orleans, and the road changes names by town, including Main Street, Old King's Highway and Cranberry Highway.

The Barnstable portion is listed on the National Register of Historic Places as the Old King's Highway Historic District. This is a village road along Cape Cod Bay's north side.

The route is 32.7 miles and takes about 1 hr 10 min, with gentle curves and 900 feet of climbing.

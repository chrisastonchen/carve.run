The drive

US-199 leaves the coast through the redwoods of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park at Hiouchi, then follows the clear Middle Fork of the Smith River upstream past Gasquet and Patrick Creek, through the Collier Tunnel to the Oregon line.

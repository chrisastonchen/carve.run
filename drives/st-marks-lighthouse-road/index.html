Lighthouse Road: St. Marks Refuge

Lighthouse, 59 · Big Bend, Florida

Lighthouse Road south from US 98 through St. Marks National Wildlife Refuge to the St. Marks Lighthouse on Apalachee Bay: a flat, quiet road over salt marsh and pools full of wading birds and winter ducks. The refuge charges an entrance fee.

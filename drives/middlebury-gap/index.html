Middlebury Gap: Route 125

VT 125, Middlebury Gap, Vermont Route 125, East Main Street · Green Mountains, Vermont

VT-125, the Robert Frost Memorial Drive, from Hancock up through the Middlebury Gap and down past Middlebury College's Bread Loaf campus and Ripton to East Middlebury. Forested climbs, then a long descent beside the Middlebury River.

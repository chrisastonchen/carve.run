The drive

Highway 97 runs north from Port Jervis, beside the Delaware River. The first miles hold its most famous stretch, the Hawk's Nest: a winding road along cliffs high above the river, with a low wall beside the drop.

The Nest was a one-lane dirt track used by loggers and coal miners until it was widened and paved in the early 1930s. Past it the road runs beside the river through Barryville, where a bridge crosses to Pennsylvania, and passes the turn to Roebling's Delaware Aqueduct at Minisink Ford. It ends at Narrowsburg, where it meets Highway 52.

The highway is the Upper Delaware Scenic Byway. It takes about 45 minutes, and Hawk's Nest is the slowest part.

Good to know

  • The Hawk's Nest is posted at 40 mph, and 25 mph is advised.
  • The Nest is popular with motorcyclists, especially on weekends, and crashes are common.
  • Check 511NY before a winter trip.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check 511NY traffic and travel information.

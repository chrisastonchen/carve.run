Yakima River Canyon

SR 821, Canyon Road · Yakima River, Washington

SR 821 through the Yakima River Canyon from Ellensburg to Selah: two lanes that follow every bend of the river between sagebrush hills and basalt cliffs, past riverside recreation sites and Roza Dam. The slow, scenic way around I-82.

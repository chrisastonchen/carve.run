Lincoln Highway: Fort Loudon to Breezewood

US 30, Lincoln · Tuscarora Mountain, Pennsylvania

US 30, the Lincoln Highway, from Fort Loudon up Tuscarora Mountain, through McConnellsburg and over the ridges beyond to Breezewood: steep grades and sharp curves with long views over the valleys. Trucks use it; take the descents slowly.

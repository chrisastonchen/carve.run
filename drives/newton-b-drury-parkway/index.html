Newton B. Drury Scenic Parkway

Drury · Prairie Creek Redwoods, California

A two-lane bypass of US-101 through Prairie Creek Redwoods State Park, with old-growth redwoods at the road's edge and Roosevelt elk on Elk Prairie by the visitor center. Closed to cars on the first Saturday of each month, October to May.

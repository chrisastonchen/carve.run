Kings Hill Scenic Byway: US-89

US 89, North Main Street, East Main Street · Little Belt Mountains, Montana

US-89 over the Little Belt Mountains from near Belt to White Sulphur Springs: up Belt Creek's canyon past Monarch and the old mining town of Neihart, over Kings Hill Pass by Showdown ski area, then down to the Smith River valley.

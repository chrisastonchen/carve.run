Mount Rogers Scenic Byway: Highway 603

Konnarock Road, Fairwood Road, VA 603, Laurel Valley Road · Mount Rogers, Virginia

VA-603 from Konnarock to Troutdale, the northern leg of the Mount Rogers Scenic Byway: a quiet two-lane through Laurel Valley and Fairwood Valley below Mount Rogers, the state's highest peak, crossing the Appalachian Trail.

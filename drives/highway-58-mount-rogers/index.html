Highway 58: Damascus to Volney

Laurel Avenue, Highlands Parkway, Jeb Stuart Highway, US 58 · Mount Rogers, Virginia

US-58 east from Damascus up Whitetop Laurel Creek and across the high country below Whitetop and Mount Rogers, past Grayson Highlands State Park to Volney. Narrow and very twisty; the stretch near Damascus was rebuilt after Hurricane Helene.

The drive

Skyline Drive is the only public road in Shenandoah National Park, 105 miles along the Blue Ridge crest, with 75 overlooks looking west over the Shenandoah Valley and east to the Piedmont. This drive covers the middle section, from Thornton Gap (mile 31.5) to Swift Run Gap (mile 65.5): 33.8 miles in about an hour.

The road passes through Marys Rock Tunnel at mile 32.2, then Skyland, the road's highest point at 3,680 feet. Big Meadows, the park's largest developed area, sits at mile 51. The curves are mostly moderate, with three twisty stretches.

Good to know

  • Entrance is $30 per private vehicle, good for seven consecutive days, and the park takes only card or electronic payment.
  • The speed limit is 35 mph in most places, and deer, black bears and wild turkeys cross the road.
  • Marys Rock Tunnel at mile 32.2 has a maximum clearance of 12 feet 8 inches in both lanes.
  • Weather can close the road at any time.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Shenandoah National Park: alerts and conditions.

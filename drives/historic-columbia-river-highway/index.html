Historic Columbia River Highway

Historic Columbia River, US 30 · Columbia River Gorge, Oregon

The 1920s highway from Troutdale up the Sandy River to Vista House at Crown Point, then down past Latourell, Multnomah and Horsetail falls to Ainsworth. Narrow, with stone guard walls and tight curves; crowded with cars and walkers on summer weekends.

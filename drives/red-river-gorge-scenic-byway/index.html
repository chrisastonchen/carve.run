Red River Gorge Scenic Byway

KY 77, KY 715, Nada Tunnel, Red River Road, Tarr Ridge, Sky Bridge · Red River Gorge, Kentucky

KY 77 from Nada through the one-lane, unlit Nada Tunnel into the Red River Gorge, then KY 715 along the river past Gladie and up to Sky Bridge Road and Pine Ridge: narrow, tight and wooded, with sandstone cliffs and arches nearby.

Generals Highway

Generals Highway, CA 198, Sierra Drive · Sequoia and Kings Canyon, California

Highway 198 from Three Rivers becomes the Generals Highway in Sequoia: a steep, switchbacked climb from the Kaweah foothills to the sequoias of Giant Forest, then forest curves past Lodgepole to Grant Grove. Park fee; vehicles over 22 ft not advised low down.

When to go

Lodgepole to Grant Grove is unplowed from early January to late March and often closed by snow. Usually closed February; it's open the rest of the year.

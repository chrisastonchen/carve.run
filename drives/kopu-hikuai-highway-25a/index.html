The drive

State Highway 25A is the shortcut across the Coromandel Peninsula: 31 km (20 mi) from Kōpū, near Thames, over the ranges to Hikuai, close to Tairua on the east coast. It takes about half an hour, with long climbs through bush and moderate bends, and one twistier stretch.

The road's best-known feature is the Taparahi Bridge, a 124 m, 15 m high span that NZTA built after ex-Cyclone Hale washed out a large section of the highway in January 2023. It opened on 20 December 2023, about three months ahead of schedule.

The drive ends on Tairua Road at Hikuai, where State Highway 25 carries on to Tairua and Whangamatā.

Questions about this drive

How long does Highway 25A from Kōpū to Hikuai take?

State Highway 25A from Kōpū to Hikuai is 31 km (20 mi) and takes about 30 minutes.

What happened to Highway 25A on the Coromandel?

Ex-Cyclone Hale washed out a large section of State Highway 25A in January 2023, and the new 124 m Taparahi Bridge opened on 20 December 2023.

Is Highway 25A a good shortcut to the east coast?

Yes. State Highway 25A is the quickest way across the Coromandel ranges from Kōpū near Thames to Hikuai near Tairua.

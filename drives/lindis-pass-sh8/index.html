The drive

Lindis Pass is the high point of State Highway 8 between Central Otago and the Mackenzie country. This drive runs 74 km (46 mi) from Tarras north-east over the pass to Omarama, climbing to about 971 m on the boundary between the Central Otago and Waitaki districts.

The road is open tussock country on both sides with long sweeping bends and big sky. The Lindis Conservation Area lies beside the highway, and a car park and short walk at the summit give a view over the snow tussock. Curves are mostly gentle, and the drive takes about 55 minutes.

Tarras is where SH8A from Luggate meets SH8. Omarama is the junction for SH83 toward Kurow and Oamaru.

Good to know

  • Snow can close the pass briefly in winter; closures are short and unscheduled.
  • Few services between Tarras and Omarama; fill up beforehand.
  • The summit car park and short walk are the usual stop.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NZTA Journey Planner: highway conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does Lindis Pass SH8 take from Tarras to Omarama?

Tarras to Omarama over Lindis Pass is 74 km (46 mi) and takes about 55 minutes.

How high is Lindis Pass?

Lindis Pass is about 971 m, the highest point on SH8.

Is Lindis Pass open in winter?

Usually, but snow can close it briefly. Check NZTA before you travel.

Is there fuel on Lindis Pass?

Services are few between Tarras and Omarama, so fill up beforehand.

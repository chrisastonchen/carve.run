Roan Mountain: Highway 143

TN 143, SR 143, Roan · Roan Highlands, Tennessee

Highway 143 from the town of Roan Mountain up the Doe River through Roan Mountain State Park and on to Carvers Gap, 5,512 feet up on the North Carolina line, where the Appalachian Trail crosses the grassy balds. Tight bends near the top; icy in winter.

Akamina Parkway

Akamina · Waterton Lakes, Alberta

The Akamina Parkway from Waterton townsite up the Cameron Creek valley to Cameron Lake: 16 km of winding mountain road through forest burned in the 2017 Kenow fire. Closed past Little Prairie from early November to mid-May. Parks Canada pass required.

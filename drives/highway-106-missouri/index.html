Highway 106: Eminence to Ellington

MO 106 · Ozark National Scenic Riverways, Missouri

MO-106 east from Eminence through the hills of the Ozark National Scenic Riverways, crossing the Current River at Powder Mill near Owls Bend, then through forest to Ellington. A quiet two-lane that pairs with Highway 19 into Eminence.

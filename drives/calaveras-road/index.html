Calaveras Road

Calaveras Road · Diablo Range, California

Calaveras Road from Milpitas to Sunol: a steep, twisting climb out of the valley, then a narrow, winding run along Calaveras Reservoir through oak hills and ranchland. Popular with cyclists and motorcyclists; little room to pass.

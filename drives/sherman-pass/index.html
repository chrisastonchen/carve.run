Sherman Pass: Highway 20

SR 20, State Highway 20, Clark Avenue · Kettle River Range, Washington

SR 20 over Sherman Pass (5,575 ft), the highest pass Washington keeps open all winter, from the Columbia near Kettle Falls to Republic: steady curves through the Kettle River Range, with silver snags from old fires near the summit.

Alpine Loop: Highway 92

UT 92, SR 92, Alpine Loop, Alpine Scenic · Wasatch Mountains, Utah

UT-92 up American Fork Canyon and over the shoulder of Mount Timpanogos at 8,000 feet, through aspen and fir past Sundance to Provo Canyon. Narrow, with tight switchbacks and stretches with no center line. Closed by snow from about November to late May.

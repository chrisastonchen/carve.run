Hurricane Ridge Road

Hurricane Ridge Road, Race Street · Olympic Peninsula, Washington

Hurricane Ridge Road climbs 17 miles from Port Angeles to about 5,200 feet in Olympic National Park, through three short tunnels to subalpine meadows facing the Olympic Mountains. Park entrance fee; open only some days in winter.

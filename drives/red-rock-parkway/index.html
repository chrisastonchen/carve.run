The drive

Red Rock Parkway runs 15 km up the Blakiston Valley in Waterton Lakes National Park, through rolling grassland. It ends at Red Rock Canyon, where a self-guided trail loops around the canyon.

Parks Canada calls it a great place to see wildlife and notes scenic pulloffs along the way. The drive is 7.1 miles from the Waterton entrance road up the valley.

Closed to cars from late October (October 26 in 2026) to early May. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Parks Canada: Waterton driving.

Questions about this drive

When is Red Rock Parkway open?

Red Rock Parkway is open to vehicles from early May to late October. It is closed to motor vehicles from November through May but stays open to cyclists and hikers.

How long does Red Rock Parkway take?

Red Rock Parkway is 7.1 miles from the Waterton entrance road to Red Rock Canyon and takes about 20 minutes.

Can RVs drive Red Rock Parkway?

Parks Canada says the road is narrow and may not be suitable for larger motorhomes.

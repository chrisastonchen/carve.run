Red Rock Parkway

Red Rock · Waterton Lakes, Alberta

The Red Rock Parkway from the Waterton entrance road up the Blakiston Valley to Red Rock Canyon: 15 km where prairie grassland meets the mountains, with views of Mount Blakiston. Open to cars early May to late October. Parks Canada pass required.

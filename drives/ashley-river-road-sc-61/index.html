The drive

The Ashley River Road, part of Highway 61, runs northwest from Charleston up the Ashley River to Summerville. It is widely described as one of the oldest roads in South Carolina. It was authorized by the Lords Proprietors in 1690, and it became a State Scenic Byway in 1998 and a National Scenic Byway in 2000.

The road is four lanes near Sam Rittenberg Boulevard and becomes two lanes through the scenic stretch, with an oak canopy. It passes Drayton Hall, Magnolia Gardens, Middleton Place and St. Andrews Church.

The route is 13.9 miles and takes about 22 minutes, with 112 feet of climb.

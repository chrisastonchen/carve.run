Highway 311: Catawba to Paint Bank

Catawba Valley Drive, VA 311, WV 311, Paint Bank Road · Allegheny Highlands, Virginia and West Virginia

VA-311 from Salem over Catawba Mountain past the Dragons Tooth trailhead, down the Catawba Valley and on through New Castle and Paint Bank, then over Peters Mountain to Sweet Springs, West Virginia. Sweeping two-lane with a few tight climbs.

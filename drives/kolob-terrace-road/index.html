Kolob Terrace Road

Kolob Terrace, Kolob Reservoir · Zion National Park, Utah

Kolob Terrace Road from Virgin up into Zion's quiet western side: a climb of about 4,500 feet past white and red sandstone domes and old lava flows to pine forest at Kolob Reservoir. Paved to the reservoir; the upper part closes for winter.

The drive

The Forest Service lists this as the TN 91 Scenic Drive. From Elizabethton at US 19E it follows Stony Creek through farmland, framed by Holston and Iron Mountains, to the top of Cross Mountain.

It then drops into Shady Valley, a farming community known for its cranberry bogs. The drive is 21.5 miles, climbs about 2,200 feet to a high point of 3,500 feet, and ends at the Shady Valley crossroads with US 421, where The Snake can be driven either way.

Mohawk Trail

MA 2, Mohawk Trail · Berkshires, Massachusetts

MA-2 west from Greenfield up the Deerfield River valley through Charlemont and over Whitcomb Summit, then down the Hairpin Turn toward North Adams with the Hoosac Valley spread below. The old Massachusetts mountain route, now a scenic byway.

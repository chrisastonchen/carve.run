The drive

Provincial Road 307 enters Whiteshell Provincial Park at Seven Sisters Falls, off Highway 11, and winds through the west-central part of the park. Along the way it gives access to lakes including Nutimik, Dorothy, Betula and White, and to Otter Falls. The park has about 200 lakes in all, and this is one of its main roads in.

The drive runs east to Rennie, where PR 307 meets Highway 44, the La Vérendrye Trail. Highway 44 carries on east to West Hawk Lake, where the drive ends.

Most visitors from Winnipeg take Highway 1 to West Hawk Lake instead, which Manitoba Parks puts at 78.3 miles from the city.

Good to know

  • A Manitoba park vehicle permit is required for a motor vehicle visiting a provincial park.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Whiteshell drive on Highways 307 and 44 take?

Seven Sisters Falls to West Hawk Lake on PR 307 and Highway 44 is 63.4 miles and takes about 1 hr 30 min without stops.

Do I need a permit to drive through Whiteshell Provincial Park?

Manitoba requires a valid park vehicle permit for motor vehicles visiting a provincial park. Entering on foot or by bicycle does not need one, and the province lists a few free-entry days.

Is PR 307 in Whiteshell open?

Manitoba Parks has closed parts of PR 307 for floodwater in the past, so check Manitoba 511 for its current status before you go.

Spearfish Canyon Scenic Byway

Spearfish Canyon, US 14A, US Highway 14A · Northern Black Hills, South Dakota

US-14A up Spearfish Canyon from Spearfish to Cheyenne Crossing: the road follows Spearfish Creek between limestone walls, past Bridal Veil Falls and the lodge at Savoy, with gentle curves and gold aspen in autumn.

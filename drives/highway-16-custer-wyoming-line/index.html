The drive

This is US 16 heading west from Custer across the southern Black Hills toward the Wyoming line. It is 24.2 miles and takes about 29 minutes. The drive ends just short of the state line.

Jewel Cave National Monument sits on Highway 16 about 13 miles west of Custer, and the NPS says it is about a 25-minute drive from Custer. Newcastle, Wyoming, is 24 miles beyond the monument. Jewel Cave is a stop off the highway and is not part of this route.

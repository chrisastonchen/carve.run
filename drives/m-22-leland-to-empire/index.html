M-22: Leland to Empire

M 22, Main Street, Western Avenue, Ray Street, Leelanau Highway · Leelanau Peninsula, Michigan

M-22 through the Leelanau Peninsula from Leland to Empire: rolling two-lane past Good Harbor Bay and the Port Oneida farms, through Glen Arbor, and over the hills into Empire, much of it inside Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore.

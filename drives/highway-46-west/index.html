Highway 46 West: Templeton to Cambria

Green Valley Road, CA 46 · Santa Lucia Range, California

Highway 46 West crosses the Santa Lucia Range from Templeton's vineyards to the sea: sweeping curves through oak and vineyard country up to the ridge, then a long descent toward the ocean, ending at Highway 1 just south of Cambria.

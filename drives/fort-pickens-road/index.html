The drive

Fort Pickens Road runs west from Pensacola Beach through Gulf Islands National Seashore to the brick fort at the island's tip. It is a low road between white dunes, with the Gulf on one side and Pensacola Bay on the other. The drive is 8.6 miles.

The road dead-ends at the fort, so you come back the same way. Fort Pickens is a Civil War-era fort with a park store inside.

Sand and overwash close the road in storms and high surf.

Good to know

  • There is an entrance fee: $25 a vehicle for seven days, or an interagency pass.
  • The entrance station is open 5 a.m. to 8 p.m. March through October and 5 a.m. to 6 p.m. November through February.
  • Reaching Pensacola Beach crosses the Bob Sikes Bridge, which has a southbound toll.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Gulf Islands National Seashore: Conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long is Fort Pickens Road?

Fort Pickens Road from Pensacola Beach to the fort is 8.6 miles and takes about 20 minutes.

Is there a fee for Fort Pickens Road?

Yes. The national seashore charges $25 a vehicle, good for one to seven days; interagency passes are accepted.

Does Fort Pickens Road close?

Yes. Storms and high surf close it. Check the park's conditions page.

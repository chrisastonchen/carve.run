Highway 35: Mimbres Valley

NM 35, Sapillo · Gila National Forest, New Mexico

NM-35 from San Lorenzo up the Mimbres River valley past orchards, ranches and the village of Mimbres, then into pine forest by Lake Roberts to NM-15: a gently curving link between Emory Pass and the road to the Gila Cliff Dwellings.

The drive

US 220 heads north from Covington up the Jackson River valley, a two-lane road through ridge-and-valley country, past farms and forest. It climbs about 18 miles to Hot Springs, where the road is called Sam Snead Highway and the Homestead resort sits at the end.

The route is steady mountain curves and short.

US 220 here follows the Jackson River for a while, then crosses ridges into new valleys.

Questions about this drive

How long is US 220 from Covington to Hot Springs?

It is 18.3 miles and takes about 32 minutes.

Is US 220 a two-lane road?

Yes. US 220 is a two-lane road for the rest of its route from Covington to West Virginia.

What is at the end of the drive?

The drive ends in Hot Springs on Sam Snead Highway, near the Homestead resort.

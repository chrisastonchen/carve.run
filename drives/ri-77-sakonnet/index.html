The drive

Route 77 runs through Tiverton and Little Compton, along the Sakonnet River. This drive heads south from Tiverton Four Corners toward the water, ending near the harbor at Sakonnet Point in Little Compton.

The road is short and mostly flat, with tight bends in places and slow village stretches.

Cyclists are common.

Sunrise and Big Lake: AZ-273 and 261

273, 261, Big Lake, School Bus · White Mountains, Arizona

AZ-273 runs south past Sunrise Park Resort across high meadows and spruce forest near 9,000 feet toward Big Lake, then AZ-261 heads north past Crescent Lake and down into Round Valley at Eagar. Closed from about January to mid-April.

Highway 16: Wilkesboro to Glendale Springs

NC 16 · Blue Ridge Escarpment, North Carolina

NC-16 from Millers Creek near Wilkesboro up the Blue Ridge escarpment to the Parkway and Glendale Springs: rolling foothills, then a steep, curving climb to the crest. Glendale Springs is home to Holy Trinity Church and its frescoes.

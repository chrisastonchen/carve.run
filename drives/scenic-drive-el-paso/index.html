Scenic Drive, El Paso

Scenic Drive · Franklin Mountains, Texas

Scenic Drive along the south face of the Franklin Mountains above central El Paso, from Rim Road to Alabama Street, with the Murchison Rogers Park overlook across the city and Ciudad Juárez. Closed to cars on Sunday mornings.

Ortega Highway

Ortega, CA 74, CA-74 · Santa Ana Mountains, California

Highway 74 over the Santa Ana Mountains from San Juan Capistrano to Lake Elsinore, through Cleveland National Forest past the Ortega Oaks candy store, ending in a switchback descent with the lake below. Heavy commuter traffic on weekdays.

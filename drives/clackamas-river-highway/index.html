The drive

Oregon 224 runs from Estacada up the Clackamas River canyon to Ripplebrook, a two-lane that follows the river under basalt cliffs. It is a Portland-area drive that climbs gradually into Mt. Hood National Forest, and much of the forest along the upper part is recovering from the 2020 Riverside Fire.

The drive ends at Ripplebrook, where OR 224 becomes Forest Road 46. There are no services past Estacada, so fuel up first.

Good to know

  • OR 224 was closed from August 27, 2026 for firefighting work southeast of Estacada and reopened September 16, 2026; Forest Road 46 stayed closed at that time.
  • The upper canyon burned in the 2020 Riverside Fire; check conditions before you go.
  • There are no services past Estacada.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check ODOT TripCheck.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Clackamas River Highway take?

Highway 224 from Estacada to Ripplebrook is 24 miles and takes about 35 minutes.

Is Highway 224 open to Ripplebrook?

ODOT reopened OR 224 on September 16, 2026 after a firefighting closure. Check TripCheck for current conditions, since Forest Road 46 beyond Ripplebrook was still closed at that time.

Are there gas stations on the Clackamas River Highway?

No services are available past Estacada, so fill up there.

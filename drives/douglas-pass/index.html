Douglas Pass: Highway 139

CO 139, 13 Road, Douglas Pass Road · Book Cliffs, Colorado

CO-139 from Loma north through the Book Cliffs over 8,268-foot Douglas Pass and down Douglas Creek to Rangely, part of the Dinosaur Diamond byway: open desert, then switchbacks to the pass. Remote, with no services between the ends.

Sterling Highway: Kenai River

Sterling Highway, AK 1 · Kenai Peninsula, Alaska

The Sterling Highway from Tern Lake through Cooper Landing along the turquoise upper Kenai River, past the Russian River, into the Kenai National Wildlife Refuge. Expect summer fishing traffic and bypass construction flagging near the west end.

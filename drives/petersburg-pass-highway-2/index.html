The drive

Route 2, the Taconic Trail, leaves the hamlet of Petersburgh and climbs southeast through the woods of the Taconic Mountains. It crosses Petersburgh Pass, the high point at 2,100 feet, and descends into Massachusetts.

The last two miles are Massachusetts Route 2, which ends at US 7 in Williamstown. The road has three twisty stretches on the climb and descent.

The drive takes about 15 minutes.

Questions about this drive

How long is Petersburgh Pass on Route 2?

Route 2 from Petersburgh to Williamstown is 9.1 miles and takes about 15 minutes.

Is Route 2 over Petersburgh Pass twisty?

Yes. It has three twisty stretches on the climb and descent.

Where does the Taconic Trail go?

The Taconic Trail runs from Petersburgh into Massachusetts, where it becomes Route 2 into Williamstown.

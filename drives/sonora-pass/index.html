Sonora Pass: Highway 108

CA 108, Sonora Pass, Highway 108 · Stanislaus National Forest, California

Highway 108 from Strawberry over 9,624-foot Sonora Pass to US 395: forest and granite to Kennedy Meadows, then a narrow climb with grades up to 26% and tight hairpins, and a long descent past Leavitt Meadows. Closed by snow in winter.

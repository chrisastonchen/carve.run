North Santiam Highway

OR 22, North Santiam, Santiam Boulevard · Santiam Canyon, Oregon

OR-22 up the North Santiam canyon from Mill City, along the shore of Detroit Lake to Detroit and Idanha, then climbing through Marion Forks to Santiam Junction. Much of the canyon burned in 2020; the river, the lake and the long curves remain.

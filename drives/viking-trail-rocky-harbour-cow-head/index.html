Viking Trail: Rocky Harbour to Cow Head

Viking Trail, 430 · Gros Morne, Newfoundland and Labrador

Route 430 north from Rocky Harbour along the Gulf coast of Gros Morne National Park, between the sea and the Long Range Mountains, past Sally's Cove and the trail to Western Brook Pond's fjord, to Cow Head. Park pass needed to stop.

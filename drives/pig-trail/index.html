The Pig Trail

AR 23, Highway 23, Pig Trail · Ozark Mountains, Arkansas

AR-23 north from Ozark through the Ozark National Forest, across the Mulberry River and over the Boston Mountains to Huntsville and Eureka Springs. The southern miles run under a tunnel of trees; expect motorcycles and slow trucks on the grades.

The drive

The Pig Trail is Highway 23 north from Ozark, at I-40, up through the Ozark National Forest to Eureka Springs, 75.4 miles in all. The stretch between I-40 and Highway 16 at Brashears is the designated Pig Trail Scenic Byway, and Wikipedia credits that designation to its steep hills and hairpin turns.

After Brashears the road keeps going north through the Ozarks, and the drive ends in Eureka Springs, where Highway 23 meets US 62. The drive is rated Sweeping overall.

A note on the name: people often call all of Highway 23 the Pig Trail, but the byway designation covers the section between Interstate 40 and Brashears.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Pig Trail take to drive?

Highway 23 from Ozark to Eureka Springs is 75.4 miles and takes about 1 hr 45 min without stops.

Where does the Pig Trail start and end?

The Pig Trail Scenic Byway runs on Highway 23 between I-40 north of Ozark and Highway 16 at Brashears. This drive continues on to Eureka Springs.

Is the Pig Trail twisty?

The byway section is known for steep hills and hairpin turns. The full 75-mile drive is rated Sweeping overall.

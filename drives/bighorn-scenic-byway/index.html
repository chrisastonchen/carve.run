Bighorn Scenic Byway: US-14

US 14 · Bighorn Mountains, Wyoming

US-14 from Dayton over the Bighorn Mountains to Shell: switchbacks up the steep east face, Sibley Lake and Burgess Junction on top, over Granite Pass, then down Shell Canyon past Shell Falls between granite and limestone walls. Open all year.

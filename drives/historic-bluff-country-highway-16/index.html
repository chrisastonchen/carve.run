The drive

Highway 16 across Minnesota's southeast corner was designated a National Scenic Byway in 2003 as the Historic Bluff Country Scenic Byway. This drive covers 46.4 miles of it, from Preston, where it meets US 52, east through Rushford and Houston to Hokah, rated Leisurely.

The road passes through the Richard J. Dorer Memorial Hardwood State Forest. In Rushford Highway 16 runs with Highway 43, and at Houston it runs with Highway 76.

Highway 50: Austin to Eureka

US 50, Lincoln Highway, Main Street · Great Basin, Nevada

US-50, the Loneliest Road, from the old silver town of Austin over Austin, Bob Scott and Hickison summits to Eureka: long empty valleys broken by short mountain climbs, with petroglyphs at Hickison Summit and no services between the two towns.

Highway 86: Wilmington Notch

NY 86, State Highway 86 · Adirondacks, New York

NY-86 from Lake Placid down the West Branch of the Ausable River through Wilmington Notch, a narrow, cliff-walled pass known for its trout water, past High Falls Gorge and the Whiteface ski area to Wilmington. Curvy and fast in places.

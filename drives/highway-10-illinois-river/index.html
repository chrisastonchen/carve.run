Highway 10: Illinois River

OK 10, Illinois River, Illionis · Cherokee Hills, Oklahoma

OK-10, the Illinois River Scenic Drive, north from Tahlequah beside the Illinois River past float outfitters and wooded bluffs to Kansas, Oklahoma. Rolling curves; busy with float traffic on summer weekends, and it can close when the river floods.

Caesars Head: US 276

US 276, Geer Highway, Main Street · Blue Ridge Escarpment, South Carolina

US 276 from Travelers Rest across the foothills and up the Blue Ridge Escarpment past Caesars Head State Park: gentle valley miles, then a steep climb of tight switchbacks to the overlook at the top. Continues into North Carolina toward Brevard.

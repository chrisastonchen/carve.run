The drive

This drive heads south from Ligonier on PA 711, joins PA 381 at Normalville and carries on through Mill Run to Ohiopyle and Farmington, where PA 381 meets US 40. PA 381 passes just east of Fallingwater, the house designed by Frank Lloyd Wright. The Laurel Highlands Scenic Byway is listed as a tourist route on PA 381.

Fallingwater's visitor gate is on Mill Run Road in Mill Run. Tours are ticketed, the site is closed on Wednesdays and it is cashless.

In Ohiopyle the road becomes Main Street and crosses the Youghiogheny River. Ohiopyle State Park covers about 20,500 acres and calls itself the gateway to the Laurel Highlands. The Youghiogheny gorge offers some of the best whitewater boating in the eastern United States.

Good to know

  • Fallingwater tours are ticketed, and the site is closed on Wednesdays.
  • Cell service is unpredictable in and around many Pennsylvania state parks, including Ohiopyle; download the park map first.
  • Ohiopyle State Park is open sunrise to sunset.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check 511PA (PennDOT road conditions and closures).

Questions about this drive

How long does the Laurel Highlands drive from Ligonier to Ohiopyle take?

This drive from Ligonier to Farmington by way of Ohiopyle is 41 miles and takes about 1 hr 5 min.

Do I need tickets for Fallingwater?

Yes. Fallingwater tours are sold through its website and by phone, and the site is closed on Wednesdays.

Where does Laurel Highlands: Ligonier to Ohiopyle start and end?

This drive starts at Ligonier and ends at Farmington (US 40), 41.0 miles in all.

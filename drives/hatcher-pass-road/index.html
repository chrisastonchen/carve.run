Hatcher Pass Road

Palmer-Fishhook, Hatcher Pass, Fishhook, Willow · Talkeetna Mountains, Alaska

Palmer-Fishhook Road from the Glenn Highway across farmland and up the Little Susitna River canyon, then up switchbacks into alpine tundra to the Independence Mine turnoff. Paved to here; the road over the pass beyond is gravel and closed in winter.

The drive

State Road 66 follows the Ohio River through southern Indiana, and the long stretch from near Newburgh to SR 62 at Sulphur is designated the Ohio River Scenic Byway. This drive runs west from Leavenworth, picks up SR 66 near Sulphur, and goes through Hoosier National Forest to the river at Derby, then on to Cannelton.

Hoosier National Forest covers about 205,000 acres of rolling hills in south-central Indiana. Riders describe the middle of the Sulphur to Cannelton run as the most demanding, with sharp curves and steep changes in elevation, and the last third toward Cannelton as smoother, with long straights.

The drive is 47.4 miles and takes about 1 hr 15 min. It climbs 2,400 feet in all, with a high point of 800 feet.

Questions about this drive

How long is Highway 66 from Leavenworth to Cannelton?

Indiana 66 from Leavenworth to Cannelton is 47.4 miles and takes about 1 hr 15 min.

Is State Road 66 in Indiana a scenic byway?

Yes. The stretch from near Newburgh to SR 62 at Sulphur is part of the Ohio River Scenic Byway.

Where is the twistiest part of Highway 66?

Riders point to the middle of the Sulphur to Cannelton run, through Hoosier National Forest, for sharp curves and steep elevation changes.

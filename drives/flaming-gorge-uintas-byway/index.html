Flaming Gorge–Uintas Byway

US 191, UT 44, SR 44, Vernal Avenue, Flaming Gorge, Main Street · Uinta Mountains, Utah

US-191 north from Vernal up through the tilted rock layers of the eastern Uintas and over the forested crest to Greendale above Flaming Gorge, then UT-44 west along the high country past the Red Canyon and Sheep Creek turnoffs to Manila.

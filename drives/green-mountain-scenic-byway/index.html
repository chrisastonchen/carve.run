Green Mountain Scenic Byway

455, Montverde, Oakland, Sugarloaf, Ferndale, Old 50, Old State Road 50, 7th Street, 438 · Lake Apopka, Florida

Old 50 and CR 455 from Oakland north through Montverde and Ferndale and around Sugarloaf Mountain, 312 feet high: rolling hills and bends above Lake Apopka, among Florida's favorite cycling roads. Expect riders, especially on weekends.

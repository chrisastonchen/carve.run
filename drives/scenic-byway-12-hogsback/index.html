Highway 12: Escalante to Torrey

UT 12, SR 12, Scenic Byway 12, Journey Through Time, Main Street, 875 East · Grand Staircase–Escalante, Utah

Scenic Byway 12 from Escalante over slickrock canyons and along the Hogsback, a ridge with drops on both sides and no shoulders, to Boulder, then over Boulder Mountain's aspen forest at 9,600 feet to Torrey. Open all year; snow can close it.

Highway 73: Chapel Pond to Lake Placid

NY 73, State Highway 73, Cascade Road, Chapel Pond · Adirondacks, New York

NY-73 through the Adirondack High Peaks from the Northway: up past Chapel Pond under its cliffs, through Keene Valley and Keene, between the two Cascade Lakes and into Lake Placid past the Olympic ski jumps. Expect hikers on the shoulder at trailheads.

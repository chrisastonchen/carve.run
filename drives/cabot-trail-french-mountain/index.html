Cabot Trail: Chéticamp to Pleasant Bay

Cabot Trail, CT · Cape Breton Highlands, Nova Scotia

The Cabot Trail through Cape Breton Highlands National Park, from Chéticamp up the Gulf cliffs and over the plateau of French and MacKenzie Mountains, then down a run of switchbacks to Pleasant Bay. A park pass is needed to stop.

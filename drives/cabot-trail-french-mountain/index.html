The drive

The Cabot Trail runs through Cape Breton Highlands National Park, and about a third of the whole trail is inside it, with ocean and mountain views. This leg starts at Chéticamp, an Acadian fishing village on the Gulf of St. Lawrence, where the park has its western entrance and an information centre.

From Chéticamp the road goes north past French Mountain and MacKenzie Mountain to Pleasant Bay, where the Cabot Trail follows the Grande Anse River west to the coast.

The drive is 26.4 miles and takes about 50 minutes. It climbs roughly 3,100 feet to a high point of 1,500 feet, with two twisty stretches.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Cabot Trail from Chéticamp to Pleasant Bay take?

Chéticamp to Pleasant Bay is 26.4 miles and takes about 50 minutes without stops.

Does the Cabot Trail charge a fee?

Cape Breton Highlands National Park charges daily admission, with a per-adult and a family rate.

Is the Cabot Trail open year-round?

Cape Breton Highlands National Park is open year-round, though visitor services run only from mid-May to late October.

Hoodoo Trail: Highway 10

10, Hoo Doo, Hoodoo, South Railway · Drumheller Badlands, Alberta

Highway 10, the Hoodoo Trail, from Drumheller down the Red Deer River valley through the badlands: past Rosedale's suspension bridge and the hoodoos to East Coulee and the Atlas Coal Mine. Short and gentle, with the coulee walls close by.

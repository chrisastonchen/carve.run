Pierce Stocking Scenic Drive

Pierce Stocking Scenic Drive, Pierce Stocking · Sleeping Bear Dunes, Michigan

A 7.4-mile one-way loop over the dunes above Lake Michigan and Glen Lake: tight, hilly curves at a 20 mph limit, with overlooks high above the lake. Park entrance pass required, no trailers, open to cars late May to late October.

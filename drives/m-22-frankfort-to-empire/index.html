The drive

This is M-22 heading north from Frankfort toward Empire along the Lake Michigan shore. The highway passes through Elberta and Frankfort, then turns northeast to curve around the north shore of Crystal Lake. South of the Platte River it enters Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore. East of Platte Lake it turns north to Empire, the lakeshore's headquarters town. The drive is 21.5 miles and takes about 32 minutes.

It links the Frankfort to Onekama and Leland to Empire drives and runs through wooded stretches with beach access.

Chinook Pass: Highway 410

WA 410, Chinook Pass, Mather Memorial, SR 410, State Route 410, Highway 410 · Mount Rainier, Washington

Highway 410 from Greenwater up the White River into Mount Rainier National Park, over 5,430-foot Chinook Pass beside Tipsoo Lake, then down the American and Naches rivers to the US 12 junction near Naches. No park fee; closed in winter.

Avenue of the Giants

Avenue of the Giants, 254 · Humboldt Redwoods, California

The old US-101, now Highway 254, runs beside the freeway through the old-growth redwood groves of Humboldt Redwoods State Park, past Myers Flat, the park visitor center and Founders Grove. Slow and shaded; the trees are the reason to drive it.

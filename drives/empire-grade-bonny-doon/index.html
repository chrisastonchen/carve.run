Empire Grade to Bonny Doon

Empire Grade, Ice Cream Grade, Bonny Doon Road · Santa Cruz Mountains, California

Empire Grade climbs from Santa Cruz past UC Santa Cruz into the redwoods, then Ice Cream Grade crosses to Bonny Doon Road for a winding descent through Bonny Doon to Highway 1 near Davenport. Narrow in places; watch for cyclists.

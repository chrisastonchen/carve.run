FM 730 and FM 455: Decatur to Forestburg

FM 730, FM 455, Trinity Street, Farm-to-Market Road 730, Farm-to-Market Road 455 · Cross Timbers, Texas

FM 730 north from Decatur into the Cross Timbers hills, then FM 455 west to the crossroads of Forestburg: long sweepers, steady climbs and dips, and wide views over oak and pasture. Some crests are blind, with slow farm traffic beyond.

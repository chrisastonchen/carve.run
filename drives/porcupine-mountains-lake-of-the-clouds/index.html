M-64 and M-107: Lake of the Clouds

M 64, M 107, Lincoln Street, 107th Engineers, Lake of the Clouds · Porcupine Mountains, Michigan

M-64 west from Ontonagon along the Lake Superior shore to Silver City, then M-107 into Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park, ending below the Lake of the Clouds overlook. Gentle curves; the park needs a Michigan Recreation Passport.

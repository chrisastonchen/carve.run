Oregon Caves Highway

OR 46, Oregon Caves, Caves Highway · Siskiyou Mountains, Oregon

OR-46 from Cave Junction up the Sucker Creek and Cave Creek valleys, the last miles narrow, steep and winding, to Oregon Caves National Monument and its 1930s chateau. Large RVs and trailers aren't advised; snow and ice are possible in winter.

The drive

Farrington Highway (Route 93) follows the Waianae coast for 13.7 miles from Nanakuli through Makaha and Keaau to the road end at Yokohama Bay. The drive takes about 36 minutes, with just 177 feet of climb. It is a coastal two-lane with beach after beach.

The road ends at the edge of Kaena Point State Park, where the pavement stops at a gate and vehicles cannot go further. The shore at the end is lava rock.

Weekend beach crowds are common, and surf and rip-current warnings apply at the end.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Hawaii DOT Highways.

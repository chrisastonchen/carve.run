Logan Canyon

US 89, Logan Canyon · Bear River Range, Utah

US-89 from Logan up Logan Canyon between limestone cliffs, past Ricks Spring and Beaver Mountain to the 7,800-foot summit, then down the switchbacks to the turquoise water of Bear Lake at Garden City. Open all year.

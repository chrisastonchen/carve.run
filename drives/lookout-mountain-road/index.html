Lookout Mountain Road

Lookout Mountain · Golden, Colorado

The Lariat Loop's switchback road from Golden up Lookout Mountain to Buffalo Bill's grave: a string of hairpins with views over Golden and the plains. Closed to cars at night, from an hour after sunset to an hour before sunrise, and busy with cyclists.

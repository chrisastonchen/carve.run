The drive

Highway 3A starts at the Kootenay Bay ferry landing and follows the east shore of Kootenay Lake south to Creston. It is the road drivers from Nelson meet after the ferry crossing. The route runs through Crawford Bay, Gray Creek and Boswell, with the lake beside the road, then comes out into the Creston valley.

Crawford Bay is known for its artisans' studios and galleries. Near Boswell is the Glass House, a roadside attraction built from embalming bottles starting in 1952. The curves are sweeping and mostly gentle, and cyclists and motorcyclists favor the road.

The ferry from Balfour is not part of the drive. It is free, takes about 35 minutes, and runs year-round.

Good to know

  • The Balfour to Kootenay Bay ferry is free and crosses in about 35 minutes; check the schedule, since fewer sailings run outside summer.
  • Construction with short delays has been posted on weekdays; check DriveBC.
  • Expect cyclists and motorcycles on the shoreline curves.
  • The ferry runs on Pacific time, one hour earlier than Alberta and the East Kootenays.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check DriveBC.

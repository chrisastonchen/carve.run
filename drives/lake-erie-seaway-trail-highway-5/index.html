Highway 5: Dunkirk to Barcelona

NY 5, Lake Shore Drive, East Lake Road, West Lake Road · Lake Erie, New York

NY-5 west from Dunkirk along the Lake Erie shore, part of the Great Lakes Seaway Trail, past Lake Erie State Park and the vineyards of Chautauqua County's grape belt to the harbor at Barcelona. Flat and straight; the lake carries it.

The Twisted Sisters

RM 337, Ranch Road 337, East Fourth Street, TX 55, RM 335, TX 41, RM 336, Ranch Road 336 · Hill Country, Texas

The Three Sisters loop from Leakey: RM 337 west to Camp Wood, RM 335 north up the Nueces valley, TX-41 across the plateau and RM 336 back down to Leakey. Steep, tight ranch roads with cattle guards, livestock and deer, and little traffic.

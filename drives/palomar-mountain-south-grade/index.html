The drive

South Grade Road (S6) climbs Palomar Mountain from Highway 76 in a long series of hairpins to Mother's Kitchen, then Canfield Road runs through oak and conifer forest to Palomar Observatory. Busy with motorcycles and cyclists on weekends.

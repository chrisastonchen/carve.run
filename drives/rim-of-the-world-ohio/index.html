Rim of the World: Highway 78

78, SR 13, SR 555, SR 37, Burr Oak Boulevard · Wayne National Forest, Ohio

OH-78 east from Nelsonville through Murray City and Glouster, along the Rim of the World on the south side of Burr Oak State Park, and over the hills by Ringgold to Malta on the Muskingum River. Sharp curves and wooded ridges; an Ohio Scenic Byway.

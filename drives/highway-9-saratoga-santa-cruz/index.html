The drive

Highway 9 crosses the Santa Cruz Mountains from the South Bay to the sea. It leaves Saratoga Village on Big Basin Way, becomes Congress Springs Road, and climbs hard through tight bends to Saratoga Gap, about 2,600 feet up, where it crosses Skyline Boulevard (Highway 35) and enters Santa Cruz County. The climb from Saratoga is the twistiest part of the drive, and that side is a state scenic highway.

Over the top it drops into the San Lorenzo Valley, past Waterman Gap and the Highway 236 turns for Big Basin, and through a string of mountain towns: Boulder Creek, Brookdale, Ben Lomond and Felton. Below Felton the road runs through Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park, whose old-growth grove has trees close to 280 feet tall, and follows the San Lorenzo River down into Santa Cruz, ending at Highway 1 on River Street.

The drive crosses the Skyline Boulevard drive at Saratoga Gap without following it, so the two make an easy pair.

Good to know

  • The climb from Saratoga Village to Saratoga Gap is a favorite with cyclists, especially on weekend mornings.
  • Caltrans is replacing the Kings Creek and San Lorenzo River bridges north of Boulder Creek, with temporary signals running one-way traffic around the clock; expect a wait at each.
  • Henry Cowell Redwoods charges a day-use fee per vehicle if you stop at the park.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Caltrans District 5 news: Highway 9 work.

Questions about this drive

How long does Highway 9 from Saratoga to Santa Cruz take?

Highway 9 from Saratoga Village to Highway 1 in Santa Cruz is 33.4 miles and takes about 1 hour 15 minutes, plus waits at the bridge work near Boulder Creek.

Is Highway 9 twisty?

Yes. Highway 9 is twisty most of the way, and tightest on the climb from Saratoga to Saratoga Gap; the San Lorenzo Valley side is gentler but still winding.

Is there construction on Highway 9 in the Santa Cruz Mountains?

Yes. Caltrans has run 24-hour signals with one-way traffic at two bridge replacements north of Boulder Creek, and there has been lane work in Felton. Check Caltrans before you go.

Where does Highway 9 meet Skyline Boulevard?

Highway 9 meets Skyline Boulevard (Highway 35) at Saratoga Gap, the summit of the drive at about 2,600 feet, on the Santa Clara and Santa Cruz county line.

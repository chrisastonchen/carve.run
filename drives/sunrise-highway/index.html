Sunrise Highway (S1)

Sunrise, S1, S-1 · Laguna Mountains, California

Sunrise Highway (S1) runs along the eastern edge of the Laguna Mountains from I-8 through Mount Laguna to Highway 79, with pullouts looking down on the Anza-Borrego Desert. Parking along it needs an Adventure Pass; chains may be required after snow.

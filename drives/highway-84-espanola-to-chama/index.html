The drive

US-84 from Española up the Chama River valley past Abiquiú, the red and yellow cliffs of Ghost Ranch that Georgia O'Keeffe painted, and Echo Amphitheater, then over high mesas to Tierra Amarilla and Chama: desert gives way to pine and meadow.

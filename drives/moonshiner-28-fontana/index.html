The drive

NC 28 begins at Deals Gap, where it meets US 129 at the end of the Tail of the Dragon, the 11-mile stretch of US 129 on the Tennessee line. The Moonshiner 28 drive then runs east past Fontana Village and along Fontana Lake to US 19/74 at Almond. NC 28 skirts Great Smoky Mountains National Park along the way and was extended to Deals Gap in 1954.

Fontana Dam, operated by the Tennessee Valley Authority, is 480 feet tall, the tallest dam in the eastern United States, and the Appalachian Trail crosses its top. The drive is 31.3 miles and takes about 45 minutes, with twisty stretches.

The drive

NC 107 between Cashiers and Western Carolina University in Cullowhee is a winding mountain road of about 18 miles. The drive runs north from the Cashiers crossroads, past Lake Glenville, and ends at Speedwell Road on the south edge of Cullowhee, before the highway widens to four lanes.

Lake Glenville is described by Jackson County as one of the highest-elevation lakes east of the Mississippi, with 26 miles of shoreline. The drive is 20 miles, about 30 minutes, with two twisty stretches at the start and then a long descent into the Tuckasegee valley.

Questions about this drive

How long does NC 107 from Cashiers to Cullowhee take?

Cashiers to Cullowhee on NC 107 is 20 miles and takes about 30 minutes.

Is there gravel on NC 107?

Jackson County warns that many driveways along NC 107 are unpaved and gravel occasionally washes onto the road.

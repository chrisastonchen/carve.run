Highway 299: Blue Lake to Shasta

CA 299, 299, Trinity Highway, Main Street · Trinity River Canyon, California

Highway 299 crosses Berry Summit to Willow Creek, follows the Trinity River canyon past Burnt Ranch, Big Bar and Junction City to Weaverville, then climbs over Buckhorn Summit and past Whiskeytown Lake to Old Shasta. Long, with trucks and passing lanes.

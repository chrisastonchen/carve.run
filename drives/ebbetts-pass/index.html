Ebbetts Pass: Highway 4

CA 4, Highway 4, Ebbetts Pass, CA 89 · Alpine County, California

Highway 4 from Arnold past Lake Alpine and over 8,736-foot Ebbetts Pass to Markleeville. East of Lake Alpine it narrows to about a lane and a half with no center line, with steep hairpins on both sides of the pass. Closed by snow in winter.

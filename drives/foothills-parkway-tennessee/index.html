Foothills Parkway

Foothills Parkway · Great Smoky Mountains, Tennessee

The Foothills Parkway from Wears Valley to Chilhowee Lake: the newer section's bridges and ridge-top views of the Smokies down to Walland, then the older western run past Look Rock and down to US 129. Long, flowing bends with overlooks and few junctions.

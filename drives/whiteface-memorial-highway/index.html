Whiteface Memorial Highway

NY 431, Whiteface, Veterans Memorial · Adirondacks, New York

NY-431 from Wilmington up Whiteface Mountain: a toll road climbing about 8 miles of steady switchbacks, with the High Peaks in view, to a stone castle just below the summit. Open from about mid-May to mid-October.

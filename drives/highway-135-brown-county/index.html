The drive

State Road 135 runs south from Nashville through the hilly country of Brown County. For the first stretch it shares the road with SR 46, then SR 135 carries on alone toward Freetown. Brown County State Park, nicknamed the Little Smokies, sits near Nashville, and the park calls itself a traditional fall color hot spot.

Along the way the road passes Story, where the Story Inn has stood since 1851 at 6404 Indiana Highway 135. The inn describes Story as a corridor between Brown County State Park and Hoosier National Forest. Beyond Story the road is quiet and winding, and it ends at Freetown near the junction with SR 58.

The drive is 24.5 miles and takes about 45 minutes. It has one twisty stretch, roughly miles 10 to 12, and the rest is sweeping.

Good to know

  • Fall color is a draw in Brown County, so expect more visitors around Nashville in autumn.
  • Allow the full 45 minutes: the road runs through hilly country with bends all the way.
  • Brown County State Park has a horse camp entrance on SR 135 South.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check INDOT TrafficWise (511IN).

Is Indiana Highway 135 in Brown County twisty?

Mostly sweeping, with one twisty stretch around miles 10 to 12. The hills keep it winding, and the Brown County hills are the reason to drive it.

When is the best time to drive Highway 135 in Brown County?

Fall color is the busy season, and Brown County State Park calls itself a traditional fall color hot spot. Expect more traffic near Nashville in October.

What is the Story Inn on Highway 135?

It is an inn at 6404 Indiana Highway 135 in Story, with rooms, cottages and a restaurant. The inn says it has been there since 1851.

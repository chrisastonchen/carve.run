Crater Lake Rim Drive

Rim Drive, Munson · Crater Lake National Park, Oregon

The road around the rim of Crater Lake, clockwise from Rim Village past the Watchman, Cleetwood Cove and the Phantom Ship to park headquarters, with overlooks every mile or two. Park entrance fee; open about July to October, with east rim work zones.

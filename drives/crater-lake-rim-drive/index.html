The drive

Rim Drive circles Crater Lake. This drive starts at Rim Village and runs clockwise, past the Watchman, Cleetwood Cove and the Phantom Ship Overlook, to park headquarters. It's 30.5 miles in about 1 hr 5 min without stopping, with the lake on the driver's side and overlooks every mile or two.

The climb is 3,900 feet in total and the high point is about 7,700 feet. Curves are wide and sweeping.

Good to know

  • There's a park entrance fee, valid for seven days.
  • West and East Rim Drive close to cars each year around November 1, or earlier if snow is significant.
  • East Rim Drive is under rehabilitation, so expect traffic control near work zones. Pinnacles Road is open only to hikers and bikers while a construction project continues.
  • Full opening in a normal year is mid-July, depending on snowpack.

Closed by snow from November 1 or earlier until July. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check National Park Service: Crater Lake road conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does Crater Lake Rim Drive take?

Rim Drive from Rim Village to park headquarters is 30.5 miles and takes about 1 hr 5 min without stops.

When is Rim Drive open?

Usually from about mid-July to the end of October. It closes to cars for winter on November 1 or earlier if snow is heavy.

Is there a fee for Crater Lake Rim Drive?

Yes. Crater Lake National Park charges an entrance fee for private vehicles, valid for seven days.

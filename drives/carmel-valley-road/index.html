The drive

Carmel Valley Road is the long way inland from the Monterey coast. It leaves Highway 1 at Carmel and follows the Carmel River up its valley, wide and easy at first, through the vineyards and ranches of Carmel Valley and into Carmel Valley Village.

East of the village the road changes. It becomes narrow and winding as it climbs over the hills, with two twisty stretches, and it is remote enough that you should start with fuel. The drive tops out near 2,400 feet, then drops to the Salinas Valley, where the road runs on as West Carmel Valley Road, County Route G16 and Elm Avenue and ends at 14th Street in Greenfield.

It is driven inland from Carmel, the way visitors on the coast use it. The whole drive is 50.6 miles and takes about 1 hr 30 min.

Good to know

  • Monterey County scheduled weekday resurfacing with one-lane traffic control on Carmel Valley Road between Carmel Rancho Boulevard and Via Petra through October 2026, so expect delays near Carmel.
  • East of Carmel Valley Village the road is narrow and winding, and the stretch between the village and the Salinas Valley is remote.
  • Fill the tank in Carmel or the village; the hills and the Salinas Valley end of the route are sparsely served.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Monterey County Public Works: road closures.

Questions about this drive

How long does Carmel Valley Road to Greenfield take?

Carmel Valley Road from Carmel to Greenfield is 50.6 miles and takes about 1 hr 30 min without stops.

How twisty is Carmel Valley Road?

Carmel Valley Road is mostly moderate, with two twisty stretches east of Carmel Valley Village where it narrows and winds over the hills.

Is there construction on Carmel Valley Road?

Monterey County scheduled resurfacing with one-lane control on weekdays between Carmel Rancho Boulevard and Via Petra through October 2026. Check the county's road closures page before you go.

Where does Carmel Valley Road start and end?

This drive starts at Highway 1 in Carmel, passes through Carmel Valley Village and ends at 14th Street in Greenfield in the Salinas Valley.

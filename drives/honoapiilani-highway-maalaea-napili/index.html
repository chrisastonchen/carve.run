The drive

Highway 30 from Māʻalaea along the foot of the West Maui Mountains: the Pali stretch above the sea, Olowalu, then past Lahaina and the Kāʻanapali coast to Nāpili. Two lanes with passing sections and long ocean views; busy at commute times.

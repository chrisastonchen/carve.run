Gates Pass Road

Gates Pass, Kinney, Speedway · Tucson Mountains, Arizona

Gates Pass Road climbs over the Tucson Mountains on steep, tight bends to a saddle looking out over the saguaro forest, then drops to Kinney Road past the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum into Saguaro National Park West. Not advised for vehicles over 25 feet.

Highway 128: Colorado River Byway

UT 128, SR 128, River Road, Dinosaur Diamond · Moab, Utah

UT-128 along the Colorado River from Moab, through a red-walled canyon past Castle Valley and the Fisher Towers to Dewey Bridge, then out across open desert beneath the Book Cliffs to the Cisco turnoff. Narrow shoulders; many cyclists.

Route 450: Bay of Islands

450, Main Road · Bay of Islands, Newfoundland and Labrador

Route 450 west from Corner Brook along the south shore of the Humber Arm to the Bay of Islands, through a string of coves and fishing towns under steep wooded hills, to Lark Harbour by Blow Me Down Provincial Park.

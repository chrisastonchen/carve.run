The drive

Nebraska Highway 97 runs south from Valentine through the Sandhills, with sand hills around the reservoir and very few towns along the way. It ends at Mullen, where it meets Highway 2.

About 23 miles south of Valentine is the turn for Snake River Falls. A short hike across private land leads to an overlook above the falls, which carry more water than any other waterfall in Nebraska. Merritt Reservoir lies about 26 miles southwest of Valentine. It covers nearly 3,000 acres, surrounded by sand hills, and has white sand beaches where camping and fishing are allowed.

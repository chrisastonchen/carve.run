Mount Magazine Scenic Byway

AR 309, Elm Street · Arkansas River Valley, Arkansas

AR-309 from Paris over Mount Magazine, Arkansas's highest point, to Havana: a climb through the Ozark National Forest to the state park's lodge and overlooks on the summit plateau, then a steep, curving descent to the Petit Jean valley.

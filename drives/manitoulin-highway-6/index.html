Highway 6: Espanola to South Baymouth

Highway 6, (6), Meredith Street · Manitoulin Island, Ontario

Highway 6 from Espanola over the La Cloche hills to the Little Current swing bridge, then down Manitoulin Island past Manitowaning to South Baymouth. The one-lane bridge swings open for boat traffic in summer, so expect a wait.

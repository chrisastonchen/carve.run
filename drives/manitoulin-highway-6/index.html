The drive

Highway 6 runs from Espanola over the La Cloche hills to the Little Current swing bridge, then down Manitoulin Island past Manitowaning to South Baymouth. It's 67.5 miles and about 1 hr 20 min.

The bridge at Little Current was built in 1913 and is the island's only year-round road link. It carries Highway 6 on a single lane. The drive ends on Highway 6 before the South Baymouth ferry dock.

Good to know

  • The swing bridge is one lane and opens for boats, so expect waits.
  • The province has chosen a builder for a two-lane replacement just west of the current bridge, with no construction start announced.
  • Check Ontario 511 for closures.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Ontario 511.

Questions about this drive

Is the Little Current swing bridge one lane?

Yes. It's a single-lane bridge built in 1913 and the only year-round road link to Manitoulin Island. A two-lane replacement is planned.

How long does Highway 6 from Espanola to South Baymouth take?

It's 67.5 miles and takes about 1 hr 20 min.

Does this drive include the ferry?

No. It ends on Highway 6 before the South Baymouth ferry dock.

The drive

OK-49 west from Medicine Park into the Wichita Mountains Wildlife Refuge, then the Mount Scott road, which winds about 3 miles up the granite peak to the summit. Narrow with tight bends; bison and longhorns roam the refuge. Car hours are posted at the base.

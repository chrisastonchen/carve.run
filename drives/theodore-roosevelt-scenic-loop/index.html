Theodore Roosevelt Scenic Loop Drive

Scenic Loop Drive, East River Road · North Dakota Badlands, North Dakota

The South Unit's loop road from Medora through the Little Missouri badlands: Wind Canyon above the river, prairie dog towns, bison and wild horses, and the overlooks at Scoria Point. Park entrance fee.

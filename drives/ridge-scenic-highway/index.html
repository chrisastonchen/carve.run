The drive

The Ridge Scenic Highway is SR 17 along the Lake Wales Ridge, central Florida's old sand ridge. This drive starts in Haines City and runs south for 31.7 miles through Dundee, Lake Wales, Babson Park and Frostproof, ending at US 27 south of Frostproof.

It passes lakes, citrus groves and small towns. Bok Tower Gardens is a short detour from Lake Wales.

The road is slow through the town centers and gently rolling between them.

Questions about this drive

How long is the Ridge Scenic Highway drive?

SR 17 from Haines City to Frostproof is 31.7 miles and takes about 55 minutes.

Which towns are on the Ridge Scenic Highway?

Haines City, Dundee, Lake Wales, Babson Park and Frostproof.

Is the Ridge Scenic Highway twisty?

No. It is Leisurely, with mostly gentle curves.

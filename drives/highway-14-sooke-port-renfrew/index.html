The drive

West Coast Road from Sooke to Port Renfrew: a narrow, twisting, hilly highway along the Strait of Juan de Fuca past French Beach, Jordan River, China Beach and Sombrio, with views across to the Olympic Mountains. Some bridges are single lane.

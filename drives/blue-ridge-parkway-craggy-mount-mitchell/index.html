Blue Ridge Parkway: Mount Mitchell

Blue Ridge Parkway, NC 128 · Black Mountains, North Carolina

The Blue Ridge Parkway north from Asheville past Craggy Gardens, then NC-128 to just below the summit of Mount Mitchell, the highest peak east of the Mississippi: tunnels, high meadows and long views. No gas on the way; ice often closes it in winter.

Highway 30: Long Lake to Tupper Lake

NY 30, Adirondack Trail, Lake Street, Wawbeek Avenue · Adirondacks, New York

NY-30 north from Blue Mountain Lake, with NY-28N to the hamlet of Long Lake, then on its own through the forest of the central Adirondacks to the village of Tupper Lake. Long, easy curves with few towns.

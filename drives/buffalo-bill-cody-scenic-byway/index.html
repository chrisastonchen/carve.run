Buffalo Bill Cody Scenic Byway

US 14, US 16, US 20, Yellowstone, North Fork, US-14, US-20 · Wapiti Valley, Wyoming

US-14/16/20 west from Cody toward Yellowstone's East Entrance: through the tunnels past Buffalo Bill Dam and its reservoir, then up the Wapiti Valley beside the North Fork of the Shoshone, under eroded volcanic cliffs and hoodoos. Grizzly country.

M-28: Marquette to Munising

M 28, M-28 · Lake Superior shore, Michigan

M-28 east from Harvey, at Marquette's edge, along Lake Superior to Munising, with long stretches right beside the beach between Harvey and Au Train and Grand Island offshore near the end. Mostly straight; the shoreline carries it.

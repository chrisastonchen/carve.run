The drive

This drive starts at Muscoda and follows Wisconsin 133 along the Wisconsin River to Boscobel. Wisconsin 133 runs along the river between Mount Hope and Boscobel, and shares US 61 at Boscobel. From there the route continues on US 61 and Wisconsin 60 through Wauzeka, where WIS 60 runs along the north side of the Wisconsin River, to Prairie du Chien and the Mississippi. It ends on the Great River Road. The drive is 44.0 miles and takes about 55 minutes.

The drive

Skate Creek Road, Forest Road 52, links Ashford and Packwood in about 23 miles through the Gifford Pinchot National Forest along Skate Creek and the Cowlitz River. It is a shortcut between the Nisqually side of Mount Rainier and the Stevens Canyon side, and it is mostly sweeping with two twistier stretches.

The road is paved but has had little maintenance for several years, so expect potholes, frost heaves and slumped sections. Summer and fall are the best seasons.

The drive is 21.1 miles and takes about 45 minutes.

Good to know

  • The Forest Service gates the road when snow gets too deep, often from late fall into spring.
  • Watch for potholes, frost heaves and slumps, and keep your speed down.
  • Traffic is medium to heavy, busiest in summer.

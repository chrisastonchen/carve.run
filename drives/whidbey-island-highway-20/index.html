Whidbey Island: Highway 20

WA 20, SR 20, State Route 20, Highway 20, Deception Pass Bridge · Whidbey Island, Washington

Highway 20 crosses the 1935 Deception Pass Bridge, 180 feet above the tidal channel, then runs south down Whidbey Island through forest, Oak Harbor and open farmland to Coupeville on Penn Cove. Busy through Oak Harbor.

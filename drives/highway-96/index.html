Highway 96: Willow Creek to Somes Bar

CA 96, 96, Bigfoot Scenic Byway · Klamath River, California

Highway 96 follows the Trinity River north through the Hoopa Valley, then turns up the Klamath River at Weitchpec, cut into steep forested canyon walls above the water, past Orleans to Somes Bar at the mouth of the Salmon River. Few services.

The drive

NC 63 is a two-lane mountain highway that Wikipedia calls aggressively twisty in its mountain sections. The drive runs from Leicester, through open farm valley, past Canto along Little Sandymush Creek, over Doggett Gap, the highway's high point, and down about 1,500 feet to Trust.

The descent has two twisty stretches, around miles 5 to 7 and 11 to 16. The road ends at NC 209 in Trust, beside Friezeland Creek, so it pairs with NC 209. It's 18.6 miles and about 35 minutes.

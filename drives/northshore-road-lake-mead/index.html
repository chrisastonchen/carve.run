Northshore Road

Northshore, North Shore, NV 167, SR 167 · Lake Mead, Nevada

Northshore Road through Lake Mead National Recreation Area, from the Lake Mead Parkway north toward Overton: long sweeping curves over desert washes and red sandstone, with views down to the lake and no services until Overton. National park entry fee.

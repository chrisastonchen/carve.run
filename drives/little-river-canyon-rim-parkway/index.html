Little River Canyon Rim Parkway

AL 176, Little River Canyon, Canyon Rim, Rim Parkway, State Route 176 · Lookout Mountain, Alabama

AL 176, the Little River Canyon Rim Parkway, from Little River Falls south along the canyon's rim: a winding two-lane through pine and oak with a string of overlooks into one of the deepest canyons east of the Mississippi, including Wolf Creek.

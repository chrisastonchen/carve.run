The drive

Route 381 runs from Baie-Saint-Paul on the St. Lawrence to La Baie on the Saguenay, crossing the highlands between. Québec City's tourism office lists the Grands-Jardins National Park section as a ride of incredible views, mountains and endless curves, and calls it a route worth sharing with friends.

It's driven north from the Route 138 junction at Baie-Saint-Paul, so the climb out of Charlevoix comes first and the descent to the Saguenay comes last. The drive is 69.4 miles and takes about 1 hr 30 min, and the curves are rated mostly gentle.

Good to know

  • There are no gas stations in Grands-Jardins National Park, so fill up before you go.
  • Québec City's tourism office suggests returning by Route 170, through Saint-Siméon and La Malbaie.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Québec 511.

Questions about this drive

How long does Route 381 from Baie-Saint-Paul to La Baie take?

Route 381 is 69.4 miles and takes about 1 hr 30 min without stops.

Is there gas on Route 381 through Grands-Jardins?

No. Québec City's tourism office warns riders that there are no gas stations in Grands-Jardins National Park, so fuel up before the climb.

Which direction is Route 381 driven here?

This drive goes north from Baie-Saint-Paul, at the Route 138 junction, to La Baie on the Saguenay.

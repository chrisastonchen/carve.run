Route 109: Lake Winnipesaukee

NH 109, Governor Wentworth, Main Street, North Main, Governor John Wentworth · Lakes Region, New Hampshire

NH-109, the Governor John Wentworth Highway, down the east side of Lake Winnipesaukee from Moultonborough through Melvin Village and Tuftonboro, past Mirror Lake to Wolfeboro. Rolling two-lane road with glimpses of the lake.

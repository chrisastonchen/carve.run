Indian River Drive

Indian River Drive, 707 · Treasure Coast, Florida

CR 707 south from Fort Pierce along the Indian River Lagoon to Jensen Beach: a narrow two-lane at the water's edge under oaks and palms, past riverfront homes and docks. Low speed limits and frequent cyclists.

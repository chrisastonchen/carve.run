Natchez Trace Parkway: Franklin to Hohenwald

Natchez Trace · Highland Rim, Tennessee

The Natchez Trace Parkway south from Highway 96 near Franklin, past Leipers Fork, Jackson Falls and Baker Bluff to the Meriwether Lewis site: a 50 mph parkway with no commercial traffic or billboards, through rolling woods and farms.

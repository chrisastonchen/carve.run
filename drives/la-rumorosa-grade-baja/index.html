The drive

This is the free road up from Tecate to La Rumorosa, 55 km (34 mi) on Mexico 2 in about 45 minutes. It climbs about 3,600 feet through the craggy, granite-boulder country of the Sierra de Juárez to the town at roughly 1,280 m (4,200 ft), and the curves are mostly gentle.

The toll road, 2D, runs alongside it, and Mexico 2 is the free option, with several lookouts on the way up. Wind blows hard across the summit area, which has encouraged wind-power development nearby.

The drive ends at La Rumorosa. The famous descent to the desert on the far side is not part of it.

Good to know

  • Strong wind is common near the top, and the grades are long, so use a lower gear on the way down.
  • Snow and cold can close both the free road and the toll road in winter storms, and they have closed it before. Check before leaving.
  • The 2D toll road beside it is operated by CAPUFE and charges at the El Hongo booth. This drive is on the free road, so there's no toll.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check SICT (Mexico's federal roads agency).

Questions about this drive

How long is the drive from Tecate to La Rumorosa on Mexico 2?

Mexico 2 from Tecate to La Rumorosa is 55 km (34 mi) and takes about 45 minutes without stops.

Is there a toll on Mexico 2 between Tecate and La Rumorosa?

No. Mexico 2 is the free road. The parallel Highway 2D is the toll road, collected at the El Hongo booth.

Does snow close the road to La Rumorosa?

It can. Winter snow and cold have closed both the free Mexico 2 and the 2D toll road between Tecate and La Rumorosa, usually during winter storms.

How high is La Rumorosa?

La Rumorosa sits at about 1,278 m (4,193 ft) in the Sierra de Juárez, and the drive from Tecate climbs about 3,600 feet to get there.

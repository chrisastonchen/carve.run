FM 390: La Bahia Road

FM 390, La Bahia, Farm-to-Market Road 390 · Washington County, Texas

FM 390 on the route of the old La Bahia Road, north from TX-105 through William Penn to Independence, home to the ruins of the first Baylor University, then west to Gay Hill: rolling pasture, white fences and, in late March and April, bluebonnets.

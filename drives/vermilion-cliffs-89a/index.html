Vermilion Cliffs: Highway 89A

89A, 89a, Bitter Springs · Marble Canyon, Arizona

US-89A runs from Bitter Springs below the Echo Cliffs to Navajo Bridge over the Colorado River, then west along the foot of the Vermilion Cliffs and up onto the Kaibab Plateau in a climb of bends to Jacob Lake. Long desert miles, few services.

The drive

The Oatman Highway is a 42-mile BLM-designated Route 66 back country byway. This drive runs from Kingman across Sitgreaves Pass, with hairpin curves and steep grades, past Cool Springs Station to Oatman.

Oatman has an Old West look and its burros. Posted speeds range from 20 to 45 mph.

Good to know

  • Vehicles over Sitgreaves Pass are limited to 40 feet in length.
  • An automatic transmission is recommended for the grades.
  • There is no water or toilets on the byway, and fuel is only in Kingman, Golden Valley, Bullhead City and Fort Mohave.
  • Watch for flash floods and extreme heat.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check BLM: Route 66 Historic Back Country Byway.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Oatman Highway take?

From Kingman to Oatman it is 24.8 miles and takes about 50 minutes.

Can RVs drive the Oatman Highway?

Vehicles over Sitgreaves Pass are limited to 40 feet, and wider vehicles should take care.

Is the Oatman Highway twisty?

Yes, it is rated Twisty, with hairpins at Sitgreaves Pass.

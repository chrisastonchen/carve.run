Kolob Canyons Road

Kolob Canyons · Zion National Park, Utah

Kolob Canyons Road climbs five miles from I-15 into Zion's northwest corner, winding up Taylor Creek beneath the red Finger Canyons to Lee Pass and the viewpoint at the end. A short, steep park road; park entrance fee.

The drive

Kolob Canyons Road climbs five miles from I-15 exit 40 into the northwest corner of Zion National Park. It winds up Taylor Creek beneath the red Finger Canyons to Lee Pass and ends at the Timber Creek Overlook. It is 5.3 miles and about 10 minutes, twisty and steep.

It is a short park road, and you drive back down the same way.

Zion's entrance fee applies.

Good to know

  • Zion's pass covers the entrance fee, paid at the Kolob Canyons Visitor Center.
  • The Park Service says the road is open most of the year but closes frequently in winter.
  • Check the park's alerts for closures after snowstorms.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check National Park Service: Zion road conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long is Kolob Canyons Road?

From I-15 exit 40 to Timber Creek Overlook is 5.3 miles and takes about 10 minutes.

Is Kolob Canyons Road open in winter?

It is open most of the year but closes frequently in winter, according to the Park Service.

Is there a fee for Kolob Canyons Road?

Yes, the Zion entrance fee applies, paid at the Kolob Canyons Visitor Center.

The drive

This one is about the scenery more than the corners. US 321 and Highway 67 leave Hampton and follow the shore of Watauga Lake, with open views across the water to the ridges of the Cherokee National Forest, then climb up the valley past Butler to Mountain City on long, flowing bends.

It's driven from Hampton, the end nearer Elizabethton and Johnson City, toward Mountain City, and it pairs with The Snake from there if you want something twistier. Where US 321 splits off near Butler, the drive stays on Highway 67.

When to go

Open all year, weather permitting.

Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs. Before you set off, check TDOT SmartWay.

Good to know

  • Every road in the area closed by Hurricane Helene has reopened, including US 321 in Carter and Johnson counties.
  • The lake has several overlooks and pull-offs along the shore; they're the best places to stop.
  • It's gentle by Tennessee mountain standards: good for a relaxed drive or a first trip with passengers.

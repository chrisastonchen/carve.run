Cedar Breaks: Highways 143 and 148

UT 143, UT 148, SR 143, SR 148, State Route 148, Center Street, Brian Head, Cedar Breaks · Markagunt Plateau, Utah

UT-143 from Parowan up Parowan Canyon to Brian Head, then UT-148 along the rim of Cedar Breaks' 2,000-foot amphitheater at over 10,000 feet, down to Highway 14. Closed by snow from about mid-November to late May; monument fee to stop.

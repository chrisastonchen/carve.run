Mustang and Padre Islands

TX 361, Park Road 22, PR 22, South Padre Island Drive, State Highway 361 · Mustang and Padre Islands, Texas

TX-361 south from Port Aransas down Mustang Island past the dunes of Mustang Island State Park, then Park Road 22 into Padre Island National Seashore to Malaquite Beach. Flat and straight, the Gulf just over the dunes. The national seashore charges an entrance fee.

The drive

Topanga Canyon Boulevard is State Route 27 through the Santa Monica Mountains. This drive starts at Ventura Boulevard in Woodland Hills and climbs over the divide, then drops down Topanga Canyon past Topanga village to Pacific Coast Highway. It is 11.9 miles and takes about 25 minutes.

The road has tight bends and is shaded by oaks and sycamores in the canyon. You descend about as much as you climb, and the canyon walls close in as you near the ocean. The drive ends on the boulevard a short way before PCH.

It is a commuter road as well as a pleasure drive, so it fills at commute times.

Good to know

  • Traffic is heavy at commute times, in both directions.
  • The canyon has had landslides and closures in past years, so check Caltrans before you go.
  • The road is narrow with tight bends, and cyclists use it.
  • It ends at Pacific Coast Highway, so a left or right turn there takes you up or down the coast.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Caltrans: State Route 27 road conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does Topanga Canyon Boulevard take to drive?

Topanga Canyon Boulevard from Woodland Hills to Pacific Coast Highway is 11.9 miles and takes about 25 minutes, longer at commute times.

Is Topanga Canyon Boulevard twisty?

Yes. It has tight bends through the canyon with shaded oaks and sycamores, and it climbs about 1,100 feet over the route.

Is Topanga Canyon Boulevard the same as SR 27?

Yes. Topanga Canyon Boulevard is California State Route 27.

Has Topanga Canyon Boulevard closed before?

Yes. Caltrans has closed it for landslides and repairs in past years, so check Caltrans for current status.

Mount Washington Auto Road

Mount Washington Auto, Auto Road · White Mountains, New Hampshire

A private toll road climbing 7.6 miles from Pinkham Notch to the 6,288-foot summit of Mount Washington at an average 12 percent grade, without guardrails on long stretches above treeline. Vehicle size limits apply; closes for winter.

Mount Seymour Road

Mount Seymour Road, Mt Seymour Road · North Shore Mountains, British Columbia

The climb up Mount Seymour Road to the ski area: tight hairpins through forest in Mount Seymour Provincial Park, with lookouts over Indian Arm, Deep Cove and Vancouver on the way up. A designated winter tire and chain-up route from October 1.

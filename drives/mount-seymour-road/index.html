The drive

Mount Seymour Road climbs from just above Mount Seymour Parkway to the ski area, 6.9 miles through tight hairpins in forest within Mount Seymour Provincial Park. It gains about 2,800 feet in 20 minutes, with lookouts toward Indian Arm, Deep Cove and Vancouver on the way up.

It is a dead end, so you come back down the same way.

Good to know

  • Winter tires are required to access Mount Seymour from October 1.
  • A free day-use parking pass may be required in winter.
  • The road has hairpins; allow extra time in rain or snow.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check BC Parks: Mount Seymour Park.

Questions about this drive

How long does Mount Seymour Road take to drive?

Mount Seymour Road is 6.9 miles and takes about 20 minutes to the ski area.

Do I need winter tires on Mount Seymour Road?

Yes. Winter tires are required to access Mount Seymour starting October 1.

Does Mount Seymour Road have viewpoints?

Yes. Viewpoints along the road look out over Indian Arm and Vancouver.

Highway 536: Hannibal to Woodsfield

536, 78, Eastern Avenue · Monroe County hills, Ohio

OH-536 from the Ohio River at Hannibal, climbing out of town on a run of hairpins and then along ridgetops through the Monroe County hills, joining OH-78 for the last miles west to Woodsfield. Tight, steep and very curvy; take it slowly.

Scenic Mountain Drive, Big Spring

Scenic Drive, Park Road 8, PR 8 · Big Spring, Texas

The paved loop road in Big Spring State Park, winding up and around Scenic Mountain with views over the town and the plains. One-way, narrow and shared with walkers and cyclists. Free, open 8 a.m. to sunset.

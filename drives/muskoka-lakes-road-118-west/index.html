The drive

Muskoka Road 118 West runs from Bracebridge to Port Carling, then Muskoka Road 169 carries on to Bala. It's 25 miles and about 35 minutes. This section is no longer a provincial highway and is also known as the Frank Miller Memorial Scenic Route.

It's cottage-country driving: curves through rock and pine between the Muskoka lakes, crossing the lift bridge at Port Carling.

Good to know

  • Port Carling's lift bridge is occasionally cut to a single lane for inspections, with short delays.
  • Some stretches have paved shoulders and some have little or none, so expect cyclists.
  • Check the township's closures page before going.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Township of Muskoka Lakes: Roads and Service Closures.

Questions about this drive

How long does Muskoka Road 118 West take?

Bracebridge to Bala is 25.3 miles and takes about 35 minutes.

What is the Port Carling lift bridge?

It's the bridge on Muskoka Road 118 at Port Carling. It is occasionally reduced to one lane for inspection.

Is Muskoka Road 118 West a provincial highway?

No. West of Bracebridge it's a District of Muskoka road, also called the Frank Miller Memorial Scenic Route.

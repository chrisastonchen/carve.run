Highway 197: Clarkesville to Lake Burton

GA 197, State Route 197, Washington Street · Northeast Georgia Mountains, Georgia

GA 197 north from Clarkesville up the Soque River valley past Mark of the Potter, an old gristmill turned pottery shop, and Batesville, then along the west shore of Lake Burton to US 76: a quiet, winding two-lane through farms and forest.

The drive

NM 68 is known as the River Road to Taos because it follows the Rio Grande. This drive begins at Velarde and runs north through Embudo and Pilar to Ranchos de Taos, where NM 68 meets the north end of NM 518.

Between Velarde and Taos it is a two-lane highway with very few passing lanes, so passing is rare. The full highway is 45.5 miles, from Española to US 64 in Taos.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Low Road to Taos take?

The Low Road (NM 68) from Velarde to Ranchos de Taos is 28 miles and takes about 30 min.

Why is NM 68 called the Low Road?

NM 68 is called the River Road to Taos because it follows the Rio Grande, and it is two lanes with very few passing lanes between Velarde and Taos.

Where does Low Road to Taos start and end?

This drive starts at Velarde and ends at Ranchos de Taos, 28.0 miles in all.

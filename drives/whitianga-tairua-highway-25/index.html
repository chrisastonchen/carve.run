The drive

State Highway 25 is the sealed link between the two big east-coast towns of the Coromandel, Whitianga and Tairua, and this drive takes it south: 39 km (24 mi) in about 45 minutes. The road leaves Whitianga on the bypass, passes through Coroglen and Whenuakite, and winds over the hills into Tairua.

The curves are mostly moderate with two twisty stretches, through bush and farmland across the middle of the peninsula. From Tairua a side road leads to Pauanui.

It is the quick way between the two towns. The shortcut from Coromandel town to Whitianga is a different road and is not part of this drive.

Good to know

  • The road is narrow in places, and it is busy with holidaymakers in summer, especially around Christmas and New Year.
  • Storms have closed or restricted this highway before, so check NZTA's journey planner in wet weather.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NZTA Journey Planner.

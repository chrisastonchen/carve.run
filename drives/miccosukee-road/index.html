Miccosukee Road

Miccosukee, 146, 0347 · Red Hills, Florida

Miccosukee Road northeast from Tallahassee's Capital Circle to the village of Miccosukee: one of the city's nine official canopy roads, with nearly nine miles under unbroken live oaks hung with Spanish moss. Two lanes, close to the trees.

The drive

Mount Buller Road climbs 15 km (9 mi) from Mirimbah up the mountain toward Mount Buller village, rising about 4,100 feet in hairpins and climbing curves, with mountain views opening near the top. The village sits at about 1,600 m and the summit is 1,805 m. Wikipedia gives the Mirimbah-to-village climb as 15.3 km at an average grade of 6.2 percent, with the final kilometre the steepest, above 10 percent in places.

Mirimbah is where the resort entry gate stands, at the base of the mountain. In the declared snow season, every vehicle entering Mount Buller or Mount Stirling pays a resort entry fee that supports road clearing and parking. There is a free, unmonitored carpark at the base of the mountain just before the gates.

The drive is a dead end at the resort, so you return the same way.

Good to know

  • All vehicles must carry diamond-pattern wheel chains during the declared snow season, and staff may direct you to fit them on the drive up.
  • Trailers and caravans can't be towed in when chains are required.
  • Resort entry fees apply per vehicle in the snow season; the resort recommends pre-purchasing entry and parking, and the free carpark before the gates is first come, first served.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Mt Buller road alerts.

Questions about this drive

How long does Mount Buller Road take?

Mount Buller Road from Mirimbah is 15 km (9 mi) and takes about 25 minutes without stops.

Do you need snow chains for Mount Buller?

Yes, all vehicles must carry diamond-pattern wheel chains during the declared snow season, and staff may direct you to fit them on the drive up.

Is there a fee to drive up Mount Buller?

Yes, during the declared snow season every vehicle entering Mount Buller pays a resort entry fee at the Mirimbah gate. Check the resort's website for the current price.

Can you tow a caravan up Mount Buller Road?

Not when chains are required: the resort says trailers and caravans cannot be towed in then.

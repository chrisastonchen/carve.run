Highway 244: Keystone to Hill City

SD 244, Highway 244, Highway 16A, US 16A · Black Hills, South Dakota

The road from Keystone up past the Mount Rushmore entrance and on as SD-244 below Black Elk Peak to US-16 near Hill City: steep climbs, tight curves, Horse Thief Lake, and a side view of Washington's profile.

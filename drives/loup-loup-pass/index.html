Loup Loup Pass: Highway 20

SR 20, Highway 20, 2nd Avenue South · Okanogan Highlands, Washington

SR 20 from the Methow Valley near Twisp over Loup Loup Pass (4,020 ft) to Okanogan: a steady climb through pine and fir past the Loup Loup ski area, then a long, curving descent to the Okanogan Valley. Open all year.

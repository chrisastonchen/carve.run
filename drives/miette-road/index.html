The drive

Miette Road from Highway 16 at Pocahontas to Miette Hot Springs: 17 km of tight, climbing curves up the Fiddle River valley to the hottest springs in the Canadian Rockies. Closed to cars from mid-October to mid-May. Parks Canada pass required.

Good to know

Gates close to cars when the hot springs close in mid-October and reopen in mid-May. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check the state or county's road conditions.

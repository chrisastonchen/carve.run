The drive

Miette Road climbs from Highway 16 at Pocahontas up the Fiddle River valley to Miette Hot Springs. Parks Canada describes it as narrow and winding, and the route has three twisty stretches in its 9.2 miles. The road ends at the hot springs.

Miette Hot Springs is the reason for the road. It is open daily in season and closed in the off-season.

Good to know

  • Trailers and large motorhomes can't go past Miette Campground; trailers can be left at the Lower Mine Trail parking lot.
  • A park pass is required, either a day pass or a Discovery Pass.

Gates close to cars when the hot springs close in mid-October and reopen in mid-May. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Parks Canada: Jasper bulletins.

Questions about this drive

Is Miette Road open in winter?

Miette Hot Springs, at the end of Miette Road, is open seasonally. Check Parks Canada's Jasper bulletins for the road's status before you go.

Can I take a trailer or motorhome up Miette Road?

Trailers and large motorhomes aren't allowed past Miette Campground. Trailers can be dropped at the Lower Mine Trail parking lot.

How long does Miette Road take?

Miette Road from Highway 16 at Pocahontas to Miette Hot Springs is 9.2 miles and takes about 20 minutes.

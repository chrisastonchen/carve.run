Kamehameha Highway: Kahaluʻu to Haleʻiwa

Kamehameha, HI 83 · Oʻahu, Hawaii

Kamehameha Highway along Oʻahu's windward coast past Kualoa and Lāʻie, then around to the North Shore's Sunset Beach and Haleʻiwa: beaches, fishponds and steep green ridges on a mostly two-lane road. Slow in surf season and at weekends.

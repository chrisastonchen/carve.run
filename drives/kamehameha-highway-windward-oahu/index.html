The drive

Kamehameha Highway, Route 83, is named for King Kamehameha I and informally called Kam Highway. From Kahaluʻu it follows the windward coast, then passes through Lāʻie and on to the North Shore, where it runs by Sunset Beach and through Haleʻiwa.

A bypass, the Joseph P. Leong Highway, has carried Route 83 traffic around Haleʻiwa since 1995. This drive goes into the town itself.

Good to know

  • Check HDOT's roadwork page for closures on Highway 83 before you go.
  • The drive runs through Haleʻiwa town rather than on the Leong Highway bypass.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Hawaiʻi DOT Highways: roadwork.

Cypress Hills: Highway 271

271, 724, Fort Walsh · Cypress Hills, Saskatchewan

Highway 271 from Maple Creek across the prairie and up into the West Block of the Cypress Hills, climbing switchbacks through lodgepole pine to Fort Walsh National Historic Site. Paved two-lane all the way; the road ends at the fort.

The drive

US 64 between Highlands and Rosman is part of the Waterfall Byway, a state scenic byway named for the roughly 200 waterfalls along its route. The 98-mile byway passes Cullasaja Gorge, Highlands, Cashiers and Lake Toxaway, and this drive covers the stretch from Highlands to Rosman, near Brevard.

It starts on 4th Street in Highlands, then runs through the Cashiers Crossroads and down toward Lake Toxaway. South of the highway between Highlands and Cashiers is Whiteside Mountain, a Forest Service recreation area with a day-use fee. The drive is 29.5 miles and takes about 55 minutes, with three twisty stretches.

Good to know

  • Whiteside Mountain's parking area charges a day-use fee; the Nantahala Ranger District can give the current amount.
  • The Waterfall Byway's NC 215 junction is just outside Rosman.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NCDOT DriveNC: traffic and road conditions.

Wolf Creek Pass: US-160

US 160 · San Juan Mountains, Colorado

US-160 from Pagosa Springs past Treasure Falls and up the switchbacks over 10,857-foot Wolf Creek Pass on the Continental Divide, then down the Rio Grande to South Fork. Steep grades, a tunnel and big views; plowed year-round.

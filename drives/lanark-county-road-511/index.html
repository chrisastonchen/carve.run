County Road 511: Lanark to Calabogie

511, Lanark Road, George Street · Lanark Highlands, Ontario

County Road 511 from Lanark village north through the Lanark Highlands to Calabogie: farms give way to forest, lakes and rock as the road climbs and bends toward the Madawaska. Light traffic, and it links with Calabogie Road.

Cottonwood Pass

Cottonwood, County Road 306, Main Street, County Road 209, Taylor, County Road 742, CR 306, CR 209, CR 742 · Sawatch Range, Colorado

The county road from Buena Vista over 12,126-foot Cottonwood Pass on the Continental Divide to Taylor Park, then down Taylor Canyon to Almont. Paved both sides, with switchbacks above the trees on the west. Closed from about November to late May.

The drive

This drive starts at Bridge Road in Hobe Sound and runs south down Jupiter Island on Beach Road, then follows US 1 over Jupiter Inlet and A1A south along the dunes to Juno Beach Pier. Sea grape lines the quiet beach roads, with ocean glimpses between the houses.

Blowing Rocks Preserve sits on Jupiter Island. Its Anastasia limestone shoreline is the largest on the Atlantic coast, and in rough seas at high tide the waves push water up through the rocks, sending spray as high as 50 feet.

Good to know

  • Jupiter Island allows no street parking. Blowing Rocks has its own lot, open 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
  • Hurricane damage has closed the preserve before; call 561-744-6668 or check its page before planning a stop.
  • The best surf at Blowing Rocks is on rough days at high tide, usually in winter.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check The Nature Conservancy: Blowing Rocks Preserve.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Jupiter Island drive take?

Beach Road and A1A from Hobe Sound to Juno Beach is 12.9 miles and takes about 35 min.

Is there a fee for Blowing Rocks Preserve?

Parking is free and admission has no set charge; the preserve asks for an optional $2 per person donation. It has closed after hurricane damage, so check before going.

Can you park on Jupiter Island?

No street parking is allowed on Jupiter Island. Blowing Rocks has its own lot, which can fill by late morning on weekends.

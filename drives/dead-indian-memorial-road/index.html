Dead Indian Memorial Road

Dead Indian Memorial · Southern Cascades, Oregon

Jackson County's road from Ashland, climbing curves out of the Rogue Valley and then across the high Cascade plateau past ranches, meadows and small lakes to OR-140 near Lake of the Woods, with Mount McLoughlin ahead. Expect snow on top in winter.

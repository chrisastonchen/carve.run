The drive

US 26 runs east from Prineville past Ochoco Reservoir and over the pines of Ochoco Pass, then down to Mitchell. West of Mitchell a short paved side road leads to the Painted Hills of the John Day Fossil Beds.

East of Mitchell the highway follows the John Day River and passes through the basalt walls of Picture Gorge, finishing in Dayville. It's 82 miles and about 1 hr 40 min, rated Leisurely with few bends. This section is part of the Journey Through Time Scenic Byway, and Mitchell has fuel, food and lodging.

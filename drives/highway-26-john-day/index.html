Highway 26: Prineville to Dayville

US 26, Ochoco, John Day, 3rd Street · John Day Country, Oregon

US-26 east from Prineville past Ochoco Reservoir and over the pines of Ochoco Pass, down to Mitchell near the Painted Hills, then along the John Day River and through the basalt walls of Picture Gorge to Dayville. Part of the Journey Through Time byway.

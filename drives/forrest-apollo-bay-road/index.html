The drive

This drive runs 32 km (20 mi) from Forrest, a village of about 250 people in the Otways hinterland, down to Skenes Creek on the coast, just north of Apollo Bay. It descends from the plateau through tall mountain ash forest in tight, shaded bends, with a high point of about 1,800 feet.

Forrest sits about 30 minutes from Colac and 40 minutes from Apollo Bay. It is known for nearly 100 km of mountain bike trails, a brewery and a general store famous for its pies, and it is a base for Great Otway National Park. Stevensons Falls is about a 20-minute drive away.

The drive ends where the road meets the Great Ocean Road at Skenes Creek, so it connects the Otway forest with the coast route to Lorne and Port Campbell.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Forrest to Apollo Bay road take?

The drive from Forrest to Skenes Creek, at the Great Ocean Road near Apollo Bay, is 32 km (20 mi) and takes about 35 minutes.

Is the Forrest to Apollo Bay road twisty?

Yes. It is twisty most of the way, with tight, shaded bends through tall forest as it drops about 1,800 feet toward the coast.

What is there to do in Forrest?

Forrest is known for mountain biking, with almost 100 km of trails, as well as Lake Elizabeth, a local brewery and a general store with homemade pies.

Does the Forrest to Apollo Bay road close?

Fires and storms can close roads and parks in the Otways. Check VicTraffic and the Parks Victoria change of conditions page before you go.

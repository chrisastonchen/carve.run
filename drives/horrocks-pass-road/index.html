The drive

Horrocks Pass Road crosses the southern Flinders Ranges between Wilmington and the Augusta Highway. The drive starts in Wilmington and runs 20 km (13 mi) west, climbing to the pass and descending the far side in a run of bends. The pass lies about 6 km west of Wilmington.

The pass is named for John Horrocks, who crossed it in August 1846 on an exploration north of Spencer Gulf. A lookout and a monument to Horrocks and his party stand at the top, and the monument was dedicated on 21 September 1946. The drive finishes just past the junction with the Augusta Highway (A1).

Good to know

  • There is a lookout and monument at the summit if you want to stop.
  • The pass is about 6 km west of Wilmington, so the climb begins soon after the town.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Traffic SA.

Questions about this drive

How long does Horrocks Pass Road take?

Horrocks Pass Road from Wilmington to the Augusta Highway is 20 km (13 mi) and takes about 15 minutes.

Who is Horrocks Pass named for?

It is named for John Horrocks, who passed through in August 1846 on an exploration north of Spencer Gulf. A monument to him stands at the top.

Is there a lookout on Horrocks Pass Road?

Yes. A lookout and monument to Horrocks and his party stand at the top of the pass.

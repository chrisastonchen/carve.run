Caliente–Bodfish Road

Caliente Bodfish Road, Caliente-Bodfish Road · Walker Basin, California

A Kern County road from Caliente up the steep Lion's Trail grade, across Walker Basin and through Havilah, the county's first seat, then over a pass and down to Bodfish near Lake Isabella. Narrow two lanes with sharp curves.

US-62/180: Guadalupe Mountains

US 62, US 180 · Guadalupe Mountains, Texas

US-62/180 east across the salt flats toward the cliff of El Capitan and Guadalupe Peak, the highest point in Texas, past Pine Springs in Guadalupe Mountains National Park, then over Guadalupe Pass and down to the New Mexico line.

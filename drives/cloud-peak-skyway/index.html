The drive

US-16 from Buffalo over the southern Bighorns: up through forest to 9,666-foot Powder River Pass, past Meadowlark Lake, then down Ten Sleep Canyon between tall limestone walls to the town of Ten Sleep. Open all year; rockfall advisory.

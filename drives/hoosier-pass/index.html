Hoosier Pass: Highway 9

CO 9, Main Street · Mosquito Range, Colorado

CO-9 from Breckenridge over 11,542-foot Hoosier Pass on the Continental Divide to Alma and Fairplay: switchbacks up the north side, then a run down past fourteeners into South Park. Plowed and open year-round.

The drive

The Desert Road is the stretch of State Highway 1 between Tūrangi and Waiouru, and it crosses some of the emptiest country in the North Island. South of Tūrangi the highway climbs onto the Rangipo Desert, a high, open plain beside Tongariro National Park, with Ruapehu, Ngāuruhoe and Tongariro in view.

This drive runs south, the way most traffic from Taupō and Auckland goes. The road tops out at about 3,500 feet and then runs down through an army training area to reach Waiouru. It is a trunk road, not a motorway: two lanes, long straights, and open sky.

The same height and exposure that give it the views also make it one of the first state highways to close when winter weather turns. Snow, ice and fog shut it often and briefly, sometimes overnight, and traffic is sent around the park by other roads until it clears.

Good to know

  • Snow, ice and fog close the Desert Road often in winter, usually for hours rather than days; there is no fixed closure season, so check NZTA before you leave Tūrangi.
  • When it shuts, the western bypass runs around Tongariro National Park on SH46 and SH47 and adds a good deal of time.
  • The road passes through army training land, so stay on the highway and keep to the posted limits.
  • Expect cold and wind at the top even on a clear day.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NZTA Journeys: traffic and road conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Desert Road take to drive?

The Desert Road from Tūrangi to Waiouru is 57 km (35 mi) and takes about 45 minutes in normal conditions.

Why does the Desert Road close?

State Highway 1 across the Desert Road is high and exposed, so snow, ice and fog close it in winter, usually briefly. NZTA posts closures and detours on its Journeys site.

What is the alternative when the Desert Road is closed?

A western route around Tongariro National Park on SH46 and SH47 has been used as a bypass when the Desert Road is shut. Check NZTA for the detour in force on the day.

What can you see from the Desert Road?

The road runs through the Rangipo Desert beside Tongariro National Park, with the volcanoes Ruapehu, Ngāuruhoe and Tongariro in view on a clear day.

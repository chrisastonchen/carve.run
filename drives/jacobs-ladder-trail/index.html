The drive

Jacob's Ladder is the name of the scenic byway on US 20 between Westfield and Lee, about 30 miles in all. This drive covers the climb from Huntington, at the edge of the Pioneer Valley, west through Chester and Becket and down into Lee.

US 20 clips the southern edge of Huntington, then passes through Chester. At Becket it shares a short stretch of road with Route 8 through the town center before Route 8 splits off, and then it runs beside the Massachusetts Turnpike toward Lee.

The drive is 23.8 miles and takes about 35 minutes. The road climbs 1,900 feet and tops out at 1,800 feet, with sweeping curves and one twisty stretch around miles 7 to 9.

Jacob's Ladder Trail

US 20, Jacob, Russell Road, Laurel Road, Route 20 · Berkshires, Massachusetts

US-20 from Huntington up the Westfield River valley through Chester, then over the Berkshire hills past Becket and down to Lee: the Jacob's Ladder Trail, with long climbs and sweeping curves through hill-town forest.

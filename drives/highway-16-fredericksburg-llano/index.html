The drive

Highway 16 runs north from Fredericksburg through granite hills to the Llano River at Llano, 37 miles of rolling ranch country with a few climbs and moderate bends.

Along the way, RR 965 leaves for Enchanted Rock State Natural Area, built around a 425-foot pink granite dome, and RR 1323 leads to Willow City and its Loop, known for spring wildflowers. The Loop is on county roads through private land, so the courtesy is to drive it without stopping. Both are separate trips from Highway 16 itself.

The drive ends in Llano. One rider's account suggests it as the stop for food and gas before the ride back.

Good to know

  • Enchanted Rock has a daily entry fee, and reserving passes ahead is advised in busy season, September through May.
  • The Willow City Loop is county road through private land; don't stop or leave the road.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check TxDOT DriveTexas.

Questions about this drive

How long does Highway 16 from Fredericksburg to Llano take?

Highway 16 from Fredericksburg to Llano is 37 miles and takes about 40 minutes.

How do I get to Enchanted Rock from Highway 16?

Enchanted Rock State Natural Area is on RR 965, which leaves Highway 16 north of Fredericksburg.

When is the best time to drive Highway 16 for wildflowers?

Spring is the best time, especially around the Willow City Loop, which is reached from Highway 16 by RR 1323.

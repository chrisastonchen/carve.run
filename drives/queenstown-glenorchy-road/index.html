The drive

The road from Queenstown to Glenorchy runs 44 km (27 mi) along the north-western shore of Lake Wakatipu. Glenorchy is about 45 km by road from Queenstown, at the head of the lake where the Dart and Rees rivers flow in. The drive starts on Lake Esplanade and follows the shoreline under steep mountains, past Bob's Cove and Bennetts Bluff.

The road is a narrow, curving shoreline route with limited passing, so it is slow. Curves are mostly gentle with two twisty stretches, and the drive takes about 1 hr. Glenorchy is the end of the drive; the area around it was used for filming The Fellowship of the Ring.

Heavy rain can bring slips, and the road has been closed that way before.

Questions about this drive

How long does Queenstown to Glenorchy take?

Queenstown to Glenorchy is 44 km (27 mi) and takes about 1 hr, longer if you stop at bays and viewpoints.

Is the Queenstown to Glenorchy road narrow?

Yes. The Glenorchy road is narrow and curving along Lake Wakatipu, with limited passing.

Does the Glenorchy road close?

It can, after heavy rain brings slips. Check QLDC before you go.

What is in Glenorchy?

Glenorchy is at the head of Lake Wakatipu, where the Dart and Rees rivers enter the lake, and nearby Paradise was used for filming The Fellowship of the Ring.

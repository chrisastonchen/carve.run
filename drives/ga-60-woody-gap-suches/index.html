Highway 60: Dahlonega to Morganton

GA 60, State Route 60, Morganton Highway · Chattahoochee National Forest, Georgia

GA 60 from Stone Pile Gap north of Dahlonega over Woody Gap to Suches, then on through the national forest to Morganton: a twisting climb to the Appalachian Trail crossing, a high mountain valley, and a long, winding descent along creeks.

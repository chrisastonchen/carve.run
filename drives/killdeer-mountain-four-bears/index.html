Killdeer Mountain Four Bears Byway

Veterans Memorial Highway, ND 22, ND 23, State Highway 23 · Little Missouri Badlands, North Dakota

ND-22 north from Killdeer, dropping into the Little Missouri badlands at Lost Bridge, then ND-23 east across Lake Sakakawea on the Four Bears Bridge to New Town. Few bends, big views; expect oilfield trucks.

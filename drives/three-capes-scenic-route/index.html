Three Capes Scenic Route

Bayocean, Cape Meares, Bayshore, Oceanside, Netarts, Whiskey Creek, Cape Lookout, Sandlake, Sand Lake, Galloway, Cape Kiwanda, Three Capes, OR 131, Pacific, Tillamook Avenue · Tillamook Coast, Oregon

The road from Tillamook along the bay to Cape Meares, over the cape to Oceanside and Netarts, up across Cape Lookout and past the Sand Lake dunes to Pacific City and Cape Kiwanda. Narrow and slow in places, with 12% grades on the new Cape Meares section.

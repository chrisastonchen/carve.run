The drive

RM 337 is the eastern Sister, the long ranch road that links Medina on Highway 16 to Leakey in the Frio Canyon. Leaving Medina, it follows the West Prong of the Medina River for about 12 miles through ranch land, then climbs over the ridges toward Vanderpool, where it shares about a mile with RM 187 near Lost Maples.

West of Vanderpool comes the stretch the road is known for: steep climbs and descents with hairpin turns, big drop-offs and low guardrails, past craggy hillsides with mountain laurel, before it drops into Leakey at US 83. Leakey, the Real County seat, is where weekend riders stop for the Three Sisters, and RM 337 carries on west from town as the first leg of the Twisted Sisters loop.

Good to know

  • A sign near Medina warns of the twisty miles ahead and the motorcyclists killed on them; take the curves at the posted speeds.
  • The steepest, tightest part is between Vanderpool and Leakey, with drop-offs and low guardrails.
  • Watch for deer and loose livestock, and expect motorcycles in numbers on weekends.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check TxDOT DriveTexas: road conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does RM 337 from Medina to Leakey take?

RM 337 from Medina to Leakey is 36.2 miles and takes about 45 minutes without stops.

Is RM 337 one of the Twisted Sisters?

Yes: RM 337, RM 336 and RM 335 make up the Twisted Sisters, and RM 337 from Medina to Leakey is the eastern part of it.

What's the twistiest part of RM 337?

The stretch of RM 337 between Vanderpool and Leakey has the steepest grades and tightest hairpins, with drop-offs and low guardrails.

Is RM 337 dangerous?

RM 337 has sharp curves and drop-offs, and a sign near Medina warns of past motorcycle deaths, so ride or drive it at the posted speeds and watch for animals.

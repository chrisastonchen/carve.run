Independence Pass: Highway 82

CO 82, Independence, Original Street · Sawatch Range, Colorado

CO-82 from US-24 past Twin Lakes over 12,095-foot Independence Pass on the Continental Divide and down the Roaring Fork to Aspen. Narrow in places on the Aspen side, with vehicles over 35 feet banned; closed from about November to late May.

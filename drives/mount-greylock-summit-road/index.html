Mount Greylock

Rockwell, Summit Road, Greylock · Berkshires, Massachusetts

Rockwell Road from the Lanesborough visitor center up to the summit of Mount Greylock, the highest point in Massachusetts: tight curves through hardwood forest to the War Memorial Tower. Open mid-May to late October; no vehicles over 22 feet.

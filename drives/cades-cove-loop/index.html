The drive

Cades Cove Loop Road is a one-way road around a broad valley in the Great Smoky Mountains. The park describes an 11-mile loop with three churches and historic buildings, including Cable Mill, and a self-guiding tour booklet covers the stops. The route here is 9.5 miles, from just past the loop entrance to the end of the loop.

The road is rated Intense, with three twisty stretches and about 800 feet of climb. The park says to allow at least two to four hours, and the drive time shown here, 50 minutes, assumes no stops.

Good to know

  • The loop is open sunrise to sunset and is typically closed to vehicles on Wednesdays from May through September.
  • Traffic is heavy in summer and fall and on weekends year-round; use the paved pullouts to stop.
  • Parking longer than 15 minutes needs a parking tag. It is not an entrance fee.
  • Keep your distance from wildlife; the park notes even turkeys have attacked people.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Great Smoky Mountains National Park: closures.

Do you need a pass for Cades Cove Loop Road?

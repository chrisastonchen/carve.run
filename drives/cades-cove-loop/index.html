Cades Cove Loop Road

Cades Cove · Great Smoky Mountains, Tennessee

The one-way Cades Cove Loop Road around a broad mountain valley: open fields, old churches, cabins and the Cable Mill, with deer and black bears often near the road. Open sunrise to sunset; closed to cars on Wednesdays from about May to September.

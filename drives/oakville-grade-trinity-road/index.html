Oakville Grade and Trinity Road

Oakville Grade, Trinity · Mayacamas Mountains, California

Oakville Grade climbs steeply from the Napa Valley floor into the Mayacamas Mountains, with views back over the vineyards, then Trinity Road twists down through oak woods to Highway 12 near Glen Ellen. Sharp, narrow bends; big rigs over 38 feet are banned.

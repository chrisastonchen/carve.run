Sheridan Lake Road

Sheridan Lake Road · Black Hills, South Dakota

Sheridan Lake Road from the edge of Rapid City into the hills: a winding two-lane through ranch valleys and pine forest, ending at US-385 just north of Sheridan Lake. A local favorite way into the Black Hills.

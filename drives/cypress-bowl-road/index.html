The drive

Cypress Bowl Road climbs from the Upper Levels Highway in West Vancouver to Cypress Mountain, 7 miles of wide switchbacks gaining about 2,100 feet. It is a dead end at the ski area, so the drive stops on the road short of the parking lots.

Partway up is the Highview Lookout, over Vancouver, the Lions Gate Bridge and, on clear days, Mount Baker. The climb takes about 20 minutes and is sweeping rather than tight.

Good to know

  • Winter tires are required to reach Cypress Mountain from October 1.
  • There is no fee to drive the road itself.
  • The road is a dead end, so you return the way you came.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check DriveBC.

Questions about this drive

How long does Cypress Bowl Road take to drive?

Cypress Bowl Road from Highway 1 to Cypress Mountain is 7 miles and takes about 20 minutes.

Do I need winter tires on Cypress Bowl Road?

Yes. Winter tires are required to access Cypress Mountain starting October 1.

Where does Cypress Bowl Road start?

It leaves Highway 1, the Upper Levels Highway, in West Vancouver and climbs to Cypress Mountain.

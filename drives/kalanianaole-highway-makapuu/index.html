Kalanianaʻole Highway: Makapuʻu

Kalanianaole, Kalanianaʻole, Kalaniana’ole, HI 72 · East Oʻahu, Hawaii

Kalanianaʻole Highway around Oʻahu's eastern tip, from Hawaiʻi Kai past Hanauma Bay, the Hālona Blowhole and Sandy Beach, up over Makapuʻu Point and down along the windward cliffs to Waimānalo. Short and busy; the lookout lots fill early.

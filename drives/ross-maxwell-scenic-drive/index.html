Ross Maxwell Scenic Drive

Ross Maxwell Scenic Drive · Big Bend National Park, Texas

The park road from Santa Elena Junction down the west side of the Chisos, past Burro Mesa, Sotol Vista, the Mule Ears and Tuff Canyon, to the Rio Grande at Castolon. Park entrance fee; the last stretch on to Santa Elena Canyon is closed for now.

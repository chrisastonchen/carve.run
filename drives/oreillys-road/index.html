The drive

Lamington National Park Road, usually called O'Reilly's Road, climbs from Canungra onto the Lamington Plateau and ends at Green Mountains, where O'Reilly's Rainforest Retreat sits in the rainforest. Queensland Parks puts the distance to Green Mountains at 36 km; the drive ends just short of the retreat, 30 km (19 mi) in, and takes about 50 minutes.

The road is sealed but narrow, with frequent hairpin bends and blind corners all the way up from Canungra into the rainforest. It is a dead end, so you return by the same road.

Queensland Parks does not recommend the road for anyone towing a caravan or camper trailer, or driving a recreational vehicle longer than 4 m.

Good to know

  • Caravans, large camper trailers and RVs over 4 m are not suitable: the road is narrow with hairpins and blind corners.
  • The nearest fuel is at Canungra, at the bottom of the climb.
  • There is one road in and out, so allow for the same drive back down.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Queensland Parks: Lamington National Park, visiting safely.

Questions about this drive

How long does O'Reilly's Road take to drive?

Canungra to O'Reilly's on Lamington National Park Road is 30 km (19 mi) and takes about 50 minutes.

Is the road to O'Reilly's sealed?

Yes. Lamington National Park Road from Canungra to Green Mountains is sealed, but narrow, with frequent hairpins and blind corners.

Can I take a caravan to O'Reilly's?

Queensland Parks says the road is not suitable for caravans, large camper trailers or RVs longer than 4 m.

Is there fuel on the way to O'Reilly's?

No. The nearest fuel is at Canungra, before the climb.

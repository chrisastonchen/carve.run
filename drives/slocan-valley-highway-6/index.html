Highway 6: Slocan Valley

BC 6, Vernon-Slocan, Highway 6 · Slocan Valley, British Columbia

Highway 6 north from South Slocan up the Slocan River valley past Winlaw and Slocan, then along the east shore of Slocan Lake through Silverton toward New Denver, facing the peaks of Valhalla Provincial Park across the water.

The drive

Oregon 214 climbs from Silverton into Silver Falls State Park, home to the Trail of Ten Falls. The drive runs from Silverton to the south end of the park, passing the North Falls trailhead before the turn to South Falls, a trip of about 16 miles.

The road is a gentle country two-lane that ends at the day-use area near South Falls. The park's best-known hike is a roughly 8-mile loop past ten waterfalls, with a shorter option to South Falls alone.

Good to know

  • Silver Falls charges a day-use parking fee; a state parks pass also works.
  • The park draws many visitors in summer, so allow extra time for parking.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check ODOT TripCheck.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Silver Falls Highway take?

Highway 214 from Silverton to Silver Falls State Park is 15.5 miles and takes about 25 minutes.

Is there a fee for Silver Falls State Park?

Yes, the park charges a day-use parking fee, and an Oregon State Parks pass also covers it.

How long is the Trail of Ten Falls?

Guides give it as a loop of about 8 miles, with a shorter walk to South Falls alone.

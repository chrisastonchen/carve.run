Neels Gap: US 129

US 129, US 19, Gainesville Highway, Blairsville Highway · Blue Ridge Mountains, Georgia

US 19 and US 129 from Turners Corner up to Neels Gap and down to Vogel State Park: a two-lane mountain highway of steady curves through national forest, with the Appalachian Trail passing through the Mountain Crossings store at the gap. Busy on fall weekends.

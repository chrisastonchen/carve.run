Old Philadelphia Pike: Amish Country

PA 340, Old Philadelphia · Lancaster County, Pennsylvania

PA 340, the Old Philadelphia Pike, from Bird-in-Hand through Intercourse to White Horse: Amish farmland, farm stands and village shops. Slow going: you share the road with horse-drawn buggies, and tour traffic is heavy in season.

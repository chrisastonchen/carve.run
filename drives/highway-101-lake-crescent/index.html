Highway 101: Lake Crescent

US 101, Olympic Highway, Forks Avenue · Olympic Peninsula, Washington

US 101 west from Port Angeles winds for about 10 miles along the south shore of Lake Crescent, a deep, clear lake in Olympic National Park, then follows the Sol Duc River through forest to Forks. Expect logging trucks.

River Road Scenic Byway

River Road, River Rd · Au Sable River, Michigan

River Road National Scenic Byway west from Oscoda along the high banks of the Au Sable River through Huron National Forest, past the Lumberman's Monument. Gentle bends through pine and hardwood; the river views are from the overlooks.

Silver Thread: Highway 149

CO 149, Gunnison, Silver Thread, Main Street, 7th Street · San Juan Mountains, Colorado

CO-149 from South Fork up the Rio Grande to Creede, over Spring Creek Pass on the Continental Divide and 11,530-foot Slumgullion Pass, then down past Lake San Cristobal to Lake City. Big San Juan views and few towns; steep, tight descent into Lake City.

The drive

NM 434 connects Angel Fire, on US 64, with Mora, on NM 518. This drive runs it south, through Black Lake, where NM 120 meets the road, and on through Guadalupita to Mora.

Coyote Creek State Park sits on NM 434 just north of Guadalupita. The route's high point is about 8,800 feet, and its twistiest stretch comes around miles 13 to 16.

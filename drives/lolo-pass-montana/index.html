Lolo Pass: US-12

US 12 · Bitterroot Range, Montana

US-12 from Lolo up Lolo Creek past Lolo Hot Springs to Lolo Pass on the Idaho line, on the Lewis and Clark route: an easy valley start, then forest curves and a steeper climb through the Bitterroot Range to the 5,233-foot pass.

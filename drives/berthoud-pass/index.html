Berthoud Pass: US-40

US 40, Berthoud, Park Avenue · Front Range, Colorado

US-40 from Empire over 11,307-foot Berthoud Pass on the Continental Divide to Winter Park: tight switchbacks on the climb from Clear Creek, a summit above the trees, and a long descent into the Fraser valley. Plowed and open all winter.

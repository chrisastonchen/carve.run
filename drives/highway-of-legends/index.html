Highway of Legends

CO 12, Highway of Legends, University Street, Main Street · Spanish Peaks, Colorado

CO-12 from Trinidad up the Purgatoire valley past the Stonewall rock fin, over 9,941-foot Cuchara Pass and down past the Spanish Peaks and their stone dikes to La Veta. Quiet two-lane road through old coal towns, lakes and aspen.

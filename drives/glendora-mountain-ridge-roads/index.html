Glendora Mountain and Ridge Roads

Glendora Mountain, Glendora Ridge · San Gabriel Mountains, California

Glendora Mountain Road switchbacks up from Glendora, then narrow Glendora Ridge Road follows the crest east to Mt. Baldy Village, with views over the San Gabriel Valley. Popular with cyclists; the county closes both on holiday weekends and red-flag days.

Blue Ridge Parkway: Doughton Park

Blue Ridge Parkway · Blue Ridge, North Carolina

The Blue Ridge Parkway from US-21 at Roaring Gap through Doughton Park to NC-16: open ridge-top meadows, split-rail fences and long views east over the Piedmont, with Brinegar Cabin and the Bluffs on the way. Gentle curves and light traffic.

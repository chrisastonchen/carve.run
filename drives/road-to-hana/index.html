Road to Hāna

Hana Highway, Hāna Highway, HI 36, HI 360, HI-360 · East Maui, Hawaii

The Hāna Highway from Paʻia along East Maui's rain-forest coast to Hāna: hundreds of curves and dozens of one-lane bridges, with waterfalls and the Keʻanae Peninsula along the way. Slow going; yield at the bridges and park only where it's legal.

The Triple Nickel: Highway 555

555, SR 37, SR 78, SR 377, Center Road, Marion Street · Southeast Ohio hills, Ohio

OH-555 south from Stovertown, just outside Zanesville, to Chesterhill: a roller-coaster of blind crests, dips and tight, often unmarked bends over the farm ridges of Perry and Morgan counties. Pavement is patched in places; popular with riders.

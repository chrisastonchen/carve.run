The drive

OR 82 is the Wallowa Lake Highway. This drive goes east from Elgin, over the hill and down to the Minam and Wallowa rivers, through the canyon to Wallowa and Lostine, then up the Wallowa Valley through Enterprise and Joseph to the foot of Wallowa Lake, with the Wallowa Mountains ahead.

It's 55.3 miles and about 1 hr 25 min, rated Leisurely. The highway runs between the Blue and Wallowa mountains through ranchland, and it's part of the Hells Canyon Scenic Byway. The last stretch from Joseph to the lake is Highway 351.

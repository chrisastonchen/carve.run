Sandhills Journey: Highway 2

Highway 2, N-2 · Nebraska Sandhills, Nebraska

NE-2 from Broken Bow across the heart of the Sandhills to Hyannis: grass-covered dunes to the horizon, small lakes and windmills, beside the coal trains on the BNSF line. Almost no bends and long gaps between towns, so fill up first.

Thousand Islands Parkway

Thousand Islands Parkway, 1000 Islands Parkway · Thousand Islands, Ontario

The Thousand Islands Parkway beside the St. Lawrence from Gananoque to Brockville: close to the water with island views, past the Thousand Islands Bridge at Ivy Lea and the village of Rockport, with a bike path alongside. Gentle curves.

South Boundary Road

South Boundary Road, South Boundary Rd · Porcupine Mountains, Michigan

South Boundary Road along the southern edge of Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park, from M-107 to Presque Isle Road: 24 paved miles of curves and hills through hardwood forest, with side roads to Summit Peak and waterfalls.

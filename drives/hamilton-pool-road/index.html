Hamilton Pool Road

Hamilton Pool Road, RM 3238, Ranch Road 3238, RM 962 · Pedernales River, Texas

Hamilton Pool Road west from Bee Cave through ranch hills, past Hamilton Pool Preserve and down switchbacks to the Pedernales River crossing, ending at RR 962. The preserve needs a reservation; trucks with six or more wheels are barred near the river.

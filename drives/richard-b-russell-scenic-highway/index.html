Richard B. Russell Scenic Highway

GA 348, Richard B. Russell, Russell Scenic, State Route 348 · Blue Ridge Mountains, Georgia

GA 348 from GA 75 Alternate near Helen over Hogpen Gap, where the Appalachian Trail crosses, to GA 180: a steep, winding climb of about seven miles to the gap, then a long, curving run down through forest. Can close briefly after snow or ice.

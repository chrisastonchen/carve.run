The drive

Georgia 348, the Richard B. Russell Scenic Highway, runs 13.8 miles from GA 75 Alternate near Helen to Georgia 180, crossing Hogpen Gap, where the Appalachian Trail crosses. It takes about 25 minutes. The climb is 2,700 feet to a high point of 3,500 feet, with most of the twisting in the first eight miles.

It is part of the Russell-Brasstown Scenic Byway, a loop of about 40 miles through the Chattahoochee-Oconee National Forest that follows Georgia 348, Georgia 180 and Georgia 17/75, and the Forest Service suggests allowing at least three hours. The byway also has a spur to Brasstown Bald, Georgia's tallest mountain, with a visitor center and 360-degree views.

Good to know

  • The Appalachian Trail crosses the road at Hogpen Gap.
  • The loop back to Helen uses Georgia 180 and Georgia 17/75, and Brasstown Bald is a short spur off Georgia 180.
  • Check 511GA for closures before you go.

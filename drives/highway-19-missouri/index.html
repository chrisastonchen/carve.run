The drive

Highway 19 south of Salem is a 40.8-mile run to Eminence, with two twisty stretches in an otherwise sweeping road. South of Cuba the highway is designated a scenic route, and it passes through Ozark National Scenic Riverways and parts of the Mark Twain National Forest.

Round Spring sits right on the road, north of Eminence. The park has campgrounds there, and Round Spring Caverns offers ranger-led tours in summer. The riverways were created by Congress in 1964 and protect the Current and Jacks Fork rivers; Eminence is one of the nearest towns. The drive continues on Highway 106 east from Eminence.

Good to know

  • Driving through Ozark National Scenic Riverways is free; the park says no entrance pass is required.
  • Round Spring Ranger Station is open in summer only, typically Memorial Day to Labor Day.
  • Fees apply for developed campgrounds and Round Spring Cave tours.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Ozark National Scenic Riverways: Current Conditions.

The drive

Highway 140 leaves Mariposa, climbs over the ridge at Midpines, and descends to the Merced River at Briceburg. From there it follows the river canyon up to El Portal, just outside Yosemite National Park. The curves are moderate, with two twistier stretches, one around miles 10 to 13 and one near 21 to 23.

At the Ferguson rockslide, traffic uses a temporary bypass on temporary bridges, shared in one lane. Caltrans has planned a permanent rock shed there, so expect work and check before you go. The drive ends in El Portal, before the Arch Rock entrance station.

Questions about this drive

Highway 140 from Mariposa to El Portal is 29 miles and takes about 45 min, plus any wait at the Ferguson bypass.

What is the Ferguson slide on Highway 140?

It is a rockslide area on Highway 140 in the Merced River canyon. Traffic uses a temporary bypass, and Caltrans plans a permanent rock shed there.

Is there an entrance fee on Highway 140 to El Portal?

This drive ends in El Portal before the Arch Rock entrance station, so no park fee is collected on it.

Is Highway 140 twisty?

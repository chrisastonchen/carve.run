Molly Stark Trail: Route 9

VT 9, Molly Stark, Western Avenue, Main Street · Southern Vermont

VT-9 across southern Vermont from Bennington over the Green Mountains through Woodford, Searsburg and Wilmington to the overlook at Hogback Mountain, then down to West Brattleboro. A main truck route, with broad views from Hogback.

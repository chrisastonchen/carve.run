The drive

The Swift Trail is AZ 366, climbing from the desert south of Safford up Mount Graham in more than 20 miles of switchbacks. You rise from cactus through oak to pine and fir, ending where the pavement stops near Shannon Campground.

It's steep and winding the whole way, with the sharpest bends in the upper half. Past the end of the pavement a gravel road continues, which closes in winter.

Good to know

  • ADOT closes the road past Shannon Campground in winter; last year the closure date was November 15.
  • The road is steep and narrow; allow extra time.
  • Lower parts can close for snow as needed.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Arizona 511 (ADOT).

Questions about this drive

How long does the Swift Trail take?

The Swift Trail from US 191 to Shannon Campground is 21.2 miles and takes about 50 minutes.

Is the Swift Trail open in winter?

The highest section, half a mile past Shannon Campground, closes for the winter, and lower parts can close for snow.

How twisty is the Swift Trail?

Intense. Most of the 21 miles is switchbacks.

West Lake Road: Canandaigua Lake

West Lake Road, CR 16, NY 21, State Highway 21, Main Street · Finger Lakes, New York

West Lake Road (County Road 16) down the west shore of Canandaigua Lake from the city of Canandaigua, close to the water, then NY-21 along the valley side above the lake through Woodville to Naples at the south end of the valley.

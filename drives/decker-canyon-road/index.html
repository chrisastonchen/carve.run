Decker Canyon Road

South Westlake Boulevard, Decker Road, CA 23 · Santa Monica Mountains, California

Highway 23 from Westlake Village over the Santa Monica Mountains and down Decker Canyon to the Pacific Coast Highway: steep, narrow and very tight, with ocean views on the descent. One of the twistiest roads in the range; take it slowly.

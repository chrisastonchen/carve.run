Highway 28: Central Adirondacks

NY 28, State Route 28 · Adirondacks, New York

NY-28 through the central Adirondacks from Old Forge, past the Fulton Chain of Lakes, Inlet, Eagle Bay and Raquette Lake to Blue Mountain Lake: easy curves through deep forest, with lakes beside the road for much of the way.

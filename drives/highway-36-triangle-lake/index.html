Highway 36: Cheshire to Mapleton

OR 36, Highway 36, Mapleton-Junction City · Coast Range, Oregon

OR-36 from the edge of the Willamette Valley at Cheshire west over the Coast Range to Triangle Lake, then down Lake Creek and the Siuslaw River through Deadwood and Swisshome to Mapleton. A quiet, curving two-lane through farms and fir forest beside the water.

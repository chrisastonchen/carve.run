Highway 30: Pikeville to Spencer

SR 30, TN 30 · Cumberland Plateau, Tennessee

Highway 30 from Pikeville in the Sequatchie Valley up the Cumberland Plateau's eastern wall, past the turn for Fall Creek Falls State Park, and across the plateau to Spencer: a winding escarpment climb, then woods and farms on top.

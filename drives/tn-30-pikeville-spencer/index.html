The drive

This is Tennessee Highway 30 from Pikeville, in the Sequatchie Valley, up the Cumberland Plateau's eastern wall to Spencer. It begins at US 127 in Pikeville and is 23.2 miles, climbing about 2,900 feet.

The steep part is the climb off the valley: Wikipedia describes a drop of more than 1,000 feet between the plateau near Mount Crest and the Sequatchie Valley. On top, the road meets TN 101 at Mount Crest and, just before the Van Buren-Bledsoe county line, TN 284, the main access route to Fall Creek Falls State Park. It then crosses the plateau to Spencer, where TN 30 follows College Street through town.

Good to know

  • Wikipedia notes the steep section off the plateau has occasional rockslide closures.
  • TN 284 to Fall Creek Falls State Park branches off near the Bledsoe-Van Buren county line.
  • A 2025 flood briefly closed TN 30 near the park; no current closure was found.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check TDOT SmartWay.

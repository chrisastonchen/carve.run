Scenic Highway 30A

30A, 30-A, County Road 30A · Emerald Coast, Florida

County Road 30A along the Gulf through Grayton Beach, Seaside and Rosemary Beach: a slow, low-speed beach road past rare coastal dune lakes, pine woods and planned towns. Expect heavy traffic, bikes and golf carts, especially in summer.

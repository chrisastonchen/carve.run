The drive

This is the southern end of the West Coast's State Highway 6, 120 km (75 mi) from Haast north to Fox Glacier. It is one of the emptiest main roads in the South Island: rainforest, river flats and lakes, with the Tasman Sea showing at a few places along the way.

From Haast the highway runs through a rugged 30 km stretch to Knights Point, where it meets the coast. It then runs inland past Lake Paringa and returns to the shore at Bruce Bay before the final run to Fox Glacier. Curves are mostly gentle, with one twisty stretch, and the drive takes about 1 hr 40 min.

There is no town between Haast and Fox Glacier, so the drive is about distance and quiet rather than traffic.

Good to know

  • Fill up in Haast; there is no town between Haast and Fox Glacier.
  • SH6 has been closed by slips and floods on this stretch before, including cracking at Knights Point, and there is no detour there.
  • When SH6 is shut between the glacier towns and Haast, the only way round is a very long detour east over a Southern Alps pass.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NZTA Journey Planner: highway conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does Haast to Fox Glacier take on SH6?

Haast to Fox Glacier is 120 km (75 mi) on SH6 and takes about 1 hr 40 min without stops.

Is there fuel between Haast and Fox Glacier?

There is no town between the two, so fill up in Haast before driving the SH6 stretch to Fox Glacier.

Is the Haast to Fox Glacier road often closed?

Slips and floods have closed SH6 on this stretch before, and there is no detour at Knights Point. Check NZTA's journey planner before you go.

What is there to see between Haast and Fox Glacier?

The SH6 drive passes Knights Point, Lake Paringa and Bruce Bay, with rainforest and river valleys between them.

The drive

Virginia State Route 56 crosses the Blue Ridge at Tye River Gap, where it meets the Blue Ridge Parkway. On the Nelson County side it is known as Crabtree Falls Highway. This drive runs 20.8 miles in about 45 minutes, from Massies Mill through Tyro, then along the South Fork of the Tye River past Crabtree Falls and Montebello.

After Tye River Gap the road crosses into Rockbridge County and descends to Vesuvius, passing under a Norfolk Southern railway there. It is twisty most of the way.

Questions about this drive

How long does Crabtree Falls Highway take to drive?

Crabtree Falls Highway (Virginia Route 56) from Massies Mill to Vesuvius is 20.8 miles and takes about 45 minutes.

Does Route 56 cross the Blue Ridge Parkway?

Yes. Route 56 crosses the Blue Ridge Parkway at Tye River Gap, where it enters Nelson County and becomes Crabtree Falls Highway.

Is Crabtree Falls Highway twisty?

Yes, it is twisty most of the way.

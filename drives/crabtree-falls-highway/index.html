Crabtree Falls Highway: Highway 56

Crabtree Falls Highway, Tye River Turnpike, VA 56 · Tye River Valley, Virginia

VA-56 from Massies Mill up the Tye River past Crabtree Falls and Montebello to the Blue Ridge Parkway at Tye River Gap, then down the steep switchbacks to Vesuvius. Tight and narrow on the west side; not for trucks or trailers.

The drive

Route 127 on Cape Ann starts at Gloucester's harbor front, passing Stage Fort Park, which has a marker for the city's founding, and the Gloucester Fisherman's Memorial near the Western Harbor. It then follows the Annisquam River and continues around the cape past Halibut Point State Park to Rockport.

Route 127 is described as a scenic road with a good view along much of Essex County's coast.

The drive is 8.9 miles and takes about 25 minutes. It has three twisty stretches, and the climb is only 600 feet.

Route 127 from Stage Fort Park in Gloucester to Rockport is 8.9 miles and takes about 25 minutes.

It has three twisty stretches, and the road is narrow, so it is a slow drive.

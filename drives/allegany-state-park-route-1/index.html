ASP Route 1: Allegany State Park

ASP Route 1, Scenic Drive, Asp Rte 1 · Allegany State Park, New York

ASP Route 1 through Allegany State Park, from the Salamanca entrance past Red House Lake and over forested hills to the Quaker Run area. Limits are 25 to 35 mph. A vehicle fee is collected on summer days; closed to cars in winter.

When to go

South of Red House it is not plowed after November 30 and is closed to cars from December 20 until spring, when it serves as a snowmobile trail. Usually closed January to March; it's open the rest of the year.

Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs. Check the state or county's road conditions before you set off.

The drive

The Arches park road runs from US 191 up the switchbacks above Moab Canyon, past Park Avenue and the Windows turnoff and Balanced Rock, to Devils Garden at the end of the road. The first few miles climb quickly out of the canyon, then the road runs through red rock country with fins and arches in view.

It's 18 miles one way, mostly gentle with one twisty stretch at the start. Plan for stops at the pullouts.

Good to know

  • There's a park entrance fee, $30 per car; motorcycles pay less.
  • Check the park for whether timed-entry reservations are required this year. Entrance lines can be long, especially March to October.
  • The park sometimes holds cars at the gate when parking is full.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check National Park Service: Arches conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Arches Scenic Drive take?

The Arches park road from US 191 to Devils Garden is 18 miles and takes about 40 minutes without stops.

Do I need a reservation for Arches in 2026?

Rules can change from year to year, so check the park's page before you go. Lines can be long.

How much does it cost to drive Arches?

The standard private-vehicle pass is $30.

Does the Arches road end at Devils Garden?

Yes, the road ends at Devils Garden, so you return the same way.

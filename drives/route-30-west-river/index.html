Route 30: West River Valley

VT 30, Main Street, West River Road, Vermont Route 30, Linden Street · Southern Vermont

VT-30 up the West River from Brattleboro through the village greens of Newfane and Townshend, past the Townshend Dam and the Scott Covered Bridge, to Jamaica. Gentle curves beside the river, with farms and wooded hills.

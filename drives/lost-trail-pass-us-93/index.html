Lost Trail Pass: US-93

US 93, South Main Street, Nez Perce National Historic Trail · Bitterroot Valley, Montana

US-93 south from Darby up the narrowing Bitterroot valley past Sula, then a curving climb through forest to Lost Trail Pass on the Idaho line at 7,014 feet, by the Lost Trail ski area. The valley half is gentle; the last miles wind up to the pass.

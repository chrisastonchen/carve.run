Monteagle Mountain: Highway 41

US 41, Dixie Highway · South Cumberland Plateau, Tennessee

US 41 from Monteagle down off the Cumberland Plateau to Pelham: the old highway's descent of Monteagle Mountain, which I-24 has largely replaced. Short and steep, with a run of tight bends through the woods; turn around at Pelham to climb it.

Devil's Backbone

RM 12, RR 12, RM 32, Ranch Road 32 · Hill Country, Texas

RR 12 south from Wimberley, then RM 32 west along the Devil's Backbone, a limestone ridge with views over the hills on both sides, past the Devil's Backbone Tavern to the FM 3424 turn. Short, curvy and busy with motorcycles on weekends.

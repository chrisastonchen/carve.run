Kootenay Pass: Highway 3

Crowsnest, CN 3 · Selkirk Mountains, British Columbia

Highway 3 from Salmo to Creston over Kootenay Pass, at 1,774 m one of Canada's highest highway passes open all year: a long climb to Bridal Lake at the summit, then a steep descent toward the Creston valley. Avalanche control closes it for hours in winter.

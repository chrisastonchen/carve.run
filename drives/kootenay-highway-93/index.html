Highway 93: Kootenay National Park

93, Banff-Windermere · Kootenay National Park, British Columbia and Alberta

Highway 93 from Radium Hot Springs to Castle Junction: through the red walls of Sinclair Canyon, over Sinclair Pass, up the Kootenay and Vermilion valleys past Numa Falls, and over the Continental Divide at Vermilion Pass. A park pass is needed to stop.

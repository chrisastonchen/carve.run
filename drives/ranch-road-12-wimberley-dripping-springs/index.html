RR 12: Wimberley to Dripping Springs

RM 12, RR 12, Ranch Road 12 · Hill Country, Texas

RR 12 north from Wimberley out of the Cypress Creek valley and over the hills to Dripping Springs: a two-lane road of steady curves and short climbs through cedar and oak, with weekend traffic between the two towns.

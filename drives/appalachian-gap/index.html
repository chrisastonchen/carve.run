The drive

Vermont Route 17 crosses the Green Mountains at the Appalachian Gap, a pass at 2,375 feet. This drive runs 15.6 miles west from Waitsfield on Route 100 to Route 116 near Bristol and takes about 25 minutes. The east side starts in the Mad River Valley and passes Mad River Glen, the ski area on the east side of Stark Mountain, before climbing to the gap.

The climb is serious: the road includes sustained pitches of 15 percent or more over several kilometers. It is a popular destination for both cyclists and motorcyclists, and the summit is the finish of stage 3 of the annual Green Mountain Stage Race. The Long Trail crosses the gap between Stark Mountain and Baby Stark Mountain.

From the top there are views of the Champlain Valley, Lake Champlain and the Adirondacks. The long descent runs down the western side toward Bristol, where Route 17 meets Route 116 just west of town.

Good to know

  • Expect steep grades on both sides, with sustained pitches of 15 percent or more, so use a low gear on the way down.
  • Cyclists and motorcyclists are common, and the pass is a regular cycling destination.
  • Check New England 511 before you go, especially in winter, when the site reports driving conditions such as ice.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check New England 511: Vermont road conditions.

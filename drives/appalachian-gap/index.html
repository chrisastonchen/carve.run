Appalachian Gap: Route 17

VT 17, Appalachian Gap, Main Street, Vermont Route 17, McCullough, Drake Woods Road · Green Mountains, Vermont

VT-17 from the Mad River Valley past Mad River Glen and up steep switchbacks to the Appalachian Gap, then down the long western side toward Bristol. Tight hairpins and steep grades both ways; open all year but often icy in winter.

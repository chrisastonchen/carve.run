The drive

The Mormon Emigrant Trail, a paved Forest Service road from Jenkinson Lake at Sly Park to Highway 88, climbing for 24 miles along a forested ridge to about 7,000 feet near Iron Mountain. Little traffic and no services; gated when snow covers the top.

The drive

State Highway 10 is the Far North's cross-country road, and this stretch of it runs from Waipapa, beside Kerikeri, to Mangōnui on Doubtless Bay. It is 55 km (34 mi) of rolling bush and farmland with steady bends rather than sharp ones, and it takes about 45 minutes without stops.

The route heads north-west across the hills to Kāeo, where Whangaroa Road branches off to Whangaroa Harbour, then carries on to the coast at Taipā and Mangōnui. The bridges that used to slow this road have been replaced: the Kāeo bridge is now a longer, higher two-lane crossing, and the Taipā bridge in Doubtless Bay has been two lanes since December 2019.

Kāeo and Mangōnui are the natural places to stop.

Questions about this drive

How long does Highway 10 from Waipapa to Mangōnui take?

State Highway 10 from Waipapa to Mangōnui is 55 km (34 mi) and takes about 45 minutes without stops.

Is Highway 10 in Northland still a one-lane bridge road?

Not at Kāeo or Taipā: the Kāeo bridge on State Highway 10 was replaced with a two-lane bridge, and the Taipā bridge has been two lanes since 2019.

Is Highway 10 twisty?

State Highway 10 from Waipapa to Mangōnui has mostly gentle curves, with steady bends through bush and farmland, and it is not a tight mountain road.

Does Highway 10 flood?

Parts of State Highway 10 near Kāeo can flood after heavy rain, so check NZTA's journey planner before setting out in bad weather.

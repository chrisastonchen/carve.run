Lamar Valley: Tower to Cooke City

Northeast Entrance, US 212, Main · Yellowstone, Wyoming and Montana

Yellowstone's Northeast Entrance Road from Tower Junction to Cooke City: across the Yellowstone River, through the broad Lamar Valley, known for bison and wolves, then up Soda Butte Creek under Barronette Peak. Park fee; open all year.

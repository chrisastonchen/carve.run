The drive

OH-374 south from US 33 past Cantwell Cliffs, Rock House and Conkle's Hollow, then with OH-664 by Old Man's Cave and on past Cedar Falls to Ash Cave on OH-56: tight curves through the gorges of Hocking Hills. Parking fills on weekends.

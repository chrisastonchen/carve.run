Kahekili Highway

Kahekili Highway, Honoapiilani, Honoapiʻilani, HI 340, HI-340, HI 30, HI-30 · West Maui, Hawaii

The Kahekili Highway around West Maui's north end, from Waiʻehu past Kahakuloa village and Nakalele Point to Honolua Bay: sea cliffs and blind hairpins on a road that narrows to one lane, often with no guardrail. Slow and demanding.

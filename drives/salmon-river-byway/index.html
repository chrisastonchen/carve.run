Highway 75: Stanley to Challis

ID 75, Salmon River · Salmon River, Idaho

ID-75 from Stanley down the Salmon River to Challis: the road follows the river through a narrowing canyon past Sunbeam and the Yankee Fork, then opens into high desert near Clayton. Curves that follow the water.

Loveland Pass: US-6

US 6, Loveland Pass · Continental Divide, Colorado

US-6 from the Loveland ski area over 11,990-foot Loveland Pass on the Continental Divide, past Arapahoe Basin to Keystone: hairpins up above the trees and a long descent with views over Summit County. Plowed year-round, but closes in storms.

Highway 86: Silverton to Turkey

TX 86, State Highway 86, Main Street · Caprock Canyons, Texas

TX-86 east from Silverton across the High Plains, then down off the Caprock into Quitaque, the town for Caprock Canyons State Park, and on through red breaks to Turkey and the Bob Wills Museum. Mostly straight, with one long descent.

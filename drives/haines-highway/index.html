Haines Highway

Haines Highway, AK 7, Union Street · Chilkat Valley, Alaska

The Haines Highway from Haines up the Chilkat River through the Alaska Chilkat Bald Eagle Preserve to 33 Mile Roadhouse, beneath glaciated peaks. Late fall brings the largest gathering of bald eagles; stop only at the marked pullouts.

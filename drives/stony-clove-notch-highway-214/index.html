The drive

Route 214 heads north from Phoenicia up Stony Clove, a narrow mountain pass between Plateau and Hunter mountains. It runs beside Stony Clove Creek through Chichester, in the middle of the notch.

The road winds north through Lanesville and Edgewood, past a small pond at the top of the notch, and ends at Route 23A in Hunter. It takes about 15 minutes.

Highway 42 through Deep Notch is the nearby alternative.

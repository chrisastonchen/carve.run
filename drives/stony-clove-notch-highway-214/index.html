Highway 214: Stony Clove Notch

NY 214, State Route 214 · Catskills, New York

NY-214 north from Phoenicia up Stony Clove, a narrow notch between Hunter and Plateau mountains, past Devil's Tombstone campground and Notch Lake at the top, then down to Route 23A by Hunter. Steady curves beside the creek.

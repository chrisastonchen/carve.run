The drive

US 93 between Challis and Salmon is the northern leg of the Salmon River Scenic Byway. This drive heads north from Challis, following the Salmon River through a narrow canyon and open ranch valleys, with the Lemhi Range off to one side. It is 58.3 miles and takes about 1 hr 12 min.

The highway crosses the Pahsimeroi River and rounds the northern end of the Lemhi Range, then crosses the Lemhi Valley south of Salmon. It follows parts of the Lewis and Clark Trail and passes through the Salmon-Challis National Forest. Salmon sits at about 4,000 feet.

Traffic is light and the curves are gentle. The drive continues from the end of the Highway 75 Stanley to Challis drive.

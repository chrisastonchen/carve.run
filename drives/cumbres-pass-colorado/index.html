The drive

CO 17 climbs southwest from Antonito on US 285 toward 10,022-foot Cumbres Pass in the San Juan Mountains, then crosses into New Mexico and ends at Chama on NM 17. The long grades run through meadow and spruce with steady curves.

The road runs near the Cumbres and Toltec Scenic Railroad, which the Denver and Rio Grande built in the early 1880s. Since 1970 the railroad has run the Chama to Antonito section, with trains over the pass in the tourist season.

The drive is 45.8 miles and about 57 minutes, climbing about 3,150 feet. It runs west from Antonito.

Good to know

  • The pass is rarely closed in winter, but CDOT has closed Cumbres and La Manga passes for snow removal, so check COtrip.
  • The highway crosses the Colorado and New Mexico line near the pass and becomes NM 17.
  • Winters are cold at the summit; carry layers outside summer.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check COtrip (CDOT).

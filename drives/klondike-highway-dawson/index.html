The drive

The Klondike Highway (Yukon 2) runs 107.6 miles north from Stewart Crossing to Dawson City. The drive takes about 2 hr 19 min and climbs 3,534 feet to a high point of 2,507 feet. It follows the Tintina Trench with long sweeping bends and a few climbs, and passes the Dempster Highway junction.

The Dempster is gravel and is not part of this drive. The route ends just short of Dawson City.

The road is open year round with winter driving, and services are at Stewart Crossing and Dawson only.

Good to know

  • Services are at Stewart Crossing and Dawson only, so fill up first.
  • Winter driving conditions apply.
  • The Dempster Highway turnoff is gravel and not part of this route.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Yukon 511.

Is the Klondike Highway open in winter?

Where is the Dempster Highway junction?

On the Klondike Highway between Stewart Crossing and Dawson City.

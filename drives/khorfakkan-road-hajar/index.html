The drive

Khorfakkan Road, S142, crosses the Hajar Mountains from the Masafi fork to Khor Fakkan, 21 km (13 mi) in about 15 minutes. It is a modern divided highway that goes through the range by tunnel rather than over a steep pass; the whole Sharjah to Khorfakkan highway has five tunnels. Long sweepers link them, with one tighter stretch.

Al Rafisah Dam sits beside the road, with walking areas and viewpoints around it. The dam was built over a village, and in very calm water the old rooftops can be seen. After the dam the road drops to the Gulf of Oman at Khor Fakkan, where there is a beach, an amphitheater and a waterfall. The drive starts at the fork and does not include the straight run in from the E55 junction near Dhaid.

Good to know

  • Rain is the main hazard in the mountains: check the weather and never cross flooded sections.
  • Sharjah RTA closes individual tunnels now and then for maintenance, usually with a signed diversion.
  • Fog and rain are the things to watch for in the mountains.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Visit Sharjah.

Khorfakkan Road (S142) from the Masafi fork to Khor Fakkan is 21 km (13 mi) and takes about 15 minutes.

What is Al Rafisah Dam on Khorfakkan Road?

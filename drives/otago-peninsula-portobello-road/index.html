The drive

The Otago Peninsula road out of Dunedin goes 26 km (16 mi) to Taiaroa Head at the harbour entrance. It leaves the city on Highcliff Road, which runs along the spine of the peninsula high above Otago Harbour with views over the city and the surrounding country.

The road then drops to Portobello, a harbourside township on the old Portobello road, and continues along Harington Point Road toward Taiaroa Head. The drive ends short of the albatross centre car park. Taiaroa Head has a northern royal albatross breeding colony, the only albatross colony on an inhabited mainland, and a lighthouse dating from 1864.

Curves are mostly moderate, with three twisty stretches, and the drive takes about 45 minutes. Portobello Road along the harbour is the usual alternative to Highcliff, and the two meet at Portobello.

Good to know

  • Highcliff Road is narrow and steep, with constant bends.
  • All roads on the peninsula open to cars are also open to cyclists and pedestrians.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Dunedin City Council: notified road closures.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Otago Peninsula Highcliff Road drive take?

Dunedin to Taiaroa Head via Highcliff Road, Portobello and Harington Point Road is 26 km (16 mi) and takes about 45 minutes.

What is at the end of the Otago Peninsula road?

Taiaroa Head, with a northern royal albatross colony, a viewing centre and a lighthouse dating from 1864.

Is Highcliff Road narrow?

Yes. Highcliff Road is narrow, steep and constantly bending, along the ridge above Otago Harbour.

What is the alternative to Highcliff Road on the Otago Peninsula?

Portobello Road along the harbour is the usual alternative, and the two meet at Portobello.

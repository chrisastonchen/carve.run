Cedar Canyon: Highway 14

UT 14, SR 14, Center Street, Cedar Canyon, Markaguant, Markagunt · Markagunt Plateau, Utah

UT-14 from Cedar City up Cedar Canyon onto the Markagunt Plateau, climbing to nearly 10,000 feet past red cliffs, aspen, lava fields and Navajo Lake, then down through Duck Creek Village to US-89 at Long Valley Junction.

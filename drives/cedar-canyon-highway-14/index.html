The drive

Highway 14 climbs from Cedar City up Cedar Canyon onto the Markagunt Plateau. It reaches nearly 10,000 feet, passing red cliffs, aspen, lava fields and Navajo Lake, then descends through Duck Creek Village to US 89 at Long Valley Junction. It is 40.2 miles and about 55 minutes, with gentle curves.

Duck Creek Village sits at about 8,400 feet. The Highway 148 turnoff to Cedar Breaks National Monument is on the route.

The road is a year-round highway.

Good to know

  • Winter storms can bring chain or traction requirements.
  • Rockslides have briefly closed Cedar Canyon before, in June 2023.
  • Highway 148 to Cedar Breaks closes for winter, typically from mid-November.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check UDOT Traffic.

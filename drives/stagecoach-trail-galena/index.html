Stagecoach Trail

Stagecoach, Hickory, Galena Avenue · Jo Daviess County, Illinois

The 1830s Stagecoach Trail from Galena east to Warren, through Scales Mound and Apple River: a hilly, curving county road over the ridges of Illinois's Driftless corner, with good pavement and little traffic. Some curves are sharp.

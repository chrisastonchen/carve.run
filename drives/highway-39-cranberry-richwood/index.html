Highway 39: Mill Point to Richwood

North Fork Cherry Highway, WV 39, Stamping Creek Road, East Main Street · Monongahela National Forest, West Virginia

WV-39 west from Mill Point up Stamping Creek and over Cranberry Mountain, past the nature center and Cranberry Glades, then down along the North Fork of the Cherry River to Richwood. The western half is part of the Highland Scenic Highway.

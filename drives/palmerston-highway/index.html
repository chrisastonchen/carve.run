The drive

The Palmerston Highway, State Route 25, climbs from the Bruce Highway at Goondi, near Innisfail, through Wooroonooran National Park to Millaa Millaa on the Atherton Tableland. This drive is 47 km (29 mi) of rainforest road. It leaves the cane fields behind and then has a steady run of bends until the Tableland opens out.

At Millaa Millaa the highway meets Beatrice Way, the former Old Palmerston Highway, and Main Street, and State Route 25 carries on as the Malanda-Millaa Millaa Road. The Old Palmerston Highway is a separate tourist drive that is not suitable for caravans.

Landslips and heavy rain after Cyclone Jasper closed the highway in late 2023, and it reopened in stages from early 2024.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Palmerston Highway take to drive?

Goondi to Millaa Millaa is 47 km (29 mi) and takes about 35 minutes.

Does the Palmerston Highway close?

It can. Landslips and heavy rain closed it after Cyclone Jasper in December 2023, and it reopened in stages. Check QLDTraffic for current conditions.

What is the route number of the Palmerston Highway?

It is State Route 25, from the Bruce Highway at Goondi to Millaa Millaa.

Which national park does the Palmerston Highway cross?

It runs through Wooroonooran National Park between Innisfail and the Atherton Tableland.

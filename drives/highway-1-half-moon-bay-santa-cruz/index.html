The drive

Highway 1 along the San Mateo and Santa Cruz coast from Half Moon Bay: open bluffs, beaches and farmland beside the ocean, past Pigeon Point Light Station, Año Nuevo and Davenport to the edge of Santa Cruz. Gentle curves; the draw is the coast.

Mount Charleston Loop

NV 157, Kyle Canyon, NV 158, Deer Creek, NV 156, Lee Canyon · Spring Mountains, Nevada

Kyle Canyon Road climbs from the desert at US-95 into pine forest under Charleston Peak, Deer Creek Road winds high across the mountain's flank, and Lee Canyon Road runs back down through Joshua trees to US-95. Chain controls in winter.

Strait of Juan de Fuca Highway

WA 112, Strait of Juan de Fuca, Bayview, SR 112, State Route 112, Highway 112 · Olympic Peninsula, Washington

Highway 112 follows the Strait of Juan de Fuca west from Joyce through Clallam Bay to Neah Bay on the Makah Reservation, curving along the shore and through forest, with views across the water to Vancouver Island. Slides close it at times.

Big Cottonwood Canyon

UT 190, SR 190, Big Cottonwood · Wasatch Mountains, Utah

UT-190 from the edge of Salt Lake City up Big Cottonwood Canyon, climbing nearly 4,000 feet beside the creek through tight curves and cliffs to the ski areas at Solitude and Brighton. Open all year; winter needs traction-ready tires.

Highway 97A: Wenatchee to Chelan

97 Alt, 97A, Woodin · Columbia River, Washington

US-97A up the west bank of the Columbia from Wenatchee to Chelan: under steep canyon walls past Rocky Reach Dam and Entiat, then over Knapp's Coulee and down to the foot of Lake Chelan. Gentle curves; the river carries it.

Black Mountain: Highway 160

KY 160, VA 160, SR 160, West Main Street, Inman · Black Mountain, Kentucky and Virginia

Highway 160 from the coal town of Lynch up over Black Mountain, Kentucky's highest point, and down to Appalachia, Virginia: hairpins and steep grades the whole way, promoted locally as the Dragon Slayer. Closed to tractor-trailers.

The drive

State Route 260 runs northwest from Matamoras on the Ohio River to State Route 78 near East Union, 36.6 miles and rated Twisty, with about 1 hr 5 min of driving. It crosses Washington, Monroe and Noble counties, including Bethel Township in Monroe County.

Route 260 is signed as a north-south highway even though it runs northwest to southeast. It shares pavement with Route 26 for a short distance in Ludlow Township, Washington County, and with Route 145 for a little under two miles in Elk Township, Noble County.

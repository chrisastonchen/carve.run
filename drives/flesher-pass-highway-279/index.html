The drive

Montana Highway 279 starts on Lincoln Road West on the north edge of Helena, crosses the valley and goes over Flesher Pass to Highway 200 east of Lincoln. The first part is open valley. Then the road winds up through forest to the pass and down the other side.

Highway 279 is 39 miles in all from Helena to Highway 200 near Flesher Pass.

At Highway 200, Lincoln is a few miles west and Rogers Pass is east.

Questions about this drive

How long does Flesher Pass Highway 279 take?

Highway 279 from Helena to Highway 200 is 36 miles and takes about 55 minutes.

Is Flesher Pass open in winter?

Yes. It is plowed in winter and is not on MDT's seasonally closed list.

Where does Highway 279 end?

It ends at Highway 200 near Flesher Pass, east of Lincoln.

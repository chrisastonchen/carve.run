RM 674: Brackettville to Rocksprings

RM 674, Ranch Road 674, RM 334, North Ann Street, US 377, Del Rio Road · Edwards Plateau, Texas

RM 674 north from Brackettville across the Edwards Plateau to Rocksprings: open brush country first, then hills, dips and creek crossings past Kickapoo Cavern State Park. Almost 60 miles with no services between the towns.

The drive

State Highway 75 is the main road from Christchurch to Akaroa. The drive starts at Halswell and covers 69 km (43 mi) across the Canterbury plain, along the edge of Lake Ellesmere, and up into Banks Peninsula through Little River.

From Little River the road climbs to Hilltop, the highest point on SH75 at about 476 m, where the road looks down on Akaroa Harbour. The descent follows the harbour shore through Barrys Bay and Duvauchelle to Akaroa at the end. The first part is flat and fast; the peninsula section is winding, mostly moderate curves with one twisty stretch.

The drive takes about 1 hr 20 min. It stays on the highway; the Summit Road and several peninsula roads are separate, narrower routes.

Good to know

  • The climb from Little River to Hilltop and the harbour-side descent are winding.
  • Some roads on Banks Peninsula are unsealed or narrow, so stay on SH75 unless a map shows otherwise.
  • Flooding and slips have closed SH75 between Tai Tapu and Akaroa in the past.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check NZTA Journey Planner: highway conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does Christchurch to Akaroa on SH75 take?

From Halswell to Akaroa on SH75 it is 69 km (43 mi) and about 1 hr 20 min; from central Christchurch add the city leg.

What is the highest point on the Akaroa road?

Hilltop, at about 476 m, is the highest point on SH75, with views over Akaroa Harbour.

Is the road to Akaroa winding?

The plain is straight, but the peninsula section from Little River over Hilltop and down the harbour is winding.

Does SH75 to Akaroa close?

Occasionally, for flooding or slips. Check NZTA before heading to Banks Peninsula after heavy rain.

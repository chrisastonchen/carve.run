Qu'Appelle Valley: Highway 56

56, Ellisboro, Katepwa · Qu'Appelle Valley, Saskatchewan

Highway 56 from Indian Head down into the Qu'Appelle Valley and along the Fishing Lakes: Katepwa Lake, Lebret on Mission Lake, Fort Qu'Appelle and the north shore of Echo Lake to Echo Valley Provincial Park. Valley-side curves an hour from Regina.

Platte Clove Road

Platte Clove, West Saugerties Road, CR 33, Spruce Street · Catskills, New York

A steep, winding mountain road up Platte Clove from West Saugerties to Tannersville, climbing beside the Plattekill's waterfalls with tight hairpins and stretches only one lane wide, which is why people seek it out. Gated shut every winter.

The drive

Evans Notch is a mountain pass in the White Mountains, about a mile east of the New Hampshire border, at 1,410 feet. Maine Route 113 crosses it, and this drive runs south from US 2 at Gilead to Stow.

The notch drains north through Evans Brook, a tributary of the Wild River. Route 113 itself is unusual: it repeatedly enters and leaves New Hampshire, and a few miles past Hastings Campground it enters Chatham, NH, where the signs follow New Hampshire's style but the road remains Maine 113.

The drive is 16.9 miles and takes about 25 minutes, with mostly gentle curves.

Good to know

  • Route 113 is not maintained between Gilead and Stow in winter, and a winter gate blocks the road about 1,000 feet past the Evans Notch Road and Gilead Road junction.
  • Check conditions before a late-fall or early-spring trip.
  • Part of the road runs through New Hampshire; it remains Maine 113 there.

Not maintained through the notch in winter: gated from about the first heavy snow until spring. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check New England 511: Maine road conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long does Evans Notch take to drive?

The drive

This is US 36 climbing west from the edge of Lyons through North St. Vrain Canyon to Estes Park, ending at Lake Estes. The road follows the creek, narrow in places, and rises about 3,200 feet from the plains edge into the foothills. It is 18.8 miles and takes about 26 minutes.

It is a quieter way into Estes Park than the Big Thompson road. The canyon is a place that has been rebuilt: the September 2013 floods closed the 31 miles of US 36 between Estes Park and Boulder, and North St. Vrain Canyon was among the hardest-hit areas. The road reopened about two months later.

Estes Park is also the gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park and the starting point of Peak to Peak and Trail Ridge Road drives.

Questions about this drive

How long is US 36 from Lyons to Estes Park?

US 36 from Lyons to Estes Park is 18.8 miles and takes about 26 minutes.

Is US 36 through North St. Vrain Canyon curvy?

Yes. It is a winding two-lane road along the creek with few passing zones.

Was US 36 to Estes Park damaged in the 2013 floods?

Yes. The 2013 floods closed US 36 between Estes Park and Boulder for about two months, and North St. Vrain Canyon was among the hardest-hit areas.

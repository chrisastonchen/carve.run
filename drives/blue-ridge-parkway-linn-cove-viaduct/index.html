Blue Ridge Parkway: Linn Cove Viaduct

Blue Ridge Parkway · Grandfather Mountain, North Carolina

The Blue Ridge Parkway from Moses Cone Manor at Blowing Rock past Price Lake and across the Linn Cove Viaduct, curving around the face of Grandfather Mountain, to Linville Falls: big views and smooth sweepers. Expect short one-lane work zones.

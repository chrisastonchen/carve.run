Cherokee Foothills: SC 11

SC 11, Cherokee Foothills, Highway 11, US 276 · Blue Ridge Foothills, South Carolina

SC 11, the Cherokee Foothills Scenic Highway, along the foot of the Blue Ridge Escarpment from US 25 to SC 183: a rolling, sweeping two-lane past peach stands, Table Rock's granite face and Lake Keowee, with the mountains filling the view to the north.

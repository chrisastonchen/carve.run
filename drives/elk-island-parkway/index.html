The drive

The Elk Island Parkway runs north from Highway 16 through Elk Island National Park, about 48 km from Edmonton's city centre. It is a 10.4-mile, sweeping drive that takes about 20 minutes, passes Astotin Lake, and ends at the park's north boundary.

Bison are the reason many people come, and they often linger on the road. Parks Canada says bison are hard to spot at night, and to be especially watchful at dawn and dusk. The speed limit is 60 km/h on the main parkway and 20 km/h in the Astotin Lake Recreation Area.

Good to know

  • Stay in your vehicle around wildlife, pull over only at designated viewing areas, and use hazard lights to warn other drivers.
  • There are no gas stations in the park, so start with a full tank.
  • Watch for cyclists, joggers and roller skiers, especially at dawn and dusk.
  • Park admission is charged, and youth 17 and under are free.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Parks Canada: Elk Island bulletins.

Questions about this drive

How long is the Elk Island Parkway drive?

The parkway drive is 10.4 miles and takes about 20 minutes.

What is the speed limit on the Elk Island Parkway?

Parks Canada gives a 60 km/h maximum on the main parkway and 20 km/h in the Astotin Lake Recreation Area.

Is there gas inside Elk Island National Park?

No: Parks Canada says there are no gas stations in the park, so fill up first.

Do you need to pay to enter Elk Island National Park?

Yes, there is daily admission or an annual pass, and youth 17 and under are free.

River Road: Lajitas to Presidio

FM 170 · Big Bend, Texas

FM 170, the Camino del Rio, along the Rio Grande from Lajitas through Big Bend Ranch State Park to Presidio: canyon walls, river views and the Big Hill, a steep climb and drop over a ridge above the river. No services between the two towns.

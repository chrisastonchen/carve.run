Mount Tamalpais: Panoramic and Ridgecrest

Panoramic, Pantoll, Ridgecrest · Mount Tamalpais, California

Panoramic Highway climbs from Highway 1 past Mountain Home Inn to Pantoll, then Ridgecrest Boulevard runs along the bare upper ridge of Mount Tamalpais with the Pacific far below. Narrow and tight, with cyclists; the park closes at sunset.

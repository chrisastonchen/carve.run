Highway 25: Hollister to Pinnacles

CA 25, Airline Highway · San Benito Valley, California

CA-25 south from Hollister through Tres Pinos and Paicines: long sweeping curves through the ranchland and oak hills of the San Benito Valley, ending near the road to the east entrance of Pinnacles National Park.

The drive

Bouquet Canyon Road climbs from the north edge of Santa Clarita up a creek canyon in Angeles National Forest, past Bouquet Reservoir, to the high desert at Leona Valley. Steady curves for 18 miles; the county closes it ahead of heavy storms.

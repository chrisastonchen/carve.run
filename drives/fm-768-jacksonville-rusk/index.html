FM 768: Jacksonville to Rusk

FM 768, Corinth Road, Chandler Street · Piney Woods, Texas

FM 768 from Jacksonville through Gallatin to Rusk, which Rider magazine calls the Queen of Motorcycle Roads: a string of tight reversals and short climbs through pine and hardwood that crowd the road. Watch for mud and gravel in the turns.

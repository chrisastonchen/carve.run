The drive

The 3.5-mile road up Cadillac Mountain in Acadia, switchbacking over bare pink granite to the summit with views over Bar Harbor and Frenchman Bay. Needs a park pass and, May 20 to Oct 25, a timed vehicle reservation; closed in winter.

The drive

Route 243 from Knowlton through Bolton Pass to Mansonville, then west on Chemin de la Vallée-Missisquoi along the Missisquoi River to Glen Sutton: a gentle valley drive between the Sutton mountains and Owl's Head, past farms and the river.

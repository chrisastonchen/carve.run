The drive

This is the southern part of the Seward Highway, 125 miles in all between Anchorage and Seward, completed in 1951. The drive runs from Portage over Turnagain Pass and past Moose Pass to Seward, on a road that is AK-9 for its first 37 miles from Seward.

The highway has been an All-American Road since 2000, and U.S. Bicycle Route 97 runs alongside it. Life magazine listed it among the most scenic drives in the world in 2012. The Sterling Highway leaves it at Tern Lake.

Questions about this drive

How long does the Seward Highway from Portage to Seward take?

The Seward Highway from Portage to Seward is 76.7 miles and takes about 1 hr 20 min.

Is the Seward Highway a scenic byway?

Yes. It has been a National Forest Scenic Byway and, since 2000, an All-American Road.

Seward Highway: Portage to Seward

Seward Highway, AK 1, AK 9 · Kenai Peninsula, Alaska

The Seward Highway from Portage over Turnagain Pass and past Summit Lake, Moose Pass and Kenai Lake to Seward on Resurrection Bay: alpine meadows, lakes and glacier-cut valleys on a two-lane road with occasional passing lanes.

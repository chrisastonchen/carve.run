Highwood Pass: Highway 40

40, 541, Kananaskis Trail, Highwood · Kananaskis Country, Alberta

Highway 40 south through Kananaskis Country over Highwood Pass, at 2,206 m the highest paved pass in Canada, then Highway 541 down the Highwood River to Longview. The pass section closes December 1 to June 14. Watch for wildlife and cyclists.

The drive

WI-42 up the Green Bay shore of the Door Peninsula from Egg Harbor through Fish Creek, past Peninsula State Park to Ephraim and Sister Bay, then through woods past Ellison Bay and Gills Rock to the Northport ferry dock. Busy in summer and fall color.

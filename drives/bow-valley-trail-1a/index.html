Highway 1A: Cochrane to Canmore

1A, Bow Valley Trail · Bow Valley, Alberta

Highway 1A west from Cochrane along the north side of the Bow River: past Ghost Lake, through Mînî Thnî and Exshaw at the mouth of the Bow Valley, to Canmore. A quieter two-lane alternative to the Trans-Canada with the front ranges ahead.

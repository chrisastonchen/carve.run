Highway 16: Bandera to Kerrville

TX 16, State Highway 16, Medina Highway, Main Street, Sidney Baker Street · Hill Country, Texas

TX-16 from Bandera up the Medina River valley to Medina, then north over the divide on a winding climb with long views before dropping into Kerrville. A classic Hill Country motorcycle road between two of its riding towns.

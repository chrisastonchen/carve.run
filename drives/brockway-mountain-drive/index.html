The drive

Brockway Mountain Drive climbs a ridge above the Keweenaw's Lake Superior shore between Eagle Harbor and Copper Harbor, about 720 feet above the lake, with overlooks of the water and forest. Narrow, unmarked and steep; closed by snow in winter.

Good to know

Not plowed; closed to cars from late fall, when snow makes it impassable, until about May, and used as a snowmobile trail. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check the state or county's road conditions.

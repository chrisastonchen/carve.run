Great River Road: Alton to Grafton

IL 100, Great River Road, Illinois Route 100, Main Street · Mississippi River, Illinois

IL-100 from Alton under the limestone bluffs along the Mississippi, past the Piasa bird and Elsah, to Grafton where the Illinois River meets it, and on to Pere Marquette State Park. Flat, with a bike trail alongside; high water can close it.

US 98: Carrabelle to Mexico Beach

US 98, Highway 98, Avenue E, Monument Avenue, Market Street · Forgotten Coast, Florida

US 98 west along the Forgotten Coast from Carrabelle beside St. George Sound, over the bridge into Apalachicola, and on through Port St. Joe to Mexico Beach: a flat, open two-lane with water beside the road for miles and old fishing towns.

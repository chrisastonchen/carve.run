Route 132: Gaspé Peninsula

132, Sainte-Anne, Prudent-Cloutier, Principale, Saint-François-Xavier, Renard · Haute-Gaspésie, Quebec

Route 132 along the north coast of the Gaspé from Sainte-Anne-des-Monts to Rivière-au-Renard: the road runs between the cliffs and the Gulf of St. Lawrence, dipping into fishing villages like Mont-Saint-Pierre and Grande-Vallée. Expect roadwork.

US-550: Durango to Silverton

US 550 · San Juan Mountains, Colorado

US-550 from Durango up the Animas valley past Purgatory, over 10,640-foot Coal Bank Pass and 10,910-foot Molas Pass, then down switchbacks into Silverton. Lakes and fourteeners at Molas; the Million Dollar Highway continues to Ouray.

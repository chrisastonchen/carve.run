Chief Joseph Scenic Byway

Chief Joseph, WY 296, WY-296 · Absaroka Range, Wyoming

WY-296 from Highway 120 north of Cody to the Beartooth Highway: switchbacks up to 8,071-foot Dead Indian Pass, then across Wyoming's highest bridge over Sunlight Gorge and up the Clarks Fork valley through ranch land and forest. Few services.

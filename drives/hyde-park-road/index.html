Hyde Park Road

NM 475, Hyde Park, Artist, Ski Basin · Sangre de Cristo Mountains, New Mexico

NM-475 from Santa Fe up into the Sangre de Cristo Mountains to the Ski Santa Fe basin: a winding climb of tight curves through piñon, ponderosa and then aspen, past Hyde Memorial State Park and the Aspen Vista trailhead to the ski area.

Highway 39: Ogden Canyon and Monte Cristo

UT 39, SR 39, Ogden Canyon, Monte Cristo · Wasatch Mountains, Utah

UT-39 from Ogden through narrow Ogden Canyon to Pineview Reservoir and Huntsville, then a long climb over the 9,000-foot Monte Cristo range through aspen and high meadow to Woodruff. The pass closes for winter.

Old Spiral Highway

Old Spiral · Lewiston Hill, Idaho

The Old Spiral Highway up Lewiston Hill: the 1917 road out of Lewiston, a stack of tight switchbacks that climbs the grass slope above the Snake and Clearwater rivers, with the two towns spread below. Bypassed by US-95, lightly used.

Kings Canyon Scenic Byway

Kings Canyon Scenic Byway, CA 180 · Kings Canyon, California

Highway 180 from Grant Grove down into the canyon of the South Fork Kings River, past Boyden Cavern and Grizzly Falls to Cedar Grove and Road's End beneath granite walls. Park fee; closed past Hume Lake Road from mid-November to late April.

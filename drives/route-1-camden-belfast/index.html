Route 1: Camden to Belfast

US 1, Atlantic Highway, Elm Street, Main Street, High Street, Northport Avenue · Midcoast, Maine

US-1 from Camden north along Penobscot Bay below the Camden Hills, past the beach and Islesboro ferry pier at Lincolnville Beach, to the brick downtown of Belfast. A main coastal road, busy in summer, with long bay views.

The drive

TN 116 runs from Rocky Top northeast through Briceville and along New River to Petros, where it passes the Brushy Mountain State Penitentiary. It is the western side of the Devil's Triangle.

Briceville is near Fraterville, where an 1902 mine explosion happened. Along New River the road runs beside a former rail line and many abandoned coal mines and tipples.

It is 32.1 miles and about 1 hr 5 min, rated Intense: twisty most of the way, with steep grades.

Good to know

  • Wikipedia notes TN 116 has a high crash rate, with narrow shoulders, numerous curves, unstable slopes, rockfall hazards and a lack of guardrails; TDOT announced improvements in 2013.
  • Rock and debris on the pavement are a hazard, and rockfall is a known problem on this road.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check TDOT SmartWay: Tennessee road conditions.

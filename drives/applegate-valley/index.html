The drive

Oregon 238 runs from Jacksonville through the Applegate Valley to Murphy. It passes Ruch, about 7 miles from Jacksonville, where the Upper Applegate road heads toward Applegate Lake, then Applegate, and ends at Murphy.

The valley is home to the Applegate Valley Wine Trail, with wineries along Highway 238. It is a Leisurely drive of rolling farmland and river valley.

What is on the Applegate Valley drive?

How do you get to Applegate Lake?

From Ruch, take the Upper Applegate road about 15 miles to Applegate Lake.

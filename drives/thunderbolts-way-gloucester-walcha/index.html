The drive

Thunderbolts Way crosses the Great Dividing Range for 146 km (91 mi) between Gloucester and Walcha. The road is sealed all the way and described as very hilly and winding, climbing out of Gloucester on steep switchbacks through hardwood forest with limited visibility, then running onto the New England tableland with a total climb of about 9,400 feet.

The only villages on the way are Barrington and Nowendoc, and Nowendoc sits a little north of the road. Along the way there are national parks and nature reserves, Carson's Lookout between Gloucester and Nowendoc, riverside camping at Gloryvale and Bretti Reserves, and a picnic area with toilets near the Barrington River bridge. Dingoes, koalas and wombats are occasionally seen.

The road is named for Frederick Ward, the 19th-century bushranger known as Captain Thunderbolt, who roamed this country. Eric Carson, a Gloucester sawmiller and road builder, built a 32 km route through the ranges to Nowendoc that was finished by 1961. The Gloucester section took the name Thunderbolts Way in 1990.

Good to know

  • There are few services, so fill up in Gloucester before you start.
  • The climb out of Gloucester has steep switchbacks and limited visibility.
  • Police have warned about heavy vehicles burning out brakes on the descent of Nowendoc Mountain, so expect trucks and leave room.
  • Storms have closed parts of the road in recent years, so check conditions before you set off.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Live Traffic NSW.

Questions about this drive

How long does Thunderbolts Way take to drive?

Gloucester to Walcha on Thunderbolts Way is 146 km (91 mi) and takes about 2 hr 30 min.

Is Thunderbolts Way sealed?

Yes. Thunderbolts Way is sealed between Gloucester and Walcha, though it is hilly and winding.

Why is it called Thunderbolts Way?

Thunderbolts Way is named after Frederick Ward, the 19th-century bushranger known as Captain Thunderbolt, who roamed this part of New South Wales.

Is there fuel on Thunderbolts Way?

Services are few. Barrington and Nowendoc are the only villages, and Nowendoc is slightly off the road, so fill up in Gloucester.

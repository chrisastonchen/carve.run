The drive

The White Pine Scenic Byway follows Idaho 6 and Idaho 3 through timber country, and this drive runs from Potlatch north to St. Maries. Idaho 6 starts at the Washington line near Potlatch, runs east through the town, and reaches Harvard before it climbs.

Between Harvard and Emida the road crests at about 3,600 feet, where a park-and-ski lot serves cross-country skiers at the Palouse Divide. East of Emida it crosses the Palouse River and passes old-growth white pine in the Hoodoo Mountains, inside St. Joe National Forest, then meets Idaho 3 west of Santa. Idaho 3 carries the drive the rest of the way to St. Maries, where the byway crosses the St. Maries and St. Joe rivers.

It is 48.6 miles and takes about an hour, a leisurely drive with about 2,400 feet of climb overall.

Questions about this drive

How long does the White Pine Scenic Byway from Potlatch to St. Maries take?

The drive is 48.6 miles and takes about an hour.

Where does the White Pine Scenic Byway start?

This drive starts in Potlatch on Idaho 6 and ends in St. Maries on Idaho 3. The full byway is 82 miles and begins at Cataldo.

Where does Idaho 6 meet Idaho 3 on the White Pine Scenic Byway?

Idaho 6 ends at a junction with Idaho 3 west of Santa, and Idaho 3 continues to St. Maries.

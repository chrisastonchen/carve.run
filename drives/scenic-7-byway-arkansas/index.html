The drive

Scenic 7 starts at Dover, where Highway 7 separates from Highway 27, and runs north to Harrison. The Encyclopedia of Arkansas notes that the highway passes through Jasper in Newton County, with the former Dogpatch USA park north of town.

The section from Arkadelphia to Harrison was named the first Arkansas Scenic Byway in 1993, and the parts that pass through the Ouachita and Ozark National Forests are National Forest Scenic Byways. Wikipedia says the road crosses both the Ozark and Ouachita Mountains and is popular with motorcycle tourists.

The drive ends at Harrison, where Highway 7 shares a short stretch with US 65. It is 70.3 miles, rated Sweeping, with about 3,900 feet of climbing.

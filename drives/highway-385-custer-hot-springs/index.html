The drive

This is US 385 running south from Custer to Hot Springs. The highway travels through Pringle and Wind Cave National Park on its way, and the 30.1-mile drive takes about 41 minutes. Forest curves in the Black Hills give way to open prairie hills toward the south.

Wind Cave National Park lists bison, pronghorn and prairie dogs among its animals, so keep an eye out for wildlife near the road. Hot Springs is the end of the drive.

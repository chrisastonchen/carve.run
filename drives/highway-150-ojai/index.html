Highway 150: Santa Paula to Rincon

CA 150, Ojai, Baldwin Road, Casitas Pass, Rincon Road · Ojai Valley, California

Highway 150 climbs out of Santa Paula through Upper Ojai into the Ojai Valley, runs through downtown Ojai, then winds west past Lake Casitas and over Casitas Pass to the coast at Rincon. Tight curves on both climbs.

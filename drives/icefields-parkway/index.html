Icefields Parkway

93, Icefields · Banff and Jasper, Alberta

Highway 93 from Lake Louise to Jasper: 230 km of two-lane parkway past Bow Lake, Bow Summit and the Columbia Icefield, with glaciers and peaks on both sides. A Parks Canada pass is required, and fuel and phone signal are scarce.

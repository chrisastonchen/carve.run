Stevens Canyon Road

Stevens Canyon, WA 123, Paradise, SR 123, State Route 123, Highway 123 · Mount Rainier, Washington

Stevens Canyon Road climbs west from Ohanapecosh in Mount Rainier National Park, past the slot of Box Canyon and along the canyon wall below the Tatoosh Range to Reflection Lakes and Paradise. Park entrance fee; closed in winter.

The drive

The southern end of the Blue Ridge Parkway, climbing from Cherokee past Big Witch Overlook and over Soco Gap to Waterrock Knob, near 6,000 feet: tunnels, long views over the Smokies and no stop signs. Busy in leaf season; ice closes it in winter.

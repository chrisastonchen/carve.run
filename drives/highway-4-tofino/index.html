The drive

Highway 4 crosses Vancouver Island from Coombs to Tofino. It passes through Cathedral Grove in MacMillan Provincial Park, the only highway-accessible protected old-growth Douglas-fir forest in the province, beside Cameron Lake. It climbs to a summit called The Hump, then descends to Port Alberni.

West of Port Alberni the road follows Sproat Lake, climbs over Sutton Pass, and winds down the Kennedy River with steep grades. It skirts Kennedy Lake, passes the Ucluelet junction and the Long Beach Unit of Pacific Rim National Park Reserve, and ends in Tofino.

The 100 miles take about 2 hr 25 min.

Good to know

  • Cathedral Grove has long had parking beside the narrow highway; take care with traffic.
  • The western half is winding and steep, with steep grades along the Kennedy River.
  • Parks Canada charges fees at beach parking lots in Pacific Rim National Park Reserve.
  • Maintenance work has caused short delays; check DriveBC.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check DriveBC.

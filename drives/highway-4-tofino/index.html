Highway 4: Coombs to Tofino

BC 4, Pacific Rim, Alberni Highway, Johnston, Campbell, River Road · Vancouver Island, British Columbia

Highway 4 across Vancouver Island from Coombs to Tofino: through the old-growth Douglas firs of Cathedral Grove, past Cameron Lake to Port Alberni, then winding over the mountains and along Kennedy Lake to the Pacific. Narrow, with steep grades.

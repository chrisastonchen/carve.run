The drive

Mengler Hill Road is the short climb over the Barossa Range between two wine towns. It leaves Tanunda on the valley floor, rises past Mengler Hill and its lookout, and drops into Angaston: 14 km (9 mi) in about 15 minutes. The curves are mostly gentle, with one tighter stretch on the climb.

The lookout on Mengler Hill is a popular viewpoint over the Barossa Valley. The Barossa Sculpture Park sits at its base; it was created at the first Barossa International Sculpture Symposium in 1988, with sculptures cut from local marble and granite. The hill is named after an early wine grower. For a flat run through the vineyards, Barossa Valley Way follows the valley floor.

Good to know

  • Mengler Hill Lookout and the Barossa Sculpture Park are free, and access is available at all hours.
  • The lookout has no restrooms, picnic tables or seating, according to local guides.
  • Signs and maps use both Mengler Hill Road and Menglers Hill Road.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check Traffic SA.

Questions about this drive

How long does Mengler Hill Road take from Tanunda to Angaston?

Mengler Hill Road from Tanunda to Angaston is 14 km (9 mi) and takes about 15 minutes without stopping at the lookout.

Is there a lookout on Mengler Hill Road?

Yes. Mengler Hill Lookout looks over the Barossa Valley, with the Barossa Sculpture Park at its base. It is free to visit.

Is Mengler Hill Road twisty?

Mostly gentle, with one twisty stretch on the climb over the Barossa Range.

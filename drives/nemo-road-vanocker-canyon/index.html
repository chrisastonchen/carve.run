The drive

Nemo Road up Boxelder Creek from Rapid City to the hamlet of Nemo, then Vanocker Canyon Road over the hills to Sturgis: quiet two-lane county roads through pine forest and narrow canyons, a favorite ride during the Sturgis Rally.

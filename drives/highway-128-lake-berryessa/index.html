Highway 128: Rutherford to Winters

CA 128, 128, Rutherford, Sage Canyon, Silverado, Grant · Lake Berryessa, California

Highway 128 crosses the Napa Valley at Rutherford, climbs Sage Canyon past Lake Hennessey, skirts the south end of Lake Berryessa and crosses Monticello Dam, then winds down the steep Putah Creek canyon to the orchards of Winters.

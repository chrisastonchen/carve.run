Bear Notch Road

Bear Notch · White Mountains, New Hampshire

A 9-mile road from Bartlett over Bear Notch to the Kancamagus Highway, climbing through hardwood forest with pullouts looking out over the mountains. Not plowed: gated each winter from about mid-November to early May.

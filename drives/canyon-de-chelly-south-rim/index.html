The drive

The South Rim Drive runs from the visitor center in Chinle along the south side of Canyon de Chelly to Spider Rock Overlook. It passes a series of overlooks above the canyon.

Spider Rock, an 800-foot pinnacle, is the highlight at the end, a short walk from the parking lot. The canyon is on Navajo land.

Good to know

  • Rain can cause flash flooding and rockfall; the park says to stay on paved roads and off dirt roads.
  • Entering the canyon floor generally requires an authorized Navajo guide.
  • Check the park for the status of the White House Overlook and trail, which was reported closed for safety.

Open all year, weather permitting. Roads close for snow, fire, storms and repairs: before you set off, check National Park Service: Canyon de Chelly conditions.

Questions about this drive

How long is the South Rim Drive at Canyon de Chelly?

The South Rim Drive from the visitor center to Spider Rock is 15.2 miles and takes about 35 minutes.

Is there an entrance fee for Canyon de Chelly?

There is no fee to drive the rim roads.

Do you need a guide at Canyon de Chelly?

A guide is generally required to enter the canyon floor, but not to drive the rim overlooks.

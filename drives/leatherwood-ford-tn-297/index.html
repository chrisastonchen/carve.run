Leatherwood Ford Road: Highway 297

TN 297, SR 297, Leatherwood, West 3rd, Industrial Lane · Big South Fork, Tennessee

Highway 297 west from Oneida across the plateau, then switchbacking down into the Big South Fork gorge to the river bridge at Leatherwood Ford and back up the far wall, under sandstone bluffs. No park entrance fee.

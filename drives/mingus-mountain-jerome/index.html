Mingus Mountain: Highway 89A

89A, 89a, Main, Hull, Jerome, Prescott–Flagstaff · Black Hills, Arizona

AZ-89A climbs from Cottonwood through the hillside mining town of Jerome in tight hairpins, then twists over Mingus Mountain at about 7,000 feet and down the west side toward Prescott Valley. Jerome's streets are steep and slow on weekends.

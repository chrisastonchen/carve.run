The drive

MO-94 west from Defiance along the Missouri River bluffs and the Katy Trail through wine country by Augusta, Dutzow, Marthasville and Treloar, then over the MO-19 bridge into Hermann. Rolling curves; winery traffic and cyclists on weekends.

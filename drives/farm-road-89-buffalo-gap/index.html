FM 89: Abilene to Buffalo Gap

FM 89, Buffalo Gap Road, West Street · Callahan Divide, Texas

FM 89 south from Abilene through Buffalo Gap, a cattle-trail and stagecoach stop at a pass in the Callahan Divide, and on into the hills to Abilene State Park. Short, with a few good curves; watch for deer.

The drive

Highway 556 leaves the north edge of Sault Ste. Marie and climbs into the Algoma hills to Searchmont, 18 miles away in about 30 minutes. It's paved and curving through the Goulais River country to the ski village.

The pavement ends at Searchmont. Beyond it, Highway 556 is gravel for about 80 km to Highway 129.

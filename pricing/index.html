Questions

Do I need Apex to plan on the web?

No. Planning at carve.run is free, and so are sharing the link, the QR code and the printed turns.

How does the free trial work?

New yearly subscribers get 7 days free. Cancel before it ends and you aren't charged.

Who in the group needs Apex?

Anyone can join a group drive for free, and the group sees them on the map. Seeing everyone else on your own map takes Apex.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Subscriptions renew automatically and you cancel in your Apple Account on iPhone or in Google Play on Android; Apex stays on until the period you paid for ends.

Where do I manage my subscription?

In your Apple Account on iPhone, or in Google Play on Android.

Questions

Do I need Apex to plan on the web?

No. Planning at carve.run is free, and so are sharing the link, the QR code and the printed turns.

How many routes are free?

All of them. Plan and import as many as you like, in the app and on a computer at carve.run/plan. On Android, Carve Apex is included free during early access.

How does Individual's free trial work?

New yearly subscribers get 14 days free. Cancel before it ends and you aren't charged.

Who in the group needs Apex?

Nobody. With a free account, everyone on the route sees each other on the map and the car's screen. Apex adds offline maps, so the drive keeps working where there's no signal.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Subscriptions renew automatically and you cancel in your Apple Account on iPhone or in Google Play on Android; Apex stays on until the period you paid for ends.

Where do I manage my subscription?

In your Apple Account on iPhone, or in Google Play on Android. A club plan is managed on your club's page at carve.run/club.

Does Individual include group drives?

No. Individual is Apex for your own driving: offline maps, road conditions, 3D previews, unlimited AI route planning and popular times. You can still share a route, but the people you share with don't get Apex. To plan club drives, choose Club; it starts with 30 days free.

How does the Club trial work?

Club starts with 30 days free, with everything in the plan. A card is needed to start, and it's charged when the trial ends, unless you cancel before then on your club's page; cancelling during the trial costs nothing. There's one trial for each organizer and each club. More than 5 organizers, invoicing or event help? Write to chris@carve.run.

What do my drivers get?

Each driver opens the day's link in the free Carve app and drives your route turn by turn, with the group on the map and on CarPlay and Android Auto. They don't pay anything. While your plan is active, they also get Carve Apex free on your club's routes: offline navigation, 3D preview, road conditions and popular times where available, from 14 days before the drive through the day after it ends. Your organizers have Apex while your plan is active.

Prices in US dollars; may vary by region. The free trial is for the yearly plan, and converts to a paid yearly subscription unless cancelled before it ends. Subscriptions renew automatically and are managed in your Apple Account or Google Play account.

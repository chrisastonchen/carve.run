How to set up run groups for a club drive

By Chris Aston Chen · Updated November 2026

A big club drive goes better in small groups: five to eight cars, each with a Lead who knows the route and a Sweep who makes sure nobody's left behind. In Carve Club you set the number of groups when you plan the drive, import your sign-up list, and sort everyone on one board, on a computer. For now the groups are for organizers: drivers don't see their group in Carve yet, so you tell them theirs.

Step by step

  1. Say how many groups

    In Plan a drive, How many driving groups? starts at No groups; + makes it 2, and up to 15. The groups are used on every day of the drive.

  2. Import your sign-up list

    On the drive's page, Import a list takes a spreadsheet saved as .xlsx or .csv, or rows pasted with their header (Name, Group, …), straight from your registration export, MotorsportReg's included. Match your columns: Name and Group are needed; Email, Waiver and Driver / Passenger are optional. You see a preview before anything is saved. "Lead" or "Sweep" in the Group column sets that person's role, and "Guest" or "Passenger" in Driver / Passenger marks a passenger. No file? Add people opens the same window, ready for you to paste or type the names.

  3. Sort the board

    Open groups shows a column for each group with its Lead and Sweep. Drag names between groups, or open a person to set their group, Lead or Sweep, and Driver or Passenger. Each group has one Lead and one Sweep. Find someone searches the list, and the header counts drivers and passengers apart.

  4. Keep it current

    When more people sign up, Update from a list with the new export. Anyone no longer on it is only removed if you tick them off, and people you added by hand, Leads and Sweeps are never removed this way.

  5. Tell drivers their group

    Drivers don't see their group in Carve yet. Read the groups out at the drivers' meeting, or send each Lead their group's names, and everyone drives the same route from the day's link.

Running a drive in groups

  • Five to eight cars a group. Small enough to stay together through towns and lights.
  • Space the groups out. A few minutes between groups at the start keeps them from merging on the road.
  • Same route, same link. Every group drives the day's route from the same link, so a change reaches everyone.
  • Regroup at named stops. Each group comes back together at the stops on the route.

Questions

How many run groups can a drive have?

Up to 15. Plan a drive's How many driving groups? goes from No groups to 2, then up to 15.

What does the list need?

A Name and a Group column. Email, Waiver and Driver / Passenger columns are optional. Save the export as .xlsx or .csv, or paste the rows with their header.

Can I import a MotorsportReg list?

Yes. Export the event's entry list from MotorsportReg as .xlsx or .csv and choose it in Import a list, then match the Name and Group columns.

Do drivers see their group in the app?

Not yet. For now run groups are for organizers on the web; tell drivers their group at the meeting or through each group's Lead.

Are run groups part of Club?

Yes, on every club with an active plan.

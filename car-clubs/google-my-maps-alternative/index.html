A Google My Maps alternative for club drives

By Chris Aston Chen · Updated November 2026

Google My Maps is a good way to draw a drive: places, lines and directions layers on a map you can share. But a map isn't navigation. Google says My Maps can't be used offline, and its help describes navigating to a map's places, not along the route you drew. Carve gives every driver spoken turn-by-turn on your exact route, on CarPlay and Android Auto, offline, with the group on one map.

Step by step

  1. Plan the route in Carve on a computer

    Open the planner and search the places from your My Map, or click them on the map. A Google Maps route link imports straight in, with its stops; so do Ride with GPS links and GPX files. Carve doesn't read My Maps' own KML files, so redraw a route from one with your My Map open beside it.

  2. Pick the roads

    Drag the route onto the road you want and add waypoints to hold it there. Carve keeps the route you planned and never reroutes onto a faster road.

  3. Share one link

    Save it and share the link or its QR code. It opens the same route in Carve on iPhone or Android, or as a web page with the map and printed turns.

When My Maps is still the right tool

  • Drawing and annotating. Shapes, colored layers, photos and notes on a map, and importing places from a spreadsheet or KML.
  • Embedding on your website. A public My Map can be embedded on a page.
  • Many clubs keep a My Map of the region and use Carve for the drive itself.

Questions

Can I import a My Maps map into Carve?

Not its KML file. Carve imports a Google Maps route link, a Ride with GPS link or a GPX file, so redraw a My Maps route in Carve's planner, or start from a Google Maps directions link.

Does My Maps work offline?

No. Google's help says you can't use My Maps offline.

Is there a My Maps app?

Not on Android: Google shut the My Maps Android app down on October 15, 2021. A My Map opens in the Google Maps app instead.

What does Carve cost for a club?

Planning, sharing and driving with a signal are free. Club, for organizers, is $499 a year after a 30-day free trial, and gives every driver Apex free on your routes.

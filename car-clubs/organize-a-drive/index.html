How to organize a car club drive

By Chris Aston Chen · Updated November 2026

Plan the drive on a computer with Carve Club: give it a date, a meet time and a route for each day, then send each day's link. Drivers open it in the free Carve app on iPhone or Android and drive your route turn by turn, with Carve Apex free on your routes. Organizing is on the web; the apps are for driving.

Step by step

  1. Start Club on a computer

    Go to carve.run/car-clubs and start the free trial with your club's name. Everything an organizer does happens there, on the web, on a computer.

  2. Name the drive and set its days

    Choose Plan a drive, name it, and give each day a date, a meet time and a leave time. + Add a day makes it a multi-day drive.

  3. Plan each day's route

    Plan route opens the planner for that day. Its start is the meeting place. Search places or click the map, add stops and waypoints, or start from a Google Maps route link, a Ride with GPS link or a GPX file. Carve keeps the roads you picked; it never swaps them for faster ones.

  4. Add your co-organizers

    In Club settings › Organizers, invite up to four more people by email, five with you. They can plan, change and share all of the club's drives, and each has Carve Apex while the plan runs.

  5. Send each day's link

    Each day card has Share: a link and a QR code. Paste the link into your club email, newsletter, Facebook event or group chat, or print the code on the meet sheet. Change the route later and the link stays the same; drivers get the latest when they open it.

  6. On the day, everyone drives the same route

    Drivers open the link in the free Carve app, no account needed, and get spoken turn-by-turn on your route, and on CarPlay and Android Auto with a free account. On your club's routes each driver gets Carve Apex free, from 30 days before the drive through the day after it ends: offline navigation, 3D preview, road conditions, and popular times where available. With a free account and See others on the map on, drivers see each other on one map wherever they have signal.

What Carve Club doesn't do

  • No sign-ups or RSVPs. Take them wherever your club already does, and send the day's link to the people coming.
  • Links are open. Anyone with a day's link can open the route; there's no way to keep it to members.
  • No member route library or headcount. Carve keeps your club's drives, not a list of who came.

Questions

Do my drivers need to pay or sign up?

No. The app is free, and a driver doesn't need an account to drive your route. While your plan is active, they get Carve Apex free on your club's routes. To show on the group map, they sign in to a free account.

Can I plan the drive on my phone?

Organizing is on the web, on a computer: the drive, its days, routes, links and organizers. The iPhone and Android apps are for driving.

How many organizers can a club have?

Up to five, including the club's owner.

Can I reuse last year's drive?

Yes. Duplicate drive copies the times, the pre-drive and each day's route, with new links. You pick the new dates.

What does Club cost?

$499 a year after a 30-day free trial, with a card to start. There's no limit on drivers.

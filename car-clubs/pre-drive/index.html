How to pre-drive a club route

By Chris Aston Chen · Updated November 2026

With Carve Club, a pre-drive (a pre-run) uses the same day links your drivers get later. Set a pre-drive date, send the links to your crew, and after they drive it the pre-drive report shows their reviews road by road and how long each leg and stop took next to your plan. Fix what needs fixing, and the links stay the same.

Step by step

  1. Say yes to a pre-drive

    When you plan the drive, answer Will there be a pre-drive? with Yes and pick its date, before the drive.

  2. Send your crew the day links

    Plan each day's route, then send your crew its link: the same one drivers get later. Anyone signed in to Carve who drives a day's link with the app around the pre-drive date counts as crew.

  3. The crew drives and reviews the route

    Each crew member drives the route turn by turn and rates it afterwards, road by road. You see each review with their name.

  4. Read the pre-drive report

    The report has each day's reviews by road, and a table of every leg and stop: planned, pre-drive and the difference. Each time is the middle (the median) of your crew's times; nobody's own times are shown. Update stop timers sets each timed stop's length to the crew's.

  5. Change the route if you need to

    Edit route on the day card changes the day's route; its link stays the same and drivers get the latest when they open it. The report keeps reviews and times from the earlier route, marked as such.

Good to know

  • Times come from the app. They come in as your crew drives the route with Carve.
  • Durations, not clock times. A pre-drive can run at a different hour from the drive, so the report compares how long things took.
  • Same links, open links. There's no separate pre-drive link, and anyone with a day's link can open it.

Questions

Who can be on the crew?

Anyone signed in to a Carve account who drives a day's link with the app around the pre-drive date. There's no crew list to manage.

Does the organizer see who wrote a review?

Yes. Each review shows the crew member's name. Drive times are only shown as the crew's median.

Is the pre-drive part of Club?

Yes, on every club with an active plan.

Share a drive's route in a Facebook event or WhatsApp group

By Chris Aston Chen · Updated November 2026

Copy the day's Carve link and paste it into your Facebook event's details or your WhatsApp group. On a phone with Carve it opens the route ready to drive; without the app, it opens as a web page with the map, the date and meet time, the stops and printed directions. When you change the route, the link stays the same.

Step by step

  1. Copy the day's link

    On the drive's page, the Share the drive card has each day's link with Copy, a QR code and Share. A route you planned yourself has the same link and code after you save it.

  2. Paste it where your club already talks

    In a Facebook event, put the link in the details, near the date and meet place. In WhatsApp, paste it into the group chat. Pin it or put it in the group description so it's easy to find on the morning. The link's preview shows the route's name and its map.

  3. Drivers open it

    On iPhone or Android with Carve, the link opens the route ready to drive turn by turn, and on CarPlay and Android Auto with a free account. Without the app, it's a web page with the map, the date and meet time, the stops, a QR code and printed directions, so nobody needs the app to look.

  4. Print the QR code for the meet

    Download on the route's page saves the QR code. Put it on a flyer or the meet sheet; a phone camera opens the route.

Before you post

  • Anyone with the link can open the route. In a public event or group, so can anyone who sees the post.
  • One link per day. For a weekend tour, post each day's link with its date.
  • Changes reach everyone. Edit the route and the same link opens the latest; no need to post again.

Questions

Do people need the app to see the route?

No. The link opens as a web page with the map, stops and printed directions. To drive it turn by turn they use the free Carve app on iPhone or Android.

Can I keep the route to members?

No. Anyone with a link can open it, so share it only where you'd share the route.

Does Carve take RSVPs from the event?

No. Keep using Facebook's Going list or your group chat; Carve is for the route.

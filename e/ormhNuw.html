🚗 Parking & Paddock Assignment

Parking is limited, so there will be no separate grid. Upon arrival, waiver personnel will direct you to either:

• Heat 1 (Work 2nd) paddock/grid – closest to the entrance

• Heat 2 (Work 1st) paddock/grid – far end of the lot (see diagram)

Number of runs and/or split sessions will be determined on site based on attendance and start intervals.

Each paddock has two parking lanes with a 4-ft center lane for unloading and tech prep.

APEX HPDE at Eagles Canyon Raceway

Saturday, January 16, 2027 · 2.7-mile counterclockwise layout

Thank you for choosing APEX!

More than just a track day — it’s a driver community.

At Apex, you’re not just signing up for laps; you’re joining a group of enthusiasts who share a passion for learning, improvement, and the joy of driving. Every driver — from first-timers to seasoned veterans — is welcomed, supported, and encouraged to progress at their own pace.

Our instructors and staff are here to guide you through a full day of learning and track time, helping you develop better awareness, precision, and car control in a fun, no-pressure setting.

Expect great driving, great people, and great memories. Between sessions, enjoy lunch in the clubhouse, swap stories in the paddock, and take in the trackside camaraderie that defines Apex HPDE.

What to Expect:

• Structured run groups for all experience levels

• Classroom and on-track coaching

• Community-driven environment — friendly, supportive, and inclusive

• Professional photos available, vendor partners, and an instructor team that loves to teach

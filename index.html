Questions, answered

What is Carve?

Carve is a route planner and turn-by-turn navigation app for scenic drives, on iPhone and Android. You plan the roads you want — the coast road, the canyon, the long way over the pass — stop by stop, and Carve guides you along exactly that route. Unlike everyday navigation apps, it never swaps your route for a faster one.

Does Carve work without cell signal?

Yes, with Carve Apex. Turn on Available Offline for a route while you have a connection, and Carve downloads the map and every turn along it. The drive then works the whole way with no signal — in canyons, in the mountains, and on remote back roads.

Can I see my friends on the map when we drive together?

Yes, with Group in Carve Apex. Send your group the route's link, and each of you turns on Group on the route. Wherever each of you is on it, you'll see everyone on the map and the car's screen, by name, with how far ahead or behind they are. It's off until you turn it on, works only near the route, ends with the drive, and keeps no history.

How much does Carve cost?

Planning, sharing, importing and driving routes with a signal are free. Carve Apex is $29.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, and adds offline maps, Group for driving with friends, guidance back to your route after a wrong turn, CarPlay and Android Auto, and recording your drives.

Does Carve work with CarPlay and Android Auto?

Yes. With Carve Apex, your route, turns and stops appear on the car's screen through Apple CarPlay on iPhone or Android Auto on Android.

What happens if I take a wrong turn?

Carve doesn't reroute you onto a different road. It points you back to the route you planned, and with Carve Apex it gives turn-by-turn directions back to where you'll rejoin it.

Can I import a route from Google Maps?

Yes. Share a route in Google Maps, copy the link, and paste it into Carve's Import — the stops come with it. It's free.

Is Carve good for car clubs and group drives?

Yes. Plan the drive once and share its link: everyone opens the same route in Carve, on iPhone or Android, and changes you make reach them too.

Which phones does Carve run on?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, from the App Store, and Android 10 or later, in early access on Google Play.

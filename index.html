Built for the drive, not the commute.

Mainstream navigation optimises for arriving early and assumes a live connection. Carve does neither.

⟶

The route is the route.

Up to 30 waypoints between start and finish — markers to pin the road you want, stops where you'll pause. Carve never quietly reroutes you onto something faster.

◎

Works when the signal doesn't.

The map and every turn are downloaded for the whole route before you set off. Losing coverage never cancels, pauses, or restarts a drive.

↗

Send the drive, not directions.

One link opens the exact route — stops, markers, and order — on anyone's phone. Bring a drive over from Google Maps in one paste.

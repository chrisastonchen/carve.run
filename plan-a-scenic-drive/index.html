How to plan a scenic drive

By Chris Aston Chen · Updated October 2026

Everyday map apps solve one problem: the fastest way there. A scenic drive is the opposite problem — you know roughly where you want to go, and you want the best roads to get there, then to actually drive them. Here's how to plan one in Carve (carve.run), the scenic-drive planner for cars and motorcycles, and how to make sure the route you plan is the route you drive.

Step by step

  1. Start from a great road

    Open Carve and look at the drives near you, or browse 1,100+ hand-picked drives by state at carve.run/drives. Each one shows how far, how long and how twisty it is — Leisurely, Sweeping, Twisty or Intense. Open one and it's already a route: drive it as it is, or use it as the start of your own.

  2. Or describe the drive you want

    In search, say it in your own words — “a twisty hour, back by 5” — and Carve plans it. Or tap Surprise me for a scenic loop from where you are. Five described drives a day are free; Carve Apex makes them unlimited.

  3. Build it stop by stop

    Search for places or tap the map to add them: up to 10 named stops (the overlook, lunch, fuel) and up to 100 points between start and finish, so you can steer the route onto exactly the roads you want. In Options, pick Scenic, Balanced or Shortest; Scenic leans hardest toward the good roads, and great driving roads are marked on the map as you plan.

  4. Already planned it in Google Maps? Bring it over

    Share the route in Google Maps, tap Copy Link, and paste it into Carve's Import. The stops come with it, and it's free.

  5. Check the route before you commit

    Carve shows the distance, drive time and elevation profile, and the weather along the way. Flyover flies the whole route in 3D, so you can see the switchbacks before you meet them (your first one is free). With Carve Apex, Road conditions show wet roads, ice, fog and wind, and Popular times show when the road is usually quiet.

  6. Download it for the stretches with no signal

    The best roads are where the signal isn't. With Carve Apex, turn on Available Offline in the route's preview while you still have a connection; the map and every turn download, and the route's card says Downloaded when it's ready.

  7. Drive it — exactly as planned

    Tap Go Now. You can start from anywhere: Carve guides you to the route, and if you're nearer the end, it offers to run it the other way round. Spoken turn-by-turn follows your route and never swaps it for a faster one. Take a wrong turn or skip a stop, and Carve leads you forward to where you'll rejoin the route — not back to where you left it. With Apex, it's all on CarPlay or Android Auto.

What makes a great scenic drive

  • Drive the good roads, not the way to them. A loop that starts within half an hour of home usually beats a long out-and-back. Aim for one to three hours on roads you'd choose.
  • Go early, or go on a weekday. The same canyon road is a different drive at 8 on a Sunday morning than at 2 on a Saturday. Carve Apex's Popular times shows when a road is usually quiet.
  • Mind the sun. On east–west roads, a low sun in your eyes makes twisty sections hard work; drive them with it behind you.
  • Check for seasonal closures. High passes like Tioga Pass, the Beartooth Highway and Going-to-the-Sun Road close for snow for months; Carve's drive guides list their seasons.
  • Plan fuel and breaks. Twisty roads use more fuel, and stations are far apart out there. Add a stop every hour or so.

Questions

What's the best app for planning a scenic drive?

Carve is built for it: it finds twisty, scenic roads (1,100+ hand-picked drives, plus a planner that leans toward the good roads), lets you plan stop by stop, and drives the route turn by turn without ever swapping it for a faster one. Everyday apps like Google Maps, Apple Maps and Waze are built to find the fastest route, and re-route you toward it.

How do I stop my map app from rerouting me off a scenic route?

Google Maps, Apple Maps and Waze don't have a setting that locks a route. Carve keeps your route by design: it never re-routes onto a faster road, and after a wrong turn it gives directions back to the route you planned. You can paste a Google Maps route link into Carve to drive it there.

How do I find twisty roads near me?

Open Carve and look at the drives near you — each is rated Leisurely, Sweeping, Twisty or Intense — or tap Surprise me for a scenic loop from where you are. You can also browse every drive by state at carve.run/drives.

Can I drive a scenic route with no cell signal?

Yes, with Carve Apex: turn on Available Offline for the route while you have a connection, and the map and every turn download, so the whole drive works with no signal.

Is it free?

Planning, importing from Google Maps, sharing and driving with a signal are free. Carve Apex — $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month — adds offline maps, Group, 3D previews, CarPlay and Android Auto, road conditions and popular times.

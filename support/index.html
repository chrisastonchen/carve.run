Support

Answers to the usual questions, and a person at the other end.

Write to support@carve.run. For a bug, include your iPhone model, iOS version, the Carve version (Me › About), and — if it's about a route — its share link. We read everything.

Drives and offline

How do I drive a route with no signal?

With Carve Pro, opening a route downloads the map and turns for its whole corridor. Do that while you still have coverage — at home, or before you leave the last town — and the drive works from there with no connection. The Explore card shows a “Downloaded” badge once it's ready; the route's ⋯ menu can download or remove it by hand.

My route says “Download failed”.

Carve keeps a limited number of routes offline and lets the oldest go when the limit is reached. Open the route again to retry; removing downloads you no longer need (route's ⋯ menu › Remove Download) frees room.

I left the route — what happens?

The banner turns orange and points back toward your route. Carve never swaps in a faster route. With Carve Pro and a connection, it also fetches turn-by-turn directions back to the point where you'll rejoin.

Can I start a route from somewhere other than the start?

Yes — start from anywhere. Carve guides you to the nearest point ahead on the route, and if you're closer to the destination than the start, it offers to run the route the other way round.

The app was closed mid-drive.

Reopen Carve: the drive resumes where you were, with your cleared stops intact. Losing signal, locking the phone, or restarting it never ends a drive.

Carve Pro

What's in Carve Pro, and what does it cost?

Planning your own routes (and sharing them), offline drives, turn-by-turn guidance back onto the route, CarPlay, and Google Maps import. $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month. Opening and driving a route someone shares with you is free.

I subscribed on one phone — how do I get Pro on another?

Sign in to the same Apple Account, then in Carve go to Me › Restore Purchases. No Carve account is needed.

How do I cancel?

iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Carve Pro › Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the free trial apply to the monthly plan?

No — the 7-day trial is on the yearly plan only, for first-time subscribers.

CarPlay and sharing

Carve doesn't show up on my car's screen.

CarPlay is part of Carve Pro; without it the car shows a short notice. If you're subscribed, check iOS Settings › General › CarPlay › your car, and make sure Carve is allowed. Start the drive on the phone or from the car's route list — both surfaces stay in step.

A share link opens a web page instead of the app.

That's the fallback for people without Carve. If you have Carve installed and still see the page, open Carve once from the Home Screen, then tap the link again; iOS re-checks which app owns the link.

Importing from Google Maps didn't find my stops.

Carve reads the stops named in a Google Maps directions link (share the route in Google Maps, then Copy Link). Links to a single place have no route to import, and some very long routes are truncated by Google's short links — try the full link from the browser.

Privacy and data

Where are my routes stored?

On your phone. Carve has no accounts and no servers of its own holding your routes; see the privacy policy for what the map services receive.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a route from its ⋯ menu, remove downloads the same way, or delete the app to remove everything at once.

Carve vs Apple Maps

For scenic drives, car clubs and group drives · Checked October 2026

The short answer. Apple Maps is a fine everyday map, built into every iPhone, with offline maps and up to 14 stops. But it's built for getting somewhere, not for the drive: it has no way to find twisty, scenic roads, can't share a multi-stop route, and isn't on Android — so half your group can't open it. Carve finds the good roads, shares one route to everyone on iPhone and Android, drives it without swapping in a faster road, and with Group puts everyone on the same map.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • Finds the roads worth driving. 1,100+ hand-picked drives rated Leisurely to Intense, a planner that leans toward scenic roads, and great driving roads marked on the map. Apple Maps can avoid highways, but can't find you a good road.
  • One route for the whole group — on Android too. Share one Carve link and everyone opens the same route, on iPhone, Android or in a browser. Apple Maps has no Android app, and its sharing sends your ETA, not your route.
  • Everyone on one map. With Group, every driver on the route shows by name on the map and the car's screen, with how far ahead or behind they are.
  • Your route, kept. Carve never swaps your route for a faster one, and after a wrong turn guides you back to it, turn by turn.
  • See it before you drive it. Fly the whole route in 3D, check the weather along the way, and with Apex see road conditions and when the road is usually quiet.

When Apple Maps is the better pick

For everyday driving on iPhone — errands, commutes, walking and transit — Apple Maps is free, built in, and works offline. Carve is for the drives where you care which road you take.

Questions

Is Carve better than Apple Maps for scenic drives?

Yes. Apple Maps finds the quickest way and has no scenic or twisty-road option. Carve finds great driving roads and guides you along exactly the route you planned, without swapping it for a faster one.

Can I share a multi-stop Apple Maps route with friends?

Apple Maps' sharing is built around Share ETA, which lets people follow your drive; it doesn't send them your multi-stop route. In Carve, one link gives everyone the whole route, on iPhone or Android, and your edits reach them.

My friends have Android phones. Can we drive together?

Yes, in Carve: it runs on iPhone with CarPlay and Android with Android Auto, and a shared route opens on both. Apple Maps has no Android app.

Does Carve work offline and on CarPlay?

Yes, both with Carve Apex: each route downloads with its map and every turn, and the drive shows on CarPlay (or Android Auto).

Can I see my friends on the map while we drive?

With Carve's Group, yes: everyone driving the route shows on the map and the car's screen by name. In Apple Maps, Share ETA lets others follow you one way; friends' locations live in Find My.

Is Carve free?

Planning, sharing and driving with a signal are free. Carve Apex is $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month.

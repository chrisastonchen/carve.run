The best apps for scenic drives and group drives, compared

Checked October 2026

In one line. Most map apps are built to get you there fastest; Carve is built for the drive itself — the twisty road you picked, driven exactly as planned, with your whole group on the map and turns that keep working with no signal.

Carve (carve.run) is a scenic-drive planner and turn-by-turn navigation app for cars (and motorcycles), on iPhone with CarPlay and Android with Android Auto. These pages put it side by side with the apps people usually reach for instead, feature by feature, with sources — and say where the other app is the better pick.

What Carve is made for

  • Driving for the drive. 1,100+ hand-picked driving roads, each rated for how twisty it is, and routes you plan stop by stop that Carve never swaps for a faster one.
  • Group drives, club runs and rallies. Plan once, share one link, and everyone — on iPhone or Android — drives the same route. With Group, everyone sees each other on the map by name.
  • Roads with no signal. With Apex, the map and every turn download, so canyons and mountain passes don't break the drive.

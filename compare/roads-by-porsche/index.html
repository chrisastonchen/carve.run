Carve vs ROADS by Porsche

For driving enthusiasts, car clubs and group drives · Checked October 2026

Carve (carve.run) is a scenic-drive planner and turn-by-turn navigation app for cars and motorcycles, on iPhone with CarPlay and Android with Android Auto — the ROADS by Porsche alternative that never drags you back to a waypoint you skipped.

The short answer. ROADS by Porsche and Carve both exist for the love of a good road, and ROADS is free. Carve goes further on the drive itself: planning on a computer or from a GPX file, 1,100+ hand-picked drives with a guide and twistiness rating each, navigation that never swaps your route for a faster one and leads you back after a wrong turn, the map and every turn downloaded for roads with no signal, and Group, which puts everyone on the route on the map and the car's screen. For club runs and group drives, Carve is the better tool.

Skip a stop, and Carve lets it go.

One of the most common complaints about ROADS by Porsche: miss a waypoint or skip a stop, and the navigation keeps trying to send you back to it — so one wrong turn can mean loops you never planned, or giving up and switching to the car's own navigation. Carve was built the other way round. It follows you along the route: take a wrong turn or skip a stop, and Carve's directions lead you forward to where you'll rejoin the road ahead, going the right way — never back to the place you passed. The stop stays on your list in case you want to go back for it; that's your call, not the app's.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • Hand-picked roads with the details. Carve's 1,100+ drives are chosen road by road and rated Leisurely to Intense, each with distance, drive time and what's nearby — not just whatever's been uploaded nearby.
  • Skipped a stop? Carry on. Carve never sends you back to a waypoint you missed. After a wrong turn or a skipped stop, its directions lead forward to where you'll rejoin the route ahead — free. With ROADS, drivers report being routed back to the missed waypoint.
  • The group, live. With Group, everyone driving the route shows on the map and the car's screen by name, with how far ahead or behind they are. ROADS has groups and events, but its listings don't mention a live map of the group.
  • No signal, no problem. Carve Apex downloads the map and every turn, so the drive works through canyons and over passes. ROADS's listings don't mention offline navigation.
  • Plan on a computer, bring your GPX. Build a route on a big screen at carve.run/plan, or drop in a GPX file or Ride with GPS export and Carve matches it to the same roads. Then scan the QR code to drive it, send the link, or print the turns.
  • Know before you go. Weather along the way and hazard reports from drivers on the road are free; road conditions (wet, ice, fog, wind) and popular times come with Apex.
  • No brand required. Carve is independent and made for any car — and riders too.

When ROADS by Porsche is the better pick

If you want a free app, like generating loops from a drive time and direction, need to export GPX files, or want to be part of Porsche's own community and events, ROADS is a good fit.

Questions

Is Carve better than ROADS by Porsche?

For group drives and roads with no signal, yes. Carve shows the whole group live on the map and car screen, never reroutes, guides you back after a wrong turn, and downloads routes for offline driving. Carve also imports GPX and plans on the web. ROADS is free and good for finding roads, with community routes and events.

Does Carve make you go back to a waypoint you skipped?

No. Carve follows you along the route: after a wrong turn or a skipped stop, it gives turn-by-turn directions forward to where you'll rejoin the route ahead, going the route's way. The stop stays on your list if you want to go back for it, and one tap clears it. A common complaint about ROADS by Porsche is that it keeps routing you back to a missed waypoint.

Do I need a Porsche to use either app?

No. ROADS by Porsche is open to anyone with an account, and Carve is independent and works with any car, or a motorcycle.

Which is better for a Porsche Club drive?

Carve: plan the route once, share one link, and every member drives the same route turn by turn, sees the group on the map with Group, and keeps going with no signal. Carve isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Porsche or the Porsche Club of America.

Does Carve work offline?

Yes, with Carve Apex: the map and every turn download with the route. ROADS by Porsche's listings don't mention offline navigation.

Can Carve find twisty roads near me?

Yes. Carve shows the best drives near you from its 1,100+ hand-picked roads, rated Leisurely, Sweeping, Twisty or Intense, and its planner can roll a scenic loop from where you are.

What does Carve cost?

Planning, sharing and driving with a signal are free. Carve Apex is $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month.

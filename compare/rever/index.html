Carve vs REVER

For drivers, riders, car clubs and group drives · Checked October 2026

Carve (carve.run) is a scenic-drive planner and turn-by-turn navigation app for cars and motorcycles, on iPhone with CarPlay and Android with Android Auto — the REVER alternative for drivers and riders who want free turn-by-turn and the whole group on one map.

The short answer. REVER is a well-loved motorcycle app with a big ride library and free ride tracking. Carve is the better pick when you're driving a car, leading a group, or want navigation that sticks to the road you chose: route planning on scenic roads, spoken turn-by-turn and wrong-turn guidance are free in Carve, while REVER puts turn-by-turn and twisty routing behind Pro. Carve's Group puts the whole group on the map and the car's screen, on CarPlay and Android Auto — REVER has no Android Auto.

Apex is Carve's paid plan: $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month. Everything without “Apex” is free. “Not listed” means REVER's own listings and help pages don't mention it.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • Made for cars as well as bikes. REVER's listing is written for motorcycle, off-road and snowmobile riders. Carve is built for car drivers, road-trippers and car clubs — and works just as well on a motorcycle with a helmet headset.
  • The essentials are free. Planning on scenic roads, spoken turn-by-turn and directions back after a wrong turn cost nothing in Carve. In REVER, turn-by-turn and twisty routing need Pro.
  • A real group drive. Carve's Group shows everyone on the route by name, with how far ahead or behind they are, on the phone and the car's screen. REVER's answer to group rides is a community post plus Pro location sharing.
  • On the car's screen, both kinds. Carve runs on CarPlay and Android Auto. REVER has no Android Auto, and says its CarPlay hasn't been optimized in a while.
  • Know before you go. Weather along the route is free in Carve, with road conditions, popular times and 3D previews in Apex.

When REVER is the better pick

If you ride a motorcycle off-road or adventure-style, live in the GPX world, or want free ride tracking and REVER's rider communities and challenges, REVER fits well.

Questions

Is Carve better than REVER for car drives?

Yes. REVER is built around motorcycle riding. Carve is built for driving a car on great roads: 1,100+ hand-picked drives, routes that never get swapped for a faster one, and free spoken turn-by-turn, with CarPlay and Android Auto in Apex.

Which is better for group drives and group rides?

Carve. Share one link and everyone drives the same route; with Group, everyone sees each other on the map and the car's screen, by name, with how far ahead or behind they are. REVER suggests a community post for group rides, and its live location sharing is a Pro feature.

Is turn-by-turn navigation free?

In Carve, yes: spoken turn-by-turn on your planned route, and directions back after a wrong turn, are free with a signal. In REVER, turn-by-turn directions are part of Pro ($39.99 a year).

Does Carve work with Android Auto?

Yes, with Carve Apex — and CarPlay on iPhone. REVER doesn't support Android Auto.

Does Carve work on a motorcycle?

Yes. Mount the phone and pair your helmet headset; the spoken directions come through it like any Bluetooth audio, and the twisty roads, offline maps and Group work the same on two wheels.

How do the prices compare?

Carve Apex is $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month. REVER Pro is $39.99 a year after a 14-day trial.

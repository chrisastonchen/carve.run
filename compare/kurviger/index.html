Carve vs Kurviger

For twisty roads, drivers, riders and group drives · Checked October 2026

Carve (carve.run) is a scenic-drive planner and turn-by-turn navigation app for cars and motorcycles, on iPhone with CarPlay and Android with Android Auto — the Kurviger alternative with free turn-by-turn, hand-picked driving roads and a live group map.

The short answer. Kurviger is a sharp curvy-route planner, made mostly for motorcycle riders in Europe. Carve is built for drivers and riders who want the great roads found for them and the whole group along: 1,100+ hand-picked drives, a planner that leans toward the good roads, free spoken turn-by-turn that never reroutes, and Group, which shows everyone on the route on the map and the car's screen. Kurviger's navigation needs its paid Tourer+ plan; in Carve it's free.

Apex is Carve's paid plan: $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month. Everything without “Apex” is free. “Not listed” means Kurviger's own listings and help pages don't mention it.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • Navigation is free. Spoken turn-by-turn on your planned route, and directions back after a wrong turn, cost nothing in Carve. Kurviger's navigation is on its paid Tourer+ plan.
  • Great roads, already found. Carve has 1,100+ hand-picked drives across the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the UAE, each rated for how twisty it is — open one and go.
  • Made for the group. Share one link and everyone drives the same route; Group shows everyone by name on the map and the car's screen. Kurviger's listings don't mention a group map.
  • Google Maps routes, directly. Paste a Google Maps link into Carve and the stops come with it. Kurviger's guide sends you through a third-party converter.
  • Know before you go. Weather along the way is free; road conditions, popular times and 3D previews come with Apex.

When Kurviger is the better pick

If you ride in Europe, want fine control over how curvy a route is, plan long round trips, or move routes between devices as GPX files, Kurviger is excellent at that.

Questions

Is Carve better than Kurviger?

For drivers and for groups, yes: Carve's turn-by-turn is free, its 1,100+ drives are hand-picked and rated for twistiness, and Group shows everyone on the route on the map. Kurviger is a strong curvy-route planner for riders, with navigation on its paid plan.

Is Kurviger for cars?

Kurviger is written mainly for motorcycle riders. Carve is made for cars and works just as well on a motorcycle.

Does Carve find curvy roads?

Yes. Carve's planner leans toward the good roads, marks great driving roads on the map, and its drives are rated Leisurely, Sweeping, Twisty or Intense.

Does Carve work offline and on CarPlay or Android Auto?

Yes, both with Carve Apex: the map and every turn download with the route, and the drive shows on CarPlay or Android Auto.

How do the prices compare?

Carve is free to plan, share and drive with a signal; Apex is $29.99 a year or $4.99 a month. Kurviger's Tourer+ is €29.99 a year, and Tourer €14.99.

Carve vs Roadtrippers

For scenic drives, road trips and group drives · Checked October 2026

Carve (carve.run) is a scenic-drive planner and turn-by-turn navigation app for cars and motorcycles, on iPhone with CarPlay and Android with Android Auto — the Roadtrippers alternative for the road between the stops: twisty, scenic routes driven turn by turn.

The short answer. Roadtrippers is a trip planner: it's great at finding places to stop, attractions and campgrounds across the US and Canada. Carve is built for the road between them — finding the twisty, scenic way, then driving it turn by turn without being rerouted onto the highway. Carve's free plan has no limit on saved routes, and includes spoken navigation; Apex, at $29.99 a year, adds offline turn-by-turn, CarPlay and Android Auto, and Group for driving together — less than any of Roadtrippers' paid plans.

Apex is Carve's paid plan: $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month. Everything without “Apex” is free. “Not listed” means Roadtrippers's own listings and help pages don't mention it.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • The road, not just the stops. Carve finds the twisty, scenic way between places, and has 1,100+ hand-picked driving roads rated for how twisty they are.
  • Your route, kept. Carve's own turn-by-turn drives exactly the route you planned and never swaps it for a faster one — no handing off to an app that might.
  • Directions with no signal. With Apex, Carve downloads the map and every turn. Roadtrippers' offline maps can be looked at, but don't give directions.
  • More for less. Carve's free plan has no limit on saved routes, up to 10 named stops each, and spoken navigation. Apex is $29.99 a year, less than Roadtrippers' cheapest paid plan, and includes CarPlay and Android Auto.
  • Bring the group. Share one link and everyone drives the same route; with Group, everyone sees each other on the map.

When Roadtrippers is the better pick

If you're planning a multi-day trip around attractions, campgrounds and RV-friendly routes, Roadtrippers' places database and RV routing are made for that. Many drivers plan the stops there and the driving roads in Carve.

Questions

Is Carve better than Roadtrippers for scenic drives?

Yes, for the driving. Roadtrippers finds places to stop; Carve finds the twisty, scenic roads between them and drives you along exactly that route, turn by turn, without rerouting onto the highway.

Does Carve navigate offline?

Yes, with Carve Apex: the map and every turn of your route are downloaded. Roadtrippers' offline maps can be viewed but don't give directions.

Does Carve work with Android Auto?

Yes, with Carve Apex, and with CarPlay on iPhone. Roadtrippers supports CarPlay on its Pro and Premium plans and doesn't support Android Auto.

Is Carve cheaper than Roadtrippers?

Carve's free plan has no limit on saved routes, up to 10 named stops each, and turn-by-turn with a signal. Carve Apex is $29.99 a year (or $4.99 a month); Roadtrippers' paid plans are $35.99, $49.99 and $59.99 a year.

Can I plan a road trip with friends?

Yes. Share the route's link and everyone drives the same route in Carve, on iPhone or Android; with Group, you see each other on the map along the way.

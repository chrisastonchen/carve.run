Carve vs Road & Rally

For car clubs, club tours and rallies · Checked November 2026

Carve (carve.run) is a scenic-drive planner and turn-by-turn navigation app for cars and motorcycles, on iPhone with CarPlay and Android with Android Auto — the Road & Rally alternative for club tours: plan the route or bring yours, share one link, and every driver follows it turn by turn.

The short answer. Road & Rally is a social app for car enthusiasts, strongest at events: finding twisty roads, driving them together in a live convoy, clubs and leaderboards, and hosting meets and multi-day rallies with RSVPs and paid registration. Carve is built for the planned route and the club tour: plan it stop by stop in the app or on a computer at carve.run/plan, or bring the one you have from a GPX file, Ride with GPS or Google Maps, share one link, and every driver gets spoken turn-by-turn on exactly that route, free. Carve has 1,200+ hand-picked drives, Group, CarPlay and Android Auto for free, and Carve Apex adds offline maps.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • Made for the planned route. Plan it stop by stop on Scenic, Balanced or Shortest roads, and Carve drives exactly that route; it never swaps it for a faster one. After a wrong turn, it leads you forward to where you rejoin.
  • Bring the routes you already have. Paste a Ride with GPS or Google Maps link, or drop in a GPX file, in the app or on a computer at carve.run/plan, free. Carve matches it to the same roads.
  • Plan on a computer, print the turns. Build the tour on a big screen, then send one link or QR code; it opens on iPhone, Android or in a browser, with printed turn-by-turn directions for anyone who wants paper.
  • CarPlay and Android Auto. Free with an account: your route, turns and stops are on the car's screen on iPhone and Android. Road & Rally lists CarPlay on iPhone.
  • 1,200+ hand-picked drives. Rated Leisurely to Intense across the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the UAE and Baja California, free to browse and drive.
  • Weather along the route. Forecasts for each part of the drive, for when you'll reach it, free; road conditions with Apex.

When Road & Rally is the better pick

If your club runs events, Road & Rally is built for it: RSVPs with a live headcount, multi-day rally schedules, and paid registration with tickets, add-ons and waivers, where it takes a flat fee per ticket and no percentage. Its live convoy map is free, and clubs and leaderboards keep driving stats over every run. Carve doesn't take sign-ups or keep driving stats; it's built for planning the route and leading the tour, so a club can host the event in Road & Rally and drive the route in Carve.

Questions

Is Carve a good Road & Rally alternative?

For planning and leading a club tour, yes: plan the route stop by stop or import it, share one link, and every driver follows exactly that route with free spoken turn-by-turn. For hosting events with RSVPs and paid registration, Road & Rally is built for that and Carve isn't.

Which is better for a car club tour?

Carve, if the route matters: plan it on a computer or in the app, or bring a GPX, Ride with GPS or Google Maps route, and share one link that opens on iPhone, Android or in a browser, with printed turns. Road & Rally is stronger for the event around it: RSVPs, multi-day schedules and tickets.

Does Road & Rally work with Android Auto?

Its site and store listings mention Apple CarPlay on iPhone, not Android Auto. Carve supports both CarPlay and Android Auto, free with an account.

Can I sell tickets for a rally in Carve?

No. Carve doesn't take registrations or payments; set up the event where your club already does, or in Road & Rally, and share the Carve route link for the drive.

Can I import routes into Carve?

Yes, free: a GPX file, or a Ride with GPS or Google Maps link, in the app or at carve.run/plan. Carve matches it to the same roads.

Is Carve free?

Planning, importing, sharing, driving with a signal, Group, CarPlay and Android Auto are free, and Individual, Apex for one driver at $49.99 a year with a 14-day free trial or $5.99 a month, adds offline maps, 3D previews and more.

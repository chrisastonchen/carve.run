Carve vs Ride with GPS

For car clubs, riders and group drives · Checked November 2026

Carve (carve.run) is a scenic-drive planner and turn-by-turn navigation app for cars and motorcycles, on iPhone with CarPlay and Android with Android Auto — the Ride with GPS alternative for car clubs and riders: bring the routes you already have and drive them turn by turn, with the whole group on one map.

The short answer. Ride with GPS is a superb cycling app, and plenty of car clubs plan their tours in it. Carve is built for driving those tours in a car or on a motorcycle. Bring the routes you already have: paste a Ride with GPS link or drop in the GPX file, in the app or on a computer at carve.run/plan, and Carve matches it to the same roads. Share one link, and on drive day every car gets free spoken turn-by-turn on exactly that route. Group puts everyone on the route on the map and the car's screen, on CarPlay and Android Auto, for free, and with Carve Apex every turn works with no signal.

Already have your club's routes in Ride with GPS?

Keep them. Paste a public or unlisted Ride with GPS route or ride link into Carve's Import — in the app, or on a computer at carve.run/plan — and Carve matches it to the same roads. For a private route, export its GPX file and drop that in instead. Then send the group one Carve link or QR code: it opens the same route on iPhone, Android or in a browser, with printed turns for anyone who wants paper.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • Bring the routes you already have. Paste a Ride with GPS route link, or drop in a GPX file, in the app or on a computer, and Carve matches it to the same roads. Your club's tours come with you; nothing to redraw.
  • Plan on a computer, drive it on the phone. Build or tidy the route on a big screen at carve.run/plan, free, then scan its QR code to drive it, send the link, or print the turns.
  • Made for cars and motorcycles. Carve routes on driving roads and leans toward the twisty ones, with 1,200+ hand-picked drives rated Leisurely to Intense. Ride with GPS is built for cycling, with routing that favors bike lanes and paths.
  • The whole group, live, on drive day. With Group, everyone on the route shows on the map and the car's screen, by name for everyone on your shared link or drive plan, with how far ahead or behind they are. Ride with GPS's Live Logging shares one rider's ride with friends and family.
  • On the car's screen. Carve runs on Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, with your route, turns and stops on the dash. Ride with GPS doesn't list either.
  • Turn-by-turn is free. Spoken turn-by-turn on your planned route, and directions forward after a wrong turn, cost nothing in Carve. In Ride with GPS, voice navigation is Premium.

When Ride with GPS is the better pick

For cycling, Ride with GPS is excellent: a huge library of cycling routes, sync with Garmin, Wahoo and other bike computers, ride stats, and club and event accounts with RSVPs and waivers for organizers. If your club rides bicycles, or you need event sign-ups, it's the stronger tool. Carve is built for cars and motorcycles; many clubs keep planning in Ride with GPS and bring the route into Carve to drive it.

Questions

Can I import Ride with GPS routes into Carve?

Yes, free. Paste a public or unlisted Ride with GPS route or ride link into Carve's Import, in the app or at carve.run/plan, and Carve matches it to the same roads. For a private route, export a GPX file from Ride with GPS and import that.

Is Ride with GPS good for car or motorcycle drives?

Ride with GPS is built and listed for cycling: its routing favors bike lanes, paths and quiet roads, and CarPlay and Android Auto aren't listed. Carve is made for cars and motorcycles, with routes that lean toward great driving roads, and CarPlay and Android Auto for free.

Which is better for a car club tour?

Plan the tour wherever you like, then drive it in Carve: share one link and every member gets the same route with spoken turn-by-turn, and with Group everyone shows on the map and the car's screen. Ride with GPS's club and event accounts handle RSVPs and waivers; Carve doesn't take sign-ups, so set the meet-up in your club's usual channel.

Can I plan a drive on a computer?

Yes, at carve.run/plan, free: import a GPX file or a Ride with GPS or Google Maps link, or build the route stop by stop on Scenic, Balanced or Shortest roads. Then save it, scan the QR code to drive it, send the link or print the turns.

Is turn-by-turn navigation free?

In Carve, yes: spoken turn-by-turn on your planned route, and directions back after a wrong turn, are free with a signal. In Ride with GPS, turn-by-turn voice navigation is part of Premium.

Is Carve free?

Planning, importing, sharing, driving with a signal, Group, CarPlay and Android Auto are free; Individual, Apex for one driver — $49.99 a year with a 14-day free trial, or $5.99 a month — adds offline maps, 3D previews, road conditions and more.

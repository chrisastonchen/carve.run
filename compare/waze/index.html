Carve vs Waze

For scenic drives, car clubs and group drives · Checked October 2026

The short answer. Waze is brilliant at one thing: beating traffic. It re-routes on traffic to save a minute, allows one stop per route, and needs a signal. On a scenic drive that's everything you don't want. Carve plans the whole drive on great roads, with up to 10 named stops, and drives it exactly as planned; your group gets the same route from one link and, with Group, everyone's on the same map — even where there's no signal.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • A whole drive, not one stop. Plan the coast road, the canyon and the lunch stop in one route. Waze allows a single stop.
  • No rerouting, ever. Carve drives exactly the route you planned. Waze is built to swap roads to save time.
  • The good roads, found. 1,100+ hand-picked drives rated Leisurely to Intense, and a planner that leans toward scenic roads.
  • The group on one map. One link gives everyone the same route; with Group, every driver shows on the map and the car's screen by name. Waze's Share drive only lets people follow you.
  • Works with no signal. With Apex, Carve downloads the map and every turn of the route. Waze needs a connection.

When Waze is the better pick

For the daily commute and beating traffic, with crowd-sourced alerts for police, hazards and speed cameras, Waze is great and free. Carve is for the drives where you came for the road, not to save five minutes.

Questions

Is Carve better than Waze for scenic drives?

Yes. Waze is built to beat traffic and re-routes to save time. Carve finds great driving roads and keeps you on exactly the route you planned.

Can Waze plan a route with several stops?

Waze allows one stop per route. A Carve route can have up to 10 named stops and 30 points in all, and you can import a Google Maps route with its stops.

Which is better for driving with friends?

Carve. Share one link and everyone drives the same route; with Group, everyone on it shows on the map and the car's screen by name. Waze's Share drive lets people follow one driver.

Does Carve work without signal?

Yes, with Carve Apex: the map and every turn of the route download, so the drive works the whole way. Waze needs a connection.

Does Carve work with CarPlay and Android Auto?

Yes, both, with Carve Apex.

Is Carve free?

Planning, sharing and driving with a signal are free. Carve Apex is $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month.

Carve vs Joinr

For car clubs, rallies and group drives · Checked October 2026

The short answer. Joinr is a free organizer for car events and rally trips: you host an event, people join, and everyone sees where the others are. Carve does the part Joinr doesn't advertise — the roads. Plan the drive on hand-picked twisty roads, share one link, and every car gets the same route with spoken turn-by-turn that never reroutes, offline maps for the stretches with no signal, CarPlay and Android Auto, and Group so everyone sees each other on the map. For a club run or a rally where the route matters, Carve is the stronger choice.

Where Carve pulls ahead

  • The route is the point. A club run lives or dies on the roads. Carve finds them — 1,100+ hand-picked drives rated for how twisty they are, plus a planner that leans toward the good roads — and keeps every car on exactly that route.
  • Navigation that doesn't break the convoy. Carve's spoken turn-by-turn never swaps the route for a faster one, and after a wrong turn it leads the driver back to the route instead of sending them a different way.
  • Works where the good roads are. Canyons and mountain passes lose signal. With Apex, Carve downloads the map and every turn, so the drive keeps going.
  • On the car's screen. Carve's route, turns, stops and the group's cars show on CarPlay and Android Auto. Joinr's listings don't mention either.
  • One link for everyone. Plan once and share a link: it opens in Carve on iPhone or Android, or in any browser, and your later edits reach every driver.

When Joinr is the better pick

If what your group needs most is event logistics — a schedule, stops, voice messages, alerts and a shared photo gallery — Joinr does that for free, and it also covers bikes and trips on foot. Carve doesn't do sign-ups or schedules; many clubs use both, Joinr or their usual chat for the meet-up and Carve for the drive.

Questions

Is Carve or Joinr better for a car club drive?

For the drive itself, Carve: plan the route on great roads, share one link, and everyone gets the same turn-by-turn route with Group to see each other on the map, offline maps and CarPlay or Android Auto. Joinr is built around organizing the event — schedules, stops, alerts and photos.

Can I see everyone in the group on the map?

Yes, in both. In Carve, Group shows everyone driving the route by name, with how far ahead or behind they are, on the phone and the car's screen (Carve Apex). Joinr shows members' locations during an event.

Which is better for a rally?

For a road rally or tour where the route matters, Carve: it plans and holds the exact route, guides each car turn by turn, brings drivers back after a wrong turn, and keeps working with no signal. Neither app is a timing or scoring tool for competitive rallies.

Does Carve keep working without cell signal?

Yes, with Carve Apex: the map and every turn of the route are downloaded, so the drive works the whole way. Joinr's listings don't mention offline maps.

Does Carve schedule events or take sign-ups?

No. Carve plans and leads the drive; set the time and meeting point in your club's usual channel, then share the Carve link.

Is Carve free?

Planning, sharing and driving with a signal are free. Carve Apex — $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $4.99 a month — adds Group, offline maps, CarPlay and Android Auto, 3D previews and more.

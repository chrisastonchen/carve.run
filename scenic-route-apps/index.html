The best scenic route apps for 2026

By Chris Aston Chen · Updated November 2026

Finding a great road is half the job; the other half is driving it without your map app dragging you back to the highway. These apps find scenic, twisty roads, and here's which of them will also get you down them, turn by turn, and what that costs.

The short answer. Carve finds the roads (1,200+ hand-picked drives and a Scenic route option) and drives them turn by turn without rerouting, free, with your group on one map. calimoto and Kurviger have excellent curvy routing for motorcycles but charge for navigation; Road & Rally grades roads with Road Finder; Roadtrippers is for the stops, not the road.

1. Carve Our app

Best for finding great roads and driving them

Plan a drive on great roads, from 1,200+ hand-picked drives or stop by stop in the app or on a computer at carve.run/plan, or import a GPX file, a Ride with GPS link or a Google Maps link. Share one link and every driver gets spoken turn-by-turn on exactly that route; Carve never reroutes you onto a faster road, and after a wrong turn it leads you forward to where you rejoin it. With Group, everyone on the route is on one map, and on CarPlay or Android Auto.

Price: Free. Individual (Apex for one driver), $49.99 a year with a 14-day free trial, adds offline maps, 3D previews and road conditions. Platforms: iPhone with CarPlay, Android with Android Auto, web planner. See Carve

2. Road & Rally

Best for hosting meets and rallies

Road Finder grades roads on curvature and flow, Convoy puts the group on a shared live map, and organizers can host meets and multi-day rallies with RSVPs and paid registration. Turn-by-turn and Convoy are free; VIP adds offline maps and full curated guides. It lists CarPlay on iPhone, not Android Auto.

Price: Free; VIP is an in-app purchase. Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Android. Carve vs Road & Rally

3. calimoto

Best for motorcycle riders

A motorcycle app with a free twisty-roads planner and a Calimeter that rates how curvy a ride is. Turn-by-turn, offline maps, group rides, CarPlay and Android Auto come with Premium.

Price: Free planning; Premium $79.99 a year after a 14-day trial, or $12.99 a week. Platforms: iPhone, Android, web planner. Carve vs calimoto

4. Kurviger

Best curvy-route planner for riders

Curvy and extra-curvy route profiles and round trips, free to plan. Navigation, offline maps and recording are on its paid Tourer+ plan; CarPlay and Android Auto need a subscription.

Price: Free planning; Tourer €14.99 and Tourer+ €29.99 a year. Platforms: iPhone, Android, web. Carve vs Kurviger

5. ROADS by Porsche

Best for a free scenic route generator

A scenic route generator for loops and A-to-B routes, routes shared by its community, and turn-by-turn on CarPlay and Android Auto. Free, with an account; its listings don't mention offline navigation or a live group map.

Price: Free; account required. Platforms: iPhone, Android, web. Carve vs ROADS by Porsche

6. REVER

Best for a riders' community

A motorcycle-first app with thousands of routes, mostly from riders. Turn-by-turn, twisty routing, offline maps and live location sharing are Pro, and there's no Android Auto.

Price: Free; Pro $39.99 a year after a 14-day trial. Platforms: iPhone, Android, web. Carve vs REVER

7. RevRoutes

Best for curated drives on iPhone

Curated scenic drives by region and road type, rated for turns, scenery, sound and traffic, with voice turn-by-turn and car meets. Its free tier saves 3 drives a month, and its listing doesn't mention offline maps, CarPlay, Android Auto or an Android app.

Price: Free; Pro $39.99 a year or $4.99 a month. Platforms: iPhone. Carve vs RevRoutes

8. Roadtrippers

Best for road-trip stops

A road-trip planner for places, attractions and campgrounds along the way. Turn-by-turn and CarPlay come with paid plans, offline maps don't give directions, and it doesn't support Android Auto.

Price: Free for 1 trip with 3 stops; paid plans from $35.99 a year. Platforms: iPhone, Android, web. Carve vs Roadtrippers

9. Google Maps

Best for everyday navigation

Live traffic, up to 9 stops, offline directions inside downloaded areas, CarPlay and Android Auto. It's built to get you there fastest and re-routes on traffic, so on a road you picked for the corners it keeps pulling you toward the highway.

Price: Free. Platforms: iPhone, Android, web. Carve vs Google Maps

How we picked

  • Does it find the good roads? A library of known driving roads, a twisty or scenic routing option, or both.
  • Can you drive what it finds? Spoken turn-by-turn on the scenic route, without being rerouted onto a faster one.
  • What's free? Several of these are free to plan and charge for navigation.

Carve is our app; we've listed it first because it's built for this, and said what each of the others is best for. What this page says about other apps comes from their own store listings and pages, checked in November 2026; each comparison page lists its sources. Apps change; if something here is out of date, write to support@carve.run and it'll be fixed.

Questions

What's the best app for finding scenic drives?

Carve: 1,200+ hand-picked drives rated Leisurely to Intense, a Scenic route option that leans toward the good roads, and free turn-by-turn on exactly that route.

How do I find twisty roads near me?

Open Carve and look at the drives near you, each rated Leisurely, Sweeping, Twisty or Intense, or tap Surprise me for a scenic loop from where you are. You can also browse every drive by state at carve.run/drives.

Can Google Maps find scenic routes?

Not really: it can avoid highways and tolls, but it has no scenic or winding-road option, and it re-routes on traffic. You can paste a Google Maps route link into Carve and drive it there.

What's the best scenic route app for motorcycles?

calimoto and Kurviger both have strong curvy routing for riders, with navigation on their paid plans. Carve works on a motorcycle too, with free spoken turn-by-turn through your helmet headset.
